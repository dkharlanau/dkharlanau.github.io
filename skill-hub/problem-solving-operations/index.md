<section class="section atlas-hero">
  <p class="eyebrow">Skill Hub / Problem Solving &amp; Operations</p>
  <h1>Diagnose the system. Then decide how it should evolve.</h1>
  <p class="lead">The same reasoning can be used across SAP, SaaS, APIs, files, cloud services, custom applications, and AI workflows. The lower layer helps find and control problems. The Lead layer turns evidence into architecture, ownership, controls, and operating decisions.</p>
</section>

<section class="section">
  <header class="section-heading"><h2>The operating model</h2></header>
  <p><strong>Situation → Map → Trace → Diagnose → Decide → Design → Change → Validate → Operate → Reuse</strong></p>
  <pre><code>Business outcome
├─ Service Dependency Mapping
├─ Business Rule Ownership Analysis
└─ Cross-System Ownership
       ↓
End-to-End Flow Trace
       ↓
Evidence-Driven Troubleshooting
       ↓
Root Cause / Decision / Change
       ↓
Lead architecture layer
├─ Non-Functional Requirements
├─ Solution Option Evaluation
├─ Exception Strategy
├─ Control Design
├─ Failure Mode / Resilience Review
├─ Technical Debt Decision
├─ Architecture Fitness Review
└─ Operating Model Design
       ↓
Release → Cutover → Monitoring → Improvement
</code></pre>
</section>

<section class="section">
  <header class="section-heading"><h2>Lead / Architect layer</h2></header>
  <p>This layer is about choices and accountability. It connects architecture to business outcomes, quality attributes, controls, ownership, and the real cost of operating the solution.</p>
  <div class="topic-grid">
    <div class="topic-card"><h3><a href="/skill-hub/problem-solving-operations/solution-option-evaluation-working-skill/">Solution Option Evaluation</a></h3><p>Compare materially different system shapes against outcomes, constraints, quality attributes, risk, cost, and reversibility.</p></div>
    <div class="topic-card"><h3><a href="/skill-hub/problem-solving-operations/non-functional-requirements-working-skill/">Non-Functional Requirements</a></h3><p>Turn words like fast, resilient, secure, and scalable into measurable architecture and operational conditions.</p></div>
    <div class="topic-card"><h3><a href="/skill-hub/problem-solving-operations/cross-system-ownership-working-skill/">Cross-System Ownership</a></h3><p>Make accountability explicit across business outcomes, data, interfaces, incidents, changes, and end-to-end validation.</p></div>
    <div class="topic-card"><h3><a href="/skill-hub/problem-solving-operations/exception-strategy-working-skill/">Exception Strategy</a></h3><p>Design detection, retries, holds, compensation, reconciliation, escalation, and closure instead of leaving the unhappy path to support.</p></div>
    <div class="topic-card"><h3><a href="/skill-hub/problem-solving-operations/control-design-working-skill/">Control Design</a></h3><p>Choose preventive, detective, corrective, or compensating controls and make evidence, ownership, bypass, and effectiveness explicit.</p></div>
    <div class="topic-card"><h3><a href="/skill-hub/problem-solving-operations/technical-debt-decision-working-skill/">Technical Debt Decision</a></h3><p>Decide whether debt should be accepted, contained, refactored, replaced, or retired using cost of carry and change frequency.</p></div>
    <div class="topic-card"><h3><a href="/skill-hub/problem-solving-operations/architecture-fitness-review-working-skill/">Architecture Fitness Review</a></h3><p>Check whether the living system still satisfies intended outcomes, NFRs, boundaries, controls, and operating assumptions.</p></div>
    <div class="topic-card"><h3><a href="/skill-hub/problem-solving-operations/operating-model-design-working-skill/">Operating Model Design</a></h3><p>Design ownership, support, monitoring, incidents, changes, data responsibilities, suppliers, governance, and improvement after go-live.</p></div>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="section">
  <header class="section-heading"><h2>Core diagnostic and delivery skills</h2></header>
  <div class="topic-grid">
    <div class="topic-card"><h3><a href="/skill-hub/problem-solving-operations/service-dependency-mapping-working-skill/">Service Dependency Mapping</a></h3><p>Map runtime dependencies, owners, failure impact, and health evidence behind one business outcome.</p></div>
    <div class="topic-card"><h3><a href="/skill-hub/problem-solving-operations/business-rule-ownership-analysis-working-skill/">Business Rule Ownership Analysis</a></h3><p>Trace rules across source, owner, data, enforcement points, exceptions, and change paths.</p></div>
    <div class="topic-card"><h3><a href="/skill-hub/problem-solving-operations/end-to-end-flow-trace-working-skill/">End-to-End Flow Trace</a></h3><p>Follow one business object across systems and find the first boundary where evidence breaks.</p></div>
    <div class="topic-card"><h3><a href="/skill-hub/problem-solving-operations/evidence-driven-troubleshooting-working-skill/">Evidence-Driven Troubleshooting</a></h3><p>Reproduce a problem, isolate the first failing layer, test hypotheses, and avoid random changes.</p></div>
    <div class="topic-card"><h3><a href="/skill-hub/problem-solving-operations/api-contract-troubleshooting-working-skill/">API Contract Troubleshooting</a></h3><p>Trace identity, route, schema, provider logic, dependencies, and consumer handling.</p></div>
    <div class="topic-card"><h3><a href="/skill-hub/problem-solving-operations/authorization-identity-diagnosis-working-skill/">Authorization &amp; Identity Diagnosis</a></h3><p>Separate authentication, effective identity, propagation, resource scope, and authorization.</p></div>
    <div class="topic-card"><h3><a href="/skill-hub/problem-solving-operations/batch-queue-troubleshooting-working-skill/">Batch &amp; Queue Troubleshooting</a></h3><p>Trace asynchronous work through scheduling, states, retries, locks, acknowledgement, backlog, and throughput.</p></div>
    <div class="topic-card"><h3><a href="/skill-hub/problem-solving-operations/data-discovery-mapping-working-skill/">Data Discovery &amp; Mapping</a></h3><p>Profile data, find keys and relationships, propose mappings, and validate them on real rows.</p></div>
    <div class="topic-card"><h3><a href="/skill-hub/problem-solving-operations/data-reconciliation-working-skill/">Data Reconciliation</a></h3><p>Compare populations and values, classify exceptions, and explain differences.</p></div>
    <div class="topic-card"><h3><a href="/skill-hub/problem-solving-operations/data-migration-validation-working-skill/">Data Migration Validation</a></h3><p>Prove migrated population, identity, relationships, transformations, values, and usability.</p></div>
    <div class="topic-card"><h3><a href="/skill-hub/problem-solving-operations/delta-cutoff-control-working-skill/">Delta / Cutoff Control</a></h3><p>Control watermarks, late arrivals, duplicates, replay, and business cutoffs.</p></div>
    <div class="topic-card"><h3><a href="/skill-hub/problem-solving-operations/configuration-drift-analysis-working-skill/">Configuration Drift Analysis</a></h3><p>Compare effective environment state and prove which difference explains changed behavior.</p></div>
    <div class="topic-card"><h3><a href="/skill-hub/problem-solving-operations/process-deviation-analysis-working-skill/">Process Deviation Analysis</a></h3><p>Find the first point where the actual process diverged from expected behavior.</p></div>
    <div class="topic-card"><h3><a href="/skill-hub/problem-solving-operations/procedure-design-working-skill/">Procedure / Runbook Design</a></h3><p>Turn repeated work into an executable procedure with evidence, branches, stop conditions, rollback, and ownership.</p></div>
    <div class="topic-card"><h3><a href="/skill-hub/problem-solving-operations/decision-facilitation-working-skill/">Decision Facilitation</a></h3><p>Turn discussion into a decision or explicit deferment with trade-offs, owner, and next action.</p></div>
    <div class="topic-card"><h3><a href="/skill-hub/problem-solving-operations/integration-contract-change-review-working-skill/">Integration Contract Change Review</a></h3><p>Review schema, semantics, compatibility, producer/consumer impact, rollout, and retirement.</p></div>
    <div class="topic-card"><h3><a href="/skill-hub/problem-solving-operations/failure-mode-resilience-review-working-skill/">Failure Mode / Resilience Review</a></h3><p>Identify realistic failures, detection gaps, containment, recovery, and resilience tests.</p></div>
    <div class="topic-card"><h3><a href="/skill-hub/problem-solving-operations/ai-evaluation-guardrail-testing-working-skill/">AI Evaluation &amp; Guardrail Testing</a></h3><p>Test task quality, authority boundaries, tool use, failure handling, and regression before release.</p></div>
    <div class="topic-card"><h3><a href="/skill-hub/problem-solving-operations/release-readiness-working-skill/">Release Readiness</a></h3><p>Make a go/no-go decision from risk, evidence, dependencies, recovery, monitoring, and ownership.</p></div>
    <div class="topic-card"><h3><a href="/skill-hub/problem-solving-operations/cutover-hypercare-control-working-skill/">Cutover &amp; Hypercare Control</a></h3><p>Control production transition, checkpoints, stop decisions, validation, stabilization, and handover.</p></div>
    <div class="topic-card"><h3><a href="/skill-hub/problem-solving-operations/operational-metrics-alert-triage-working-skill/">Operational Metrics &amp; Alert Triage</a></h3><p>Translate alerts into business impact, correlate evidence, route ownership, recover safely, and tune monitoring.</p></div>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="section">
  <header class="section-heading"><h2>How the Lead layer composes</h2></header>
  <pre><code>Business need / architecture pressure
  ↓
Non-Functional Requirements
  ↓
Solution Option Evaluation
  ↓
Decision Facilitation / ADR
  ↓
Cross-System Ownership
  ├─ Business Rule Ownership Analysis
  ├─ Exception Strategy
  └─ Control Design
  ↓
Failure Mode / Resilience Review
  ↓
Release Readiness → Cutover & Hypercare
  ↓
Operating Model Design
  ↓
Operational Metrics & Alert Triage
  ↓
Architecture Fitness Review
  ├─ keep
  ├─ correct drift
  └─ Technical Debt Decision
</code></pre>
</section>
