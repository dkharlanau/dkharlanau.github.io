      <p class="eyebrow">What this course is</p>
      <h2>A field guide, not a certification crib sheet.</h2>
      <p>The SAP Architecture Center covers broad technology domains: AI and machine learning, application development and automation, data and analytics, integration, and operations and security. This course takes those domains seriously, but brings them back to the moment an architect actually has to make a call: a process is blocked, an extension is proposed, data does not agree, or a new interface will become somebody’s 3 a.m. problem.</p>
      <p>Each module gives you a way to think, a small artifact to make, and a few rules for avoiding the predictable bad decisions. Read them in order for a complete path, or use one when your current project needs it.</p>

<section class="section">
  <header class="section-heading"><p class="eyebrow">A useful boundary</p><h2>This course will not teach product buttons.</h2></header>
  <p>It will not turn you into an SAP product specialist overnight, and it will not pretend every landscape should use the newest service. Product documentation tells you what a platform can do. Architecture work asks whether that capability belongs here, who will own it, how it fails, what it costs to reverse, and how it changes the business process.</p>
  <p class="course-source">Course framing informed by the official <a href="https://architecture.learning.sap.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">SAP Architecture Center</a> and its public <a href="https://github.com/SAP/architecture-center" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">reference-architecture repository</a>. This is an independent practical interpretation, not SAP training or official SAP guidance.</p>
