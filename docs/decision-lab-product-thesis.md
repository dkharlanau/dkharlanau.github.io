# SAP Enterprise & Business AI Decision Lab

Status: canonical product thesis  
Roadmap: #314  
Updated: 2026-08-18

## Product thesis

DKHARLANAU.github.io is an evidence-backed SAP Enterprise and Business AI Decision Lab. It connects enterprise processes, public evidence, architecture controls, and reusable decision methods so a Lead can explain what should be done, why it is safe, and how success should be measured.

The site is not a second SAP documentation portal and not a collection of AI demos. Its job is to turn public knowledge into defensible enterprise decisions.

## Primary audiences

- **SAP and ERP Leads** who need end-to-end process, integration, data, control, and delivery reasoning.
- **Enterprise AI, data, and integration practitioners** who need to connect AI patterns to real process boundaries and systems of record.
- **Assessment reviewers, recruiters, and buyers** who need public proof of structured Lead-level thinking instead of unsupported seniority claims.

## Evidence chain

Every important Decision Lab output should be traceable through this chain:

`business problem -> enterprise process -> decision point -> evidence -> architecture/control -> outcome -> reusable lesson`

For Business AI work, the chain can add AI-specific detail without changing the logic:

`business problem -> enterprise process -> decision point -> AI/automation pattern -> technology -> authority/control -> public evidence -> outcome/limitation -> Lead recommendation`

A page, case, graph view, agent output, or service claim does not need to show every step visually. The underlying data and links should make the chain recoverable.

## Role of each product area

| Product area | Role in the Decision Lab | Evidence contribution |
|---|---|---|
| Profile | Identifies the accountable practitioner and public professional context. | Experience, certifications, publications, and clearly bounded public claims. |
| Knowledge | Keeps durable explanations, scenarios, research, and reviewed concepts. | Sources, domain explanations, diagnostic reasoning, and changing research evidence. |
| Labs | Tests active ideas, cases, practice, and cross-domain decisions. | SAP Enterprise maps, Business AI cases, assessment exercises, experiments, and proof gaps. |
| Frameworks | Turns repeated reasoning into reusable methods. | Decision methods, diagnostic protocols, controls, and repeatable operating patterns. |
| Machine layer | Makes the same knowledge usable by tools and agents. | Stable IDs, structured datasets, graph artifacts, context packs, skills, and manifests. |
| Services | Applies the evidence and methods to buyer problems. | A clear link from a service problem to method, public proof, control boundary, and expected output. |

These areas are different views of one knowledge system. They should link to shared sources rather than copy the same explanation into several directories.

## Authority themes

The project should build public authority around five connected themes:

1. **SAP logistics and end-to-end process decisions.** Sales, procurement, inventory, fulfilment, master data, and cross-functional effects are explained as one enterprise flow rather than isolated transactions.
2. **ERP integration and data diagnosis.** Interfaces, ownership, master data, reconciliation, failure recovery, and system boundaries are treated as Lead decisions, not only technical symptoms.
3. **Business AI with evidence and control.** AI use cases are connected to business process, system authority, evidence quality, autonomy, human review, measurable outcomes, and limitations.
4. **Lead decision quality.** Recommendations make assumptions, trade-offs, controls, proof gaps, metrics, and exit criteria visible.
5. **Reusable agent and operating methods.** Agents use the same evidence model, graph, and skills as the site. They may prepare and challenge work, but they do not manufacture proof or silently approve their own evidence.

## Non-goals

- Do not create a seventh top-level product called Decision Lab. The thesis connects the six existing product areas.
- Do not redesign the homepage only to announce the thesis.
- Do not duplicate SAP or Business AI source material to make a new view look complete.
- Do not require a hosted graph database, runtime agent platform, authentication service, or other server-side system for the static site.
- Do not publish private client information or convert private project memory into public evidence.
- Do not promote provider claims, supported inferences, or untested assumptions to runtime proof.
- Do not measure progress by page count, case count, or graph density alone.

## Roadmap decision rule

Future work should strengthen at least one part of the evidence chain and should reuse the canonical architecture and data contract. A new view is useful when it improves a real decision, exposes a proof gap, strengthens evidence, or makes existing knowledge easier to reuse.

The north-star question is simple: **Can a reviewer move from a business problem to a defensible Lead recommendation and inspect the evidence and control logic behind it?**
