SAP IDOC EVIDENCE CHECKLIST

Use this worksheet to prepare an IDoc investigation or handover. Adapt the
checks to the actual systems, authorizations, interfaces and processing path.
Do not put customer data in a public copy. This is a diagnostic aid, not an
authorization to reprocess or resend messages.

1. Business symptom and scope
[ ] Expected business result and affected process: __________________________
[ ] Observed result, first occurrence and business impact: ___________________
[ ] Source and receiver systems; isolated issue or backlog: _________________

2. Identify each message separately
[ ] IDoc number and direction in each system: ________________________________
[ ] Message type, sender, receiver and timestamp: ___________________________
[ ] Current status, complete status history and exact message text: __________
[ ] Business reference and other landscape-specific correlation evidence: _____
[ ] If applicable, transaction ID and related queue or job: __________________

3. Follow the evidence
[ ] Confirm that source and receiver records refer to the same business event.
[ ] Identify the last confirmed handoff and the first step not yet proven.
[ ] Check the relevant application object, log, transport, queue or background
    job according to the actual processing path.
[ ] Record observed facts separately from hypotheses and missing evidence.

Status boundary: outbound 03 indicates handoff to the port, not proof of
receiver posting. Inbound 53 indicates an application document was posted;
inspect the referenced object to determine whether it is the expected result.
Neither status establishes that two records are the same business message.

4. Before any recovery action
[ ] Establish whether the original message already produced a business object.
[ ] Confirm sequencing, duplicate handling, ownership and recovery authority.
[ ] Agree the correction and validation with the appropriate owners.
[ ] Record how the business result and any downstream effects will be checked.

SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE — TRAINING ONLY
A source team reports outbound IDoc 800001 with status 03. A receiver team
reports inbound IDoc 900004 with status 53. A user cannot find an order. The
records are not yet correlated; the numbers and situation are fictional.

Known: each team reports one status. Unknown: whether both messages concern
the same business event, and which application object was posted.
Next: compare sender, receiver, message type, business reference and timing;
use the system-specific correlation evidence. If they match, inspect the
referenced application object and its business state. Do not resend while the
original outcome and duplicate risk remain unconfirmed.
