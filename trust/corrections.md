# Corrections policy and ledger

The site should not silently rewrite material published factual claims when the correction matters to a reader, search system, or AI agent evaluating the evidence.

## What belongs in the ledger

Record a correction when it materially changes the meaning of a published professional, technical, research, benchmark, dataset, identity, status, or evidence claim.

A ledger entry should identify the affected canonical URL, the previous claim or state, the corrected claim or state, the date, the reason, and supporting evidence where available.

## What normally does not belong in the ledger

Spelling fixes, layout changes, broken links, accessibility improvements, metadata normalization, generated inventory refreshes, and equivalent editorial changes do not need a material correction entry unless they alter the meaning of an important claim.

## Current state

No material corrections are recorded in the ledger as of 2026-09-05.
