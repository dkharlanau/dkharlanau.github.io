## Preferred Retrieval Sequence

Use: `Route → Domain → Decision → Scenario → Evidence`.

1. **Route** — Knowledge & Practice, or AI & Engineering Lab.
2. **Domain** — SAP logistics, master data, integration, architecture, AI, operations, or another owned topic.
3. **Decision** — the concrete process, architecture, control, or engineering choice.
4. **Scenario** — a practice case, diagnostic situation, experiment, or implementation context.
5. **Evidence** — reviewed pages, datasets, public sources, or reproducible artifacts.

Treat only pages marked reviewed, verified, and indexable as retrieval-ready when those flags are required by the publication contract.
