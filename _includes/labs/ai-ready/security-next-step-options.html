The four next-step options

Use these four actions when deciding what should happen next. The choice should follow the evaluation charter, operating-model readiness, evidence quality, and customer-owned review.

Next step Use when What it means
Proceed The workflow is authorized, bounded, reviewed, evidenced, and customer-owned. Move to a clear bounded next action inside the approved scope.
Pause A required operating-model condition is unclear. Identify the missing condition, evidence, approval, owner, or boundary and name who can resolve it.
Escalate The request is sensitive, advanced, outside the current team’s authority, or requires OpenAI or partner subject-matter confirmation. Bring in the required subject-matter expertise before making stronger claims, changing permissions, or recommending a higher-risk action.
Expand The bounded workflow has demonstrated useful evidence quality, reviewability, repeatability, and governance control. Broaden the workflow without removing review gates, customer-owned decisions, or evidence limits.

Pause is a valid outcome. A missing approval, unclear owner, weak evidence path, or uncertain environment should be resolved before the workflow continues.

Expand is a new governance decision, not automatic scale-up. Reconfirm the evaluation charter, people, systems, actions, evidence ceiling, metrics, retention, monitoring, and system of record before crossing a new boundary.
