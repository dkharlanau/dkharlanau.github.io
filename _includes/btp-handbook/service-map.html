11 / Service map

A broad map, not a shopping list.

Start with the responsibility you need, then choose a service. The map separates services, runtimes, product capabilities, development models, components and neighbouring products. A capability nested inside a product is not automatically another subscription. Lifecycle entries are deliberately separated from normal new-project candidates.

Actual availability has four gates: product lifecycle → region/hyperscaler → commercial/service plan → your account's entitlement and configuration. Use the live SAP Discovery Center service catalogue for the full, changing inventory. No static list can mean “all of these are orderable in every account”.
