14 / Draw what you mean

Six views of the same architecture questions

A diagram is useful when you know what its boxes and arrows mean. The examples below are original, monochrome and deliberately small. They separate administrative hierarchy, software structure, business process, runtime interaction, business relationships and method phases.

Read the caption before interpreting a line. On a narrow screen, scroll the figure horizontally instead of shrinking the labels. Every view has a text explanation; no meaning depends on color.
