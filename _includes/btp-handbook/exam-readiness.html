What you must be able to explain, choose and verify

Certification check — 9 October 2026: the official page for SAP Certified – Solution Architect – SAP Business AI Platform identifies C_BAIPA, one scenario-based activity, two hours and a 60% passing score. The previous handbook referenced P_BTPA. Use C_BAIPA for this Business AI Platform preparation; your actual enrollment page remains the authority for the attempt, language and instructions. The code of a course is not an exam code.

SAP's public description emphasizes translating business requirements into end-to-end solutions, integration, technical architecture, application design and governance. It does not provide an official question-by-question weighting here. The priorities and cases below are our preparation framework, not a disclosed exam blueprint or a prediction of the questions.

A reading route for today

Start with a diagnosis, not a complete reread. Explain the supplier-review case aloud: business goal, ERP owner, selected services, access, failure handling and cost. Mark the point where your explanation becomes vague. Follow the links in the skills table to repair that gap. Then repeat the explanation without the page.

For a first full read, use the platform map and accounts; then commercial checks, extension choice, integration paths and security. Finish with AI, governance, methodology phases and the two oral rehearsals. Use the glossary to resolve a term immediately, not as a separate memorization marathon.

Core skill versus specialist depth

“Specialist depth” means implementation detail to look up or delegate after the design is clear. It does not mean that a topic cannot appear in an assessment.

Business and architectureName the outcome, system of record, constraints and two plausible alternatives. Explain why one was rejected. Practice the decision gates.Complete enterprise repository administration or every reference-model element.
Platform mechanicsSeparate global account, subaccount, runtime, plan, entitlement, subscription, instance, binding and user authorization. Trace provisioning.Every cockpit screen, CLI flag or Kubernetes resource.

Required controls are not a compulsory shopping list

For the supplier-review case, the responsibilities in the middle column must be addressed. The products in the last column are conditional ways to implement them. A technology can become mandatory under a specific company standard, contract or selected pattern; that is a documented constraint, not a universal SAP rule.

NeedWhat cannot be omittedWhat is not automatically required
User and agent accessTrusted identity, least privilege, case/tenant authorization and accountability.Every identity service, an API gateway for every internal call, or an AI agent.
Supplier decisionAn accountable approval process and an authoritative ERP result.A new Build Process Automation workflow when standard approval already meets the need.
Application behaviorA supported implementation and runtime when custom behavior is required.Both CAP and RAP, both Cloud Foundry and Kyma, or a separate microservice per step.
Remote business operationA supported interface contract, permitted caller, error handling and reconciliation.Cloud Integration for a simple supported direct call; Cloud Connector for a public cloud API.
State and evidenceDurable state and audit/retention appropriate to the risk.A separate HANA database for every app or a copy of the complete supplier master.
Decision supportAgreed measures, sources, data quality, access and freshness.BDC, Datasphere and SAC together for a small operational list.
AI interpretationA suitable model, authorized evidence, evaluation and safe failure behavior, if AI is justified.Generative AI for a deterministic approval rule; a graph or multi-agent design without a need.
OperationsMonitoring, owners, tests, recovery, change control and consumption visibility.Cloud ALM, Focused Run and Solution Manager simultaneously, or every premium monitoring feature.

Before opening the real assessment

Check the exact enrollment, remaining attempt, SAP Universal ID, the technical readiness instructions and the available practice tool. Test the supported browser, audio/video requirements and network for your selected format. Follow that attempt's resource-use rules rather than assuming another certification's rules apply. This handbook cannot inspect or confirm your booking.

A useful final rehearsal: answer “why this option?”, “what changes when ERP is unavailable?”, “who pays for it?” and “what proves it worked?” for the same design. State an unknown precisely and name the document or test that would resolve it. Do not invent a service feature, price or hands-on experience to make an answer sound complete.
