Review method

Read for failure, not for prose polish.

The review is complete only when the page can survive a Lead-level follow-up: what owns the decision, what evidence proves the cause, what changes across releases, and what is still consultant judgement.

Scope

  • Does the page claim only what its evidence supports?
  • Are product and release boundaries visible?
  • Are synthetic examples clearly separated?

Reasoning

  • Is the first wrong decision explicit?
  • Are ownership and integration boundaries clear?
  • Is proof of business completion defined?

Consistency

  • Does the human page match the graph?
  • Do source refs point to the same behavior?
  • Do links and machine endpoints still work?

Machine-readable control

The queue is generated, not hand-maintained.

Promotion Readiness, Factual Review, and Evidence Coverage remain the source contracts. The queue only orders work.
