Signal interpretation

Do not over-read one attempt.

The scoring contract treats a signal as stable only after several attempts.

Weak

A dimension average below the weak threshold, or a case total below 12/21. Review soon and repeat a related case.

Watch

A result between the weak and solid thresholds. Keep the topic in normal rotation and compare several attempts.

Lead signal

A case total of 18/21 or higher. Maintain it with mixed and cross-process cases rather than extra reading.
