Signal interpretation

Do not over-read one attempt.

Use several attempts before treating a score as a stable weakness.

Weak

Average below 1.5 in a reasoning dimension, or below 12/21 in a track. Review soon and repeat a related case.

Watch

Average from 1.5 to below 2.2 in a dimension, or 12 to below 17/21 in a track. Keep it in normal rotation.

Solid

Average 2.2 or higher in a dimension, or 17/21 or higher in a track. Maintain it with mixed cases rather than extra reading.
