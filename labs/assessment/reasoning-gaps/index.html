Gap plan

Three thin cells, three explicit treatments.

The published corpus has one case in each cell. The response depends on the reasoning type, not on our desire to make every generator look universal.

AI/Data · Diagnose

Existing review signal: CAND-AIAG-RAW-MCP survived semantic review. Do not generate a second Diagnose candidate before the first promotion decision.

Sales · Design

New review candidate: choose between stock, make-to-order, and third-party supply for a new offering. The answer must compare operating models and state what changes the choice.

AI/Data · Challenge

New review candidate: challenge an autonomous-agent proposal for a mostly deterministic SAP process. Compare workflow, bounded agent, and broader autonomy.

Design candidate

Choose the supply model before the document type.

RCAND-SALES-DESIGN-SUPPLY-MODEL tests whether the learner can choose a sales-to-supply operating model from business constraints rather than SAP familiarity.

Alternatives

Sell from stock · Make to order · Third-party supply.

Decision pressure

Customer lead time, demand uncertainty, customization, working capital, supplier dependency, inventory ownership, ATP, billing, and financial consequences.

Lead signal

Choose a default plus exception rules, explain ownership, and name the business condition that would change the design.

Challenge candidate

Challenge autonomy where deterministic control is cheaper.

RCAND-AI-CHALLENGE-AUTONOMY tests whether the learner can separate the part that needs probabilistic reasoning from approvals, authorization, writes, and recovery that should remain controlled.

Alternatives

Deterministic workflow · Bounded agent · More open-ended agent.

Decision pressure

Task variability, side-effect risk, latency, auditability, identity, tool scope, fallback, trajectory evaluation, and post-action verification.

Lead signal

Accept only the autonomy that the operating model can prove, observe, recover, and govern.

Pattern contract

Design and Challenge need different proof.

A good Design answer makes a choice. A good Challenge answer attacks an assumption and defines when it becomes acceptable.
