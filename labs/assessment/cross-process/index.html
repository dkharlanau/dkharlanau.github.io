Answer frame

Use seven moves.

  1. 01Business goal

    What outcome is actually required?

  2. 02Owner

    Which domain owns the first important decision?

  3. 03Flow

    Which documents and events connect the domains?

  4. 04Decision logic

    Which master data, configuration, or rule shapes the next state?

  5. 05Boundary

    Where does responsibility cross module, company, or system?

  6. 06Evidence

    How do you prove the first wrong state?

  7. 07Trade-off

    What does your fix protect, and what can it break?

What good looks like

Do not solve the last document first.

A Lead answer should explain where the process first became inconsistent and how the correction preserves downstream traceability.

Strong signal

  • Starts from business outcome
  • Names system/domain ownership
  • Builds a hypothesis tree
  • Uses document and value evidence
  • Checks integration and financial consequences

Weak signal

  • Lists transaction codes
  • Repairs the final document manually
  • Assumes the visible module owns the cause
  • Calls a green interface "complete"
  • Ignores reversals and reconciliation

Lead challenge

Explain what changes your decision. A design without conditions and trade-offs is just a confident default.
