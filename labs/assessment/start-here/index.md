  <section class="research-canvas__method" data-reveal>
    <div><p class="research-canvas__eyebrow">Answer frame</p><h2>Use seven moves, then stop.</h2></div>
    <ol>
      <li><span>01</span><strong>Business goal</strong><p>State the outcome before the SAP mechanism.</p></li>
      <li><span>02</span><strong>Owner</strong><p>Name the domain or system that owns the decision or durable state.</p></li>
      <li><span>03</span><strong>Flow</strong><p>Trace the important documents, events, and state changes.</p></li>
      <li><span>04</span><strong>Decision logic</strong><p>Explain the master data, configuration, rules, or algorithms that shape the result.</p></li>
      <li><span>05</span><strong>Boundary</strong><p>Show where responsibility crosses module, system, company, or partner.</p></li>
      <li><span>06</span><strong>Proof</strong><p>State what evidence proves the first wrong state and the corrected result.</p></li>
      <li><span>07</span><strong>Trade-off</strong><p>Explain why the chosen solution is reasonable and what would change the decision.</p></li>
    </ol>
  </section>

    <div class="ecg-decision-columns">
      <div><h3>Mon–Thu</h3><ul><li>20–30 min: one domain mechanism</li><li>20 min: one assessment case</li><li>10 min: follow-up questions</li><li>5 min: note one weak dimension</li></ul></div>
