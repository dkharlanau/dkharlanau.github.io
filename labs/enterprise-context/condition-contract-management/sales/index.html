Study route: learn the CCM core model first. Use this page only for the Sales-specific differences: customer side, billing-based business volume, customer settlement, and Sales diagnostics.

Boundary: SD pricing calculates document conditions. Sales CCM manages a customer agreement whose final value depends on accumulated eligible billing business volume and later settlement.

Back to the CCM core model arrow_back

What changes on the Sales side

Customer → billing volume → customer settlement.

The shared CCM engine does not change. The Sales-specific part is where the business volume comes from and which commercial relationship is settled.

Partner side

The condition contract represents an agreement with a customer or customer group.

Business volume

Customer billing data is the common evidence base for a standard sales-rebate scenario. Prove the eligible billed population before debugging the percentage.

Commercial result

Settlement produces the customer-side rebate result and connects it to tax and Financial Accounting according to the configured process.

First diagnostic question

Does the contract see the billing items that the business believes are eligible? If not, stay in volume selection before moving to settlement pricing.
