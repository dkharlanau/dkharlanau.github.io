Blocks and release

A release is a risk decision, not a technical repair.

When a negative result creates a credit block, the next step belongs to the credit-control process. The analyst should understand the failed checks and then release, reject, or keep the block according to policy.

Negative Check→ Credit Block→ Documented Credit Decision→ Release / Reject / Keep→ Delivery / PGI Continues or Stops
