Problem: teams often call every large data movement a migration. Then an initial-load tool becomes a nightly interface, or a spreadsheet becomes an application architecture.

Working rule. Classify the need first: initial migration, recurring integration, mass maintenance, or controlled user upload. The tool choice comes after that.
