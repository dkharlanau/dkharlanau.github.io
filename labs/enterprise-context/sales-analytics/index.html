C_S4CS / Public Edition

Six course topics, six different jobs.

The current SAP Sales Automation and Analytics course contains six units. They belong together because they improve Sales execution and decision-making, but they do not solve the same problem. Keep the business job and the evidence boundary separate.

01

Omnichannel Convergent Billing (1MC)

Bring eligible external billing data into SAP S/4HANA Cloud as billing document requests and combine it with internal billing due items when the business needs one customer invoice. The learning point is the billing-source boundary: external billing data can join the billing process, but it does not become a sales order or delivery.

Ask: what is the billing source, what object reaches the billing due list, and what must be compatible before items can be combined?
02

Sales Order Fulfillment Monitoring and Operations (BKK)

Find current orders with fulfillment issues, understand which process phase is affected, and move from the issue to the document or role that can act. This is operational exception handling. It is not the same job as a monthly KPI trend or a predictive model.

Ask: which orders need action now, where is the first unresolved phase, and who owns the next step?
03

AI-based sales order entry

One current Public Edition lesson shows how unstructured purchase-order data can be extracted into a sales order request, completed, checked and then used to create the actual sales order. Treat extracted or proposed values as preparation evidence, not as proof that a valid sales order already exists.

Ask: what was extracted, what was determined, what is still incomplete, and what validation happens before creation?
04

Planning Apps for Sales (1O0)

Create and release sales plans, then compare planned values with actual Sales performance. A plan is a target model for a period and selected dimensions. It is not a transactional promise and not the same as an analytical observation of what already happened.

Ask: what are we planning, at which grain, for which period, and which actual measure will be compared with it?
05

SAP Fiori Analytical Apps for Sales (1BS)

Use embedded analytical views to understand measures such as incoming orders, sales volume, confirmations, delivery performance, returns or open sales, and drill into the dimensions or business documents behind them. The chart is useful only when its measure, population and date basis are understood.

Ask: what exactly is measured, which documents are included, and what operational evidence explains the result?
06

Predictive Analytics Model Training — Sales (2YJ)

Train and evaluate a predictive model on historical business data, activate a suitable model version, and consume the prediction in the relevant Sales app. A prediction is an estimate derived from a trained model; it must not be confused with current document status or a guaranteed business outcome.

Ask: which outcome is predicted, which historical data supports the model, and how do we judge whether the active model is usable?

Recall rule: BKK tells us where current fulfillment needs action; 1BS tells us how a measured Sales situation looks across dimensions; 1O0 adds a plan to compare against; 2YJ estimates a future or unknown outcome; AI-based order entry changes how demand is captured; 1MC changes which billing sources can converge into the billing process.

Applied study case

One symptom can require more than one analytical view.

Assume a sales manager sees a weak delivery-performance trend while an internal sales representative has several orders that cannot move forward. The two observations are related, but they are not the same question.

  1. Use 1BS for the pattern. Confirm which delivery-performance measure moved and segment it by the relevant business dimensions. Do not call the first visible correlation a root cause.
  2. Use BKK for the current exceptions. Open the affected fulfillment situations, identify the failing phase or block, and follow the document relationship to the owner who can act.
  3. Use the KPI model for consistency. State the population, date basis, numerator/denominator or event pair before comparing teams or periods.
  4. Add 1O0 only when the question is plan versus actual. A plan explains the target; it does not explain why an individual order is blocked.
  5. Add 2YJ only when the question is predictive. Check the trained model and its quality before using a prediction as decision input. Keep prediction separate from actual order or delivery status.

If you can explain why each tool enters the story at a different point, you understand more than the app names. You can connect observation, evidence and action without turning analytics into a dashboard tour.
