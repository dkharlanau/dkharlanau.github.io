Five useful boundaries: Agentic ≠ autonomous. Memory ≠ business state. Structured output ≠ correct output. Retry ≠ recovery. Tool access ≠ business authority.

These boundaries are deliberately stronger than a long list of framework names because frameworks change faster than the architecture problems.
