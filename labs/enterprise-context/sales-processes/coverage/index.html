Complaint decision map

Start with what the customer actually needs.

“Complaint” is not a process. First decide whether goods move, money changes, or the customer needs replacement. That decision determines the document family and the downstream systems.

Goods come back?

Simple physical return

SD.RET.LEAN when receipt back to valuated stock and credit are enough. No inspection-driven disposition is required.

Goods come back?

Inspect and decide

SD.RET.ARM when inspection, follow-up activity, refund logic, supplier/plant routing, scrap, repair or replacement must be controlled.

Goods stay with customer?

Reduce the receivable

SD.CLM.CMR for a commercial credit without reverse logistics.

Goods stay with customer?

Increase the receivable

SD.CLM.DMR when an additional customer charge is required without changing the physical flow.

Existing invoice too low?

Increase that invoice

SD.CLM.INC when an existing invoice is the explicit reference and the system calculates the delta.

Invoice itself is wrong?

Correct the billed value

SD.CLM.ICR when copied versus corrected invoice values matter.

Customer needs goods, not money?

Replace free of charge

SD.CLM.FOS when shortage or damage is resolved by a subsequent free delivery.

Kit came back?

Return components safely

SD.RET.KIT when commercial structure and reverse logistics live at different levels.

Repair instead of refund?

Hand off to Service

SD.RET.REPAIR makes the boundary to in-house repair explicit.

New production and project edge

Not every customer-specific order is the same kind of MTO.

Ordinary MTO, direct assembly processing, Engineer-to-Order and project-based services use different execution and cost objects. The customer sees an order; internally the responsibility may move to a production order, project stock, WBS, customer project, or subscription contract.

SD.MTO.Customer-specific production

MTO normal individual demand · MTO.VC configured demand · MTO.ATO immediate assembly object.

SD.ETOProject manufacturing

WBS, project stock, engineering and project profitability become part of the supply model.

SD.PROJ.PBSProject-based services

Customer project execution creates billing evidence instead of a normal stock-delivery chain.

SD.SUB.RECURRecurring physical delivery

A subscription-style contract owns the lifecycle; generated sales orders execute each delivery cycle.
