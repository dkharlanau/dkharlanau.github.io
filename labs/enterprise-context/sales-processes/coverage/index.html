Complaint decision map

Start with what the customer actually needs.

“Complaint” is not a process. First decide whether goods move, money changes, or the customer needs replacement. That decision determines the document family and the downstream systems.

Goods come back?

Simple physical return

SD.RET.LEAN when receipt back to valuated stock and credit are enough. No inspection-driven disposition is required.

Goods come back?

Inspect and decide

SD.RET.ARM when inspection, follow-up activity, refund logic, supplier/plant routing, scrap, repair or replacement must be controlled.

Goods stay with customer?

Reduce the receivable

SD.CLM.CMR for a commercial credit without reverse logistics. Think forgotten discount, price concession or accepted claim.

Goods stay with customer?

Increase the receivable

SD.CLM.DMR when an additional customer charge is required without changing the physical flow.

Existing invoice too low?

Increase that invoice

SD.CLM.INC when an existing invoice is the explicit reference and the system should calculate the delta to a corrected price or quantity.

Invoice itself is wrong?

Correct the billed value

SD.CLM.ICR when the dispute is fundamentally about an existing invoice and copied versus corrected values matter.

Customer needs goods, not money?

Replace free of charge

SD.CLM.FOS when shortage or damage is resolved by a subsequent free delivery rather than a financial credit.

Kit came back?

Return components safely

SD.RET.KIT when the original commercial product was a kit but returned logistics can happen at component level.

Repair instead of refund?

Hand off to Service

SD.RET.REPAIR keeps the return trace in Sales and makes the boundary to in-house repair explicit.

What was missing

Returns were one card. They are now a family.

The parent code SD.RET stays useful as a concept. Child codes hold the decisions that change stock quality, refund timing, component handling, or the handoff to Service. Claims use SD.CLM.* because they do not automatically imply a physical return.

SD.RET.Reverse logistics

LEAN · ARM · KIT · REPAIR

SD.CLM.Customer claim

CMR credit · DMR debit · ICR correction · INC increase · FOS replacement

SD.BILL.Billing lifecycle

COLL consolidation · PRELIM negotiation · RETRO repricing · CANCEL reversal

SD.*.*Meaningful child variant

TPO.SN · TPO.NSN · TPO.VC · MTO.VC · SA.CON
