Special fulfillment / SD + MM

The supplier can ship to the customer or to you. That one choice changes the process.

Third-party processing and individual purchase orders both create procurement from a sales order. The decisive difference is where the supplier sends the goods and whether your company ever owns physical stock before customer delivery.

Third-Party Sales

Atlas Germany sells P-EXT, supplier V1000 ships directly to C1000.

DE01 owns the customer relationship but does not execute the physical delivery. The sales order creates the procurement requirement, Purchasing buys from V1000, and the vendor ships to the customer.

Sales order / TAS→ Purchase requisition→ Purchase order to V1000→ Vendor ships to C1000→ Supplier invoice or statistical GR signal→ Customer billing
local_shipping

No SD outbound delivery from DE11. If your design requires the selling company to receive, pick, and ship the goods, you are no longer describing the classic third-party branch.

Individual Purchase Order

The same external supplier now delivers to our plant.

C1000 orders a special product that DE01 does not stock. The sales order creates an order-specific procurement requirement. V1000 delivers to DE11. Goods are received with reference to the sales order and are reserved for that demand. DE01 then ships to the customer.

Sales order / TAB→ Purchase requisition→ Purchase order→ GR 101 to sales order stock E→ Outbound delivery→ PGI + F2

Pitfalls

Most errors come from mixing the two physical flows.

Third-party is not “receive then ship”

If a warehouse delivery from DE11 exists by design, check whether TAB / individual procurement is the real requirement.

Billing relevance is configurable

Do not memorize one billing-relevance letter without the process. First identify whether billing waits for supplier invoice data, statistical goods receipt, or another configured quantity signal.

The PR is evidence

If the sales order is correct but no purchase requisition appears, inspect schedule-line determination and procurement settings before blaming Purchasing.

Sales-order stock is not free stock

In individual procurement, received stock is assigned to the customer demand. Do not expect another sales order to consume it.
