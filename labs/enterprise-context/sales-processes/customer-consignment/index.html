Special stock / customer site

The goods can sit at the customer and still belong to you.

Customer consignment separates physical location from ownership. Fill-up moves stock to the customer site without selling it. Issue transfers ownership and creates the commercial event. Pick-up and return reverse different parts of that cycle.

Assessment recall

Explain consignment with ownership, not document codes.

record_voice_over

60-second answer: In customer consignment, goods are physically stored at the customer but remain our property until the customer withdraws them. Fill-up moves stock into customer consignment special stock and normally has no billing. Consignment issue records the customer's withdrawal, transfers ownership, and is billing relevant. Consignment pick-up brings unused consignment stock back to our plant. Consignment return is different: it reverses a previous issue by moving returned goods back into consignment stock and can create the financial reversal. The key control is always the stock and ownership state before the document.

Synthetic example

Atlas Germany keeps 100 pieces of P-100 at customer C1000.

C1000 wants immediate access to stock but pays only when it consumes the material. SAP keeps this stock separately for the customer, commonly using customer consignment special stock.

Document flows

Keep the four flows separate.

Fill-up

KB order→Outbound delivery→PGI / stock transfer→Customer consignment stock

Issue

KE order→Withdrawal from consignment→Ownership transfer→Billing

Pick-up

KA order→Return delivery→Own plant stock→No sales invoice

Return after issue

KR order→Goods back to consignment→Commercial reversal

Pitfalls

The dangerous shortcut is “goods at customer = sold.”

Fill-up is not revenue

Physical movement alone does not transfer ownership. The stock remains ours until issue.

Pick-up is not return

Pick-up retrieves stock that was never sold. Return reverses stock that was already issued to the customer.

Availability needs the right stock segment

Do not compare customer consignment quantity with unrestricted plant stock and conclude that stock disappeared.

Billing follows ownership

When troubleshooting an invoice, first identify whether ownership actually transferred in the process.
