Special process / cross-company fulfillment

One customer sale can create two commercial relationships.

Intercompany sales starts like a normal customer order, but the selling company and the delivering company are different legal entities. That creates two value flows: the external sale to the customer and the internal charge between group companies.

Assessment recall

Explain classical and advanced intercompany as two designs for the same business problem.

record_voice_over

60-second answer: In intercompany sales, one legal entity sells to the external customer and another group company delivers the goods. In the classical process, the customer order and delivery stay in the selling sales process, while intercompany billing creates the internal charge. The key controls are plant-to-sales-area assignments, the internal customer, intercompany pricing, billing type IV, and copy control. Advanced Intercompany Sales makes the internal supply relationship explicit with an intercompany purchase order, an intercompany sales order, valuated stock in transit, transfer-of-control dates, and Value Chain Monitoring. I choose the design based on release, accounting and stock-in-transit requirements, and the required level of orchestration.

Classical intercompany sales

Atlas Germany sells. Atlas France delivers.

Synthetic example: customer C1000 buys material P-100 from Atlas Germany (company code DE01, sales organization DE10). Stock is held in French plant FR11, which belongs to Atlas France (company code FR01).

Customer order in DE10→ Delivering plant FR11→ Outbound delivery→ PGI in FR01→ F2 customer invoice from DE01→ IV intercompany invoice FR01 → DE01

Customer relationship

DE01 owns the external customer contract and invoices C1000.

Physical fulfillment

FR11 picks, ships, and posts goods issue. Inventory leaves FR01.

Internal relationship

FR01 charges DE01 using intercompany billing and an internal transfer price.

Simple value example: customer price = EUR 150, intercompany transfer price = EUR 120, inventory cost in FR01 = EUR 100. DE01 keeps EUR 30 margin before other costs; FR01 keeps EUR 20. The split is created by the internal transfer price, not by the customer invoice.

Classical configuration

Configuration connects the legal entities to one delivery.

The exact menu labels can vary by release. The control logic is stable: authorize the plant for the selling sales area, define the delivering company's sales area for intercompany billing, create the internal partner, and connect billing and pricing.

Advanced Intercompany Sales

The internal supply leg becomes an explicit value chain.

Advanced Intercompany Sales is available for SAP S/4HANA 2022 and later releases in the relevant deployment scope. SAP scope item 5D2 uses Value Chain Monitoring to orchestrate the internal purchase and sales relationship, internal billing, supplier invoice, and valuated stock-in-transit steps.

Customer-facing sales order→ Intercompany PO→ Intercompany sales order→ Outbound delivery→ Transfer of control / VSiT→ External + internal billing

troubleshoot

Lead diagnostic order: legal boundary → delivering plant → master-data extensions → internal partner → pricing → delivery / PGI → customer billing → internal billing → FI. For Advanced Intercompany Sales, insert PO/SO orchestration, transfer-of-control dates, and Value Chain Monitoring before the billing checks.

Extension readiness

Transfer of control

Internal invoicing

Advanced releases provide BAdIs around intercompany supplier-invoice behavior and mapping. Use them only for a clear business exception.
