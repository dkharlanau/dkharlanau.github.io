Enterprise structure / organizational context

Organizational units are boundaries for responsibility and process context.

In Sales, we should not treat the enterprise structure as a hierarchy to memorize. It is closer to an architecture model. Some units define a legal or accounting boundary, some define the commercial context, and others define where fulfillment is executed. Together they tell SAP which data is valid, which combinations are allowed, who owns the transaction, and where the process crosses into logistics or finance.

account_tree

Architecture vocabulary. SAP calls these objects organizational units or elements of the enterprise structure. On this page we also use terms such as boundary, context key, execution node, and responsibility overlay. These are learning terms, not SAP object names.

Memory rule: COMPANY CODE = financial truth · SALES ORG = who sells · CHANNEL = how we go to market · DIVISION = what product family · SALES AREA = commercial context · PLANT = where supply is planned or held · SHIPPING POINT = who executes the outbound delivery.

FI

Company Code

Role: legal and accounting boundary. It is the smallest unit for which a complete set of external financial statements can be produced.

Why Sales cares: every sales organization is assigned to one company code, so revenue and receivables ultimately land in a defined accounting entity.

Architecture view: financial ownership boundary.

SELL

Sales Organization

Role: the central commercial selling unit. Sales documents are processed in a sales organization.

Why it exists: a group may need different selling responsibilities by country, region, business model, or product responsibility while still posting into the same company code.

Architecture view: commercial responsibility boundary.

GTM

Distribution Channel

Role: the route-to-market dimension, for example direct sales, wholesale, retail, or another meaningful sales channel.

Why it exists: the same seller may use different commercial rules, product availability, prices, or customer data depending on how the customer is served.

Architecture view: go-to-market dimension.

WHAT

Division

Role: groups saleable products or services into a product line or responsibility area.

Why it exists: product families may need different customer agreements, analysis, responsibilities, or commercial treatment.

Architecture view: product-segmentation dimension.

CTX

Sales Area

Role: the combination Sales Organization + Distribution Channel + Division.

Why it matters: it defines an allowed commercial context and is a key scope for customer sales data. It answers a useful assessment question: who sells what, and through which route?

Architecture view: composite commercial context key, not a physical location.

PAIR

Distribution Chain

Role: the combination Sales Organization + Distribution Channel.

Why it matters: this pair is important when Sales connects to fulfillment. Delivering plants are assigned to distribution chains, so the combination links the commercial route to possible supply locations.

Architecture view: commercial-to-logistics bridge.

SUPPLY

Plant

Role: a logistics unit that can produce, plan, store, distribute, or provide services depending on the scenario.

Why Sales cares: the delivering plant is a major input for availability, sourcing, shipping-point determination, stock, valuation, and fulfillment.

Architecture view: fulfillment and planning node. A plant belongs to one company code but can serve several distribution chains.

SHIP

Shipping Point

Role: organizational unit for outbound delivery processing and goods issue.

Why it exists: the place or shipping responsibility that executes outbound work can be different from the broader plant. Each outbound delivery is managed by one shipping point.

Architecture view: shipping execution node.

PEOPLE

Sales Office & Sales Group

Role: responsibility overlays for the regional office and the sales team.

Why they are different: they help represent who manages the customer or market without changing the core Sales Area key.

Architecture view: organizational responsibility overlays rather than the primary commercial context.

Read the relationships as a graph

01finance

Company Code → Sales Organization

Which accounting entity owns the selling unit?

Relationship

A sales organization is assigned to one company code. A company code can have several sales organizations.

Design meaning

Do not create a new company code merely because Sales wants another market view. Company code is an accounting boundary; sales organization is the commercial boundary.

02commercial

Sales Organization + Channel + Division → Sales Area

Which combinations of seller, route-to-market, and product line are valid?

Relationship

The Sales Area is the valid three-part commercial context used by Sales processes and sales-area-dependent customer data.

Design meaning

Every new Sales Area creates another context that may require data, pricing, authorization, testing, reporting, and support. More combinations increase flexibility, but also increase operating cost.

03fulfillment

Distribution Chain ↔ Plant

Which supply locations may serve this selling route?

Relationship

A plant can serve several distribution chains, and a distribution chain can work with several plants. The plant itself belongs to one company code.

Design meaning

This is where commercial design meets logistics. If the selling sales organization and delivering plant belong to different company codes, the process crosses a legal/accounting boundary and intercompany logic can become relevant.

04shipping

Plant ↔ Shipping Point

Who executes the outbound delivery?

Relationship

Plants and shipping points can have many-to-many assignments. The responsible shipping point is determined for delivery processing and each outbound delivery is handled by one shipping point.

Design meaning

Plant describes the broader supply location. Shipping point describes the outbound execution responsibility. They solve different problems and should not be modeled as the same thing.

Why SAP needs these layers

Scope data

Customer and product behavior is not always global. Organizational context lets the same business partner or product behave differently in a defined commercial or logistics scope.

Constrain combinations

The enterprise structure prevents arbitrary combinations. A valid Sales Area or plant assignment makes the allowed operating model explicit.

Assign responsibility

The model separates legal ownership, commercial ownership, supply responsibility, and shipping execution instead of hiding all responsibility in one department code.

Drive determinations

Organizational values are inputs to pricing, partner data, plant selection, shipping, billing, reporting, authorizations, and other decisions.

Connect modules

Company Code connects Sales to Finance. Plant connects Sales to MM/PP and Inventory. Shipping Point connects the order to delivery execution. The model is deliberately cross-functional.

Control blast radius

A shared organizational assignment can affect many customers, materials, and documents. A Lead should understand the scope before changing enterprise-structure configuration.

Business Partner / Multiple Address Handling

One legal identity can have many operational addresses.

Multiple Address Handling changes the data model behind Sales. Instead of representing every physical location as another customer master, we can keep one business partner for the legal entity and attach several valid addresses to it. Sales documents then select the address that fits the partner function and business context.

hub

Memory model: IDENTITY → ROLE → ADDRESS → ADDRESS DATA → DOCUMENT.

A partner function answers who plays the role? An address answers which physical location of that partner is relevant now? Do not create another BP only because the same legal entity has another warehouse, office, or billing location.

Why SAP introduced this model

Old representation

The classic customer master model has one standard address per customer master. A legal entity with several physical locations was therefore often represented by several customer records, and usually several linked BP records.

Problem

The same legal identity is duplicated. Name, tax, credit, commercial, and relationship data can drift because the records are maintained separately.

New representation

One BP/customer can hold several physical addresses. Each address can have semantic usage, validity, and selected address-dependent attributes.

Architecture meaning

The legal identity stays stable while physical endpoints become child contexts. This reduces identity duplication and moves location-specific behavior to the address level.

Five layers to understand

01identity

Business Partner

Which legal or business entity are we dealing with?

Stable object

The BP represents the business identity. Customer roles and sales-area data make that identity usable in Sales.

Lead rule

Do not split identity only to represent geography. Split the BP when the business entity, governance, tax, credit, or contractual responsibility is genuinely different.

02role

Partner Function

What role does the partner play in this document?

Examples

Sold-to, ship-to, bill-to, payer, and contact person are document roles. They are not addresses.

Important consequence

The same BP can have several valid addresses for the same partner function. For example, one ship-to customer can have several valid delivery addresses.

03address

Address Usage and Validity

Which addresses are valid for this purpose and date?

Semantic usage

Addresses can be classified for purposes such as standard address, delivery address, or billing address. Address determination restricts which addresses are proposed for each partner function.

Time dimension

Addresses and their usages can have validity periods. Sales documents check validity when proposing and saving addresses, which matters for branch moves and planned address changes.

04behavior

Address-Dependent Data

Does business behavior change when the physical location changes?

Examples

Address-dependent customer data can include sales office, sales group, delivery block, shipping condition, delivering plant, Incoterm locations, sales tax classification, external references, unloading points, and receiving points.

Precedence

When both address-dependent and ordinary customer-dependent values exist for a supported field, the address-dependent value has priority. Selecting another address can therefore change document behavior, not only the printed street.

05document

Address Selection in SD Documents

Which valid address should this transaction consume?

Sales document

During creation or change, the system proposes suitable addresses for partner functions and allows an alternative valid address to be selected at header or item level where supported.

Downstream

The selected address and supported address-dependent data are propagated into follow-on processing. Delivery and billing consume the chosen context, although address selection is not generally a free-change function in delivery and billing apps.

Address determination is part of partner determination

Address type

Customizing defines semantic address types such as delivery and billing addresses.

Address determination transaction

The address type is linked to an address-determination purpose. Standard examples include BBP003 for sending goods and BBP004 for sending bills.

Partner function assignment

Ship-to and bill-to partner functions can be assigned to the corresponding address-determination transaction. This lets partner determination narrow the valid address set.

Save-time control

The system performs a final validity check when the document is saved. A selected address that is no longer valid should not silently remain part of a new transaction.

Example: one customer, three warehouses

warehouse

Assume one legal customer has warehouses in Berlin, Hamburg, and Munich. We do not need three customer identities only to represent these locations. One BP can carry three delivery addresses. Each address can have its own unloading point, receiving hours, shipping condition, or delivering plant where supported.

In the sales order, the ship-to partner can remain the same BP while the selected Business Partner Address changes. That choice can then change fulfillment inputs. This is why an address problem can look like a shipping or plant-determination problem downstream.

Document-specific address vs master-data address

Master-data address

Reusable BP address with address usage, validity, and supported address-dependent data. This is the preferred source when the location is a stable business fact.

Document-specific address

A user can manually change an address for one document in supported apps. SAP stores it as a document-specific address and retains the previous BP-derived address as a BP reference address.

Lead risk

If users repeatedly overwrite addresses in documents, the master-data model may be wrong. A stable branch or warehouse should normally be governed as master data, not recreated manually in every order.

API and integration boundary

Address identity

Sales APIs can use BusinessPartnerAddressUUID to identify the exact BP address that the document should consume.

Do not send only text

For governed integrations, address text alone is weak identity. When the API supports it, use the BP and the stable address UUID so that the receiving process can keep the link to master data.

Partner limitations

In the Sales Order A2X API, BP address UUIDs are supported for customer-type partner functions except Payer, and for contact-person partner functions. Check the exact API and release before generalizing this rule to every object.

Document-specific reference

APIs also expose a reference UUID when a document-specific address replaces a BP-derived address. This preserves the lineage between the transactional override and its original master-data address.

Migration and activation are architecture decisions

Aactivate

Enable the data model before SD consumption

On-premise / Private-style configuration

SAP documents two capabilities: the BP/customer master-data function for address-dependent data and the Q2C function that enables SD documents to consume multiple BP addresses. The master-data capability is enabled first.

Irreversibility

For systems using these business functions, activation is irreversible. It does not automatically consolidate existing customers, so migration planning is a separate workstream.

Bconsolidate

Create a Golden Record only when the records represent one entity

Goal

Multiple customer records that represent the same legal entity can be consolidated into one leading BP, with former record-specific attributes moved to address-dependent entities where supported.

Do not force consolidation

If legally important data cannot be harmonized, the records may need to remain separate. Different physical addresses do not prove that two records are the same legal entity.

Cintegrate

Review every external contract that identifies customers by old customer number

Why

Consolidation changes the relationship between legacy customer numbers, BP identity, partner functions, and physical addresses. EDI mappings, APIs, output, analytics, and downstream replicas can all depend on the old model.

Proof

Test order creation, partner/address determination, delivery, billing, output, interfaces, analytics, and historical-document display before cutover.

Diagnostic path

1

Wrong partner or wrong address?

First separate identity/partner determination from address determination. A correct BP with a wrong address is a different failure from a wrong ship-to partner.

2

Is the address valid?

Check address validity and address-usage validity for the document date.

3

Is the usage suitable?

A billing address should not automatically appear as a delivery address unless the configured usage allows it.

4

Which data came with the address?

If plant, Incoterms, shipping condition, tax classification, or unloading point changed, inspect address-dependent data and precedence.

5

Header or item?

An item-specific partner or address can diverge from the header. Always inspect the level where the wrong behavior occurs.

6

Master address or manual override?

Determine whether the document uses a BP-derived address or a document-specific address before correcting master data.

Assessment questions

  1. Why was Multiple Address Handling introduced? To represent one legal customer with several physical locations without multiplying customer identities, while allowing Sales documents to consume the correct address and location-specific data.
  2. What is the difference between partner function and address? Partner function defines the role in the process; address identifies the physical location of the selected partner for that role.
  3. Can one ship-to party have several ship-to addresses? Yes. One BP can have several valid addresses suitable for the same partner function.
  4. Can choosing another address change more than the street? Yes. Supported address-dependent data can be propagated to the document and can change fulfillment or commercial inputs.
  5. Why is this a migration topic? Existing duplicate customer records may need consolidation, external customer-number mappings can change, activation is not the same as consolidation, and integrations must preserve address identity.
  6. How would you diagnose a wrong delivering plant after the user selected another ship-to address? Confirm partner and selected BP address, then inspect address usage, validity, address-dependent delivering plant and precedence before changing plant determination logic.
