Important distinctions

Similar words do not mean the same control model.

PACKING

Packaging material ≠ returnable packaging

A packaging material helps create the physical packing structure. A returnable adds ownership, customer-held stock, return, and possible charging logic.

SERVICE

Sales service item ≠ service execution

The commercial line can describe what the customer buys. Service execution can use a different object for labor, parts, expenses, confirmations, and operational status.

STRUCTURE

Sales BOM ≠ Production BOM

The Sales structure answers how the product is sold and processed in the document. Production needs a structure that answers how the product is built. They can be related without being the same decision.

IDENTITY

Batch ≠ serial number

A batch identifies a quantity with common attributes. A serial number identifies an individual physical unit. Both improve traceability, but they solve different questions.
