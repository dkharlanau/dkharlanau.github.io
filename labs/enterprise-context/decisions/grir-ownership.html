Decision Card / Procurement + Finance

Who owns an open
GR/IR balance?

An open GR/IR balance is an accounting signal, but the cause often sits in a procurement or logistics state that did not complete as expected.

My default

01Prove quantityWhat was received and invoiced?
02Find ownerWhich business event is missing?
03Clear lastOnly after the process is understood
Do not turn reconciliation into account housekeeping.
rule

Decision in one sentence: Procurement or the relevant logistics owner should explain why receipt and invoice states differ; Finance owns accounting policy and controlled clearing, but should not be asked to erase an unexplained business mismatch.

Failure classes

Open GR/IR is a symptom with several owners.

Goods received, invoice missing

Confirm whether the supplier invoice is still expected, blocked elsewhere, or never reached invoice verification.

Invoice posted, receipt missing

Check whether the physical receipt is pending, the wrong PO/item was referenced, or the expected goods-receipt event will never occur.

Quantity difference

Compare PO history, receipt quantities, invoice quantities, reversals, returns, and final-delivery or final-invoice intent.

Price or value difference

Separate quantity reconciliation from purchase-price, invoice-price, exchange-rate, delivery-cost, or account-determination effects.

Process truly finished

If no further receipt or invoice is expected, controlled clearing may be appropriate, but only with business evidence for closure.

Ownership map

Accounting sees the balance. The process explains it.

QuestionPrimary ownerEvidence
Was the supplier commitment fulfilled?Procurement / supplier ownerPO, confirmation, open quantity, final delivery or cancellation state.
Was the material or service actually received?Warehouse, service receiver, or logistics ownerMaterial document, service entry, reversal, return, and physical evidence.
Was the supplier invoice correctly posted?Invoice verification / AP processInvoice document, quantity/value comparison, blocks, credit memo, reversal.
Can the residual balance be cleared?Finance with business confirmationProof that no further receipt or invoice is expected and the remaining balance is understood.
warning

Anti-pattern: treating MR11 or another clearing action as the diagnosis. Clearing is a financial action after the business state is explained, not a substitute for finding why the receipt and invoice did not reconcile.

Lead check: close with one sentence that proves physical completion, commercial completion, and accounting completion are aligned.

Evidence path

Start in the PO history and end in reconciliation.
