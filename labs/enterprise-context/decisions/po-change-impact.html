Decision Card / Procurement

What can break after
a purchase order change?

A PO change is not only a field update. I treat it as a change to a business contract that may already have approval, supplier communication, logistics execution, accounting, and external copies.

Impact path

01Changed meaningWhat business promise moved?
02Downstream copiesWho already consumed it?
03ReconcileHow do we prove alignment?
Document saved does not mean process synchronized.
rule

Decision in one sentence: before accepting a PO change, classify which business commitments changed, identify every downstream consumer that may already hold the old state, and define how approval, communication, execution, and financial reconciliation return to one consistent version.

Change classes

Different fields create different blast radiuses.

Quantity

Check open quantity, confirmations, receipts, invoice quantities, tolerances, and whether downstream documents already consume the original value.

Date or schedule

Check planning, supplier confirmation, inbound execution, warehouse timing, and any dependent customer or production promise.

Price or conditions

Check approval policy, supplier communication, invoice expectations, account effects, and whether a later invoice is compared with the old or new commercial state.

Plant, storage, or account assignment

Check whether the logistical or financial owner changed and whether existing follow-on documents still belong to the same process.

Supplier or source

Treat this as a major sourcing change, not a cosmetic correction. Revisit eligibility, communication, confirmations, and any external document already sent.

Impact model

Walk the process in six directions.

BoundaryQuestionEvidence of completion
ApprovalDoes the changed value require a new approval decision or make the previous approval no longer meaningful?The current document state is covered by the intended approval policy.
Supplier communicationHas the supplier already received a previous version, and how will the changed commitment be communicated?The supplier-side state matches the active PO state.
IntegrationWhich interfaces, messages, APIs, events, or external systems may hold the old value?Required consumers have the expected current state or a controlled correction path.
Logistics executionDo confirmations, inbound documents, receipts, services, warehouse tasks, or quality steps already exist?Open and completed quantities still reconcile with the changed contract.
FinanceHas the change affected expected invoice values, account assignment, liabilities, or GR/IR reconciliation?Physical and financial completion remain explainable together.
Dependent business processDid another process rely on the old date, quantity, source, or ownership?The dependent promise has been recalculated, accepted, or explicitly managed as an exception.
warning

Anti-pattern: “the PO saved successfully, therefore the change is complete.” Save success proves only that one application accepted the document state. It says nothing about supplier acknowledgement, external copies, open receipts, invoice expectations, or downstream business promises.

Recovery owner: the team changing the contract should know which downstream owners must confirm alignment. Technical teams can repair messages, but the process owner closes the reconciliation.

Assessment drill

Use one field change and trace it to completion.
