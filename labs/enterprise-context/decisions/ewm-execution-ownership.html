Decision Card / EWM

Who owns a warehouse
execution mismatch?

A delivery can be correct in S/4HANA while the warehouse execution is wrong, or EWM can confirm physical work while the ERP follow-on state is still missing. The visible system does not automatically own the cause.

My default

ASKS/4 deliveryBusiness request
DOEWM taskPhysical execution
PROVEReconcileWarehouse and ERP state
Physical completion and ERP completion are different proofs.
rule

Decision in one sentence: S/4HANA owns the business delivery or goods-movement request, EWM owns warehouse execution and warehouse-task confirmation, and the integration boundary must carry the confirmed result back so inventory and follow-on processes reconcile.

Trace

Follow the request into execution and back.

1. Business request

Start from the inbound, outbound, stock-transfer, or posting-change document and the quantity that the business expects to move.

2. Warehouse object

Check the distributed EWM document, warehouse request, warehouse task, warehouse order, stock, HU, batch, and serial context where relevant.

3. Physical execution

Prove what was actually picked, put away, staged, packed, loaded, or moved. A planned task is not execution evidence.

4. Confirmation

Check warehouse-task confirmation, exception codes, partial quantities, destination changes, and delivery confirmation.

5. ERP result

Reconcile S/4 delivery status, material document, goods receipt or goods issue, inventory quantity, and downstream billing or planning effects.

warning

Anti-pattern: post a manual goods issue or goods receipt in S/4HANA only to make ERP stock match a warehouse screen. That can hide the broken execution or interface state and create a second inconsistency.

I change the ownership model when: a non-SAP automation layer controls conveyors or machines. EWM can still own warehouse-management decisions while the automation layer owns equipment execution.

Lead proof: requested quantity, physical movement, EWM confirmation, ERP inventory, and financial or customer consequence all reconcile.

Scenario + evidence

Do not repair ERP before proving warehouse state.
