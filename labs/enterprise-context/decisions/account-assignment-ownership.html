Decision Card / Procurement

Where should procurement
cost ownership live?

The account assignment is not only an accounting field. It says which business object consumes the purchase and therefore which owner should explain the cost.

My default

01Business receiverWho consumes the purchase?
02Account categoryWhich object carries the cost?
03FI/COHow is value posted?
Do not use the G/L account to hide a wrong business receiver.
rule

Decision in one sentence: choose the account-assignment object from the business receiver of the purchase, then let account determination and FI/CO rules translate that ownership into postings.

Decision drivers

Start with consumption, not with the account number.

Operational expense

If a cost center consumes the service or material directly, the cost-center assignment should make that ownership visible.

Project work

If the purchase belongs to a project or WBS-controlled outcome, the project object should carry the cost and reporting responsibility.

Asset investment

If the purchase creates or improves an asset, the investment object is not interchangeable with a normal operating expense receiver.

Order-based work

Maintenance, production, internal orders, or customer-related work can require an order or sales-order-based receiver.

Shared cost

If several receivers consume the same purchase, multiple account assignment is a business allocation decision, not a spreadsheet correction after invoice posting.

Ownership split

Three decisions should stay separate.

DecisionOwner questionFailure signal
Business receiverWhich cost object should own this purchase?The spend is visible in the wrong project, cost center, asset, or order.
Account assignment categoryWhich type of receiver and required fields describe that ownership?The document allows the wrong data shape or misses the required receiver.
G/L and posting logicHow should the value hit accounting for the chosen business event?The business receiver is correct but the financial account or posting logic is wrong.
warning

Anti-pattern: changing the G/L account until the invoice posts. That can make accounting technically acceptable while the business cost still belongs to the wrong object.

Lead check: prove the receiver first, then the account-assignment category, then the automatic or manual account logic, and finally the downstream FI/CO result.
