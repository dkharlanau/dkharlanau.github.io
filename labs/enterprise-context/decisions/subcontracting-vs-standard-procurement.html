Decision Card / Special Procurement

Subcontracting or
standard external procurement?

The key question is not whether a supplier performs work. It is who owns the components, how they move, and what the goods receipt must consume and reconcile.

My default

STDStandard POSupplier owns its production inputs
SUBSubcontractingWe provide controlled components
PROOFReconcileFinished receipt + component consumption
Choose from ownership and material flow, not from the supplier contract name.
rule

Decision in one sentence: use subcontracting when your business owns or controls components that are provided to the supplier and their consumption must be connected to receipt of the ordered product; use standard external procurement when the supplier owns its input material and you buy the resulting product or service as the commercial object.

Decision drivers

Four questions separate the processes.

Who owns the components?

If company-owned components remain visible while physically located at the supplier, the inventory model matters and normal external procurement may be too weak.

Who plans component demand?

Subcontracting can connect the ordered assembly with components that must be provided, planned, and monitored for that supplier.

What does goods receipt mean?

The finished-material receipt can also be the point where component consumption is posted or reconciled. That is more than receiving a normal externally purchased material.

What variance must remain explainable?

Planned component quantities, supplier stock, scrap, subsequent adjustment, finished receipt, invoice, and GR/IR can all expose a different part of the same variance.

Comparison

The commercial PO can look similar while the ownership model is different.

warning

I change the design when: components are delivered directly by another supplier, special sales-order or project stock must remain separated, MRP must plan supplier-specific component provision, or the business needs another special-procurement variant.

Anti-pattern: using normal procurement and an offline spreadsheet to track components at the supplier. That removes the inventory and reconciliation problem from SAP without removing it from the business.
