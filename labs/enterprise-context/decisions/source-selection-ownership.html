Decision Card / Procurement

Where should supplier
selection logic live?

A supplier field in a PO is an output of several business rules. I first decide which object should own the rule, then I decide whether an extension is needed.

My default

01Standard sourcingUse the procurement model first
02Governed dataKeep validity and ownership explicit
03ExtensionAdd only for a real gap
Do not hide sourcing policy inside document exits.
rule

Decision in one sentence: keep supplier and source-of-supply policy in standard procurement objects when the rule fits their business meaning; use an extension only when the requirement cannot be represented clearly and the new owner, audit path, and fallback are explicit.

Decision drivers

Ask what the rule is trying to control.

Eligibility

Is the question which suppliers are allowed or blocked for a material, plant, purchasing organization, or time period?

Allocation

Does demand need to be distributed between valid sources rather than assigned to one fixed source?

Commercial relationship

Is the requirement about a purchasing relationship, agreement, conditions, units, or supplier-specific material information?

External optimization

Does the decision depend on data that SAP Procurement does not own, such as a planning or optimization result?

Override and audit

Who may override the result, for how long, and where can support explain why this supplier was selected?

Ownership model

Put each rule where its business meaning is visible.

Rule typePreferred ownerLead check
Allowed or fixed sourcesStandard source-of-supply controls and governed master data.Can a buyer and support team explain validity, plant scope, and block status without reading custom code?
Demand allocationQuota or another explicit allocation model.Is the allocation policy separate from supplier price and from technical interface routing?
Supplier-specific commercial dataPurchasing relationship objects, agreements, and conditions.Are price, unit, validity, and source eligibility treated as separate decisions?
External recommendationExternal engine proposes; SAP-side control validates and accepts.What happens when the recommendation is missing, stale, or conflicts with a blocked source?
Exceptional policyExtension with one named business owner.Is the rule testable, traceable, and removable, or is it permanent policy hidden in code?
warning

I change the default when: source selection depends on cross-enterprise optimization, supplier risk data outside the SAP ownership boundary, regulatory controls, or another system that is explicitly authoritative for the decision.

Failure ownership: a technically valid supplier value is still wrong when the sourcing policy, validity, or business authority is wrong. The owner of the selection rule must be visible before support changes the PO.
