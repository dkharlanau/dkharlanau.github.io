Decision Card / Integration Operations

When is an integration
retry safe?

A red message does not prove that the receiver changed nothing. Before replay, I prove the message identity, receiver state, duplicate behavior, ordering dependency, and the business effect that must exist after recovery.

My default

Retry is a business action, not a monitor button.
rule

Decision in one sentence: retry only when the business identity is stable, receiver commit state is understood, duplicate handling is safe, ordering constraints are known, and the recovered business result can be reconciled.

Five gates

Do not replay from technical status alone.

Identity

Use a stable message or business key. A new technical message ID can hide a duplicate business event.

Receiver state

Distinguish “not delivered” from “delivered but acknowledgement lost”. A timeout can leave the receiver committed.

Idempotency

Prefer duplicate handling close to the application that writes the business state. Middleware protection reduces risk but cannot always prove receiver commit state.

Ordering

Check whether another message depends on this one. A safe duplicate check does not make an out-of-order business sequence correct.

Reconciliation

After replay, prove the target document, quantity, value, status, and downstream effects expected by the business process.

Failure classes

The same retry button has different risk.

SituationDefault
Receiver proves nothing was committedRetry can be reasonable after the original cause is removed.
Receiver is idempotent with stable identityRetry is safer, but still check ordering and final business state.
Timeout after the receiver callDo not blind-retry. First prove whether the receiver committed.
Receiver is not idempotentUse stronger duplicate protection and accept that middleware-only handling can leave residual risk.
Business event has downstream side effectsReconcile before and after replay; transport success is not enough.
warning

Anti-pattern: “message is red, therefore resend it”. This can create duplicate orders, receipts, invoices, or follow-on events when the receiver already committed.

Lead check: name the retry owner, duplicate key, receiver guarantee, ordering rule, and post-replay evidence before approving reprocessing.

Scenario + evidence

Recover the business event, not only the message.
