Decision Card / Integration Operations

Who owns an
IDoc failure?

“The IDoc failed” is not an ownership model. The useful question is which boundary failed, who can correct it, and who proves that the business process finished.

My default

01ClassifyFind the failed boundary
02RecoverAssign the correcting team
03ReconcileProve business completion
Technical success is not the end state.
rule

Decision in one sentence: ownership should follow the failed boundary, but one process owner must remain accountable for end-to-end reconciliation so that a technically repaired IDoc does not become an invisible business gap.

Failure classification

Do not send every red status to the integration team.

Failure boundaryTypical ownerWhat must be checked
Transport / connectivityIntegration or platform operationsPartner connection, queue, RFC/HTTP destination, middleware route, certificate, network, retry policy.
Mapping / contractIntegration developmentMessage version, required fields, mapping logic, code conversion, schema compatibility.
SAP application processingFunctional or application supportCustomizing, document status, business rule, lock, posting prerequisite, application log.
Master dataData owner or governance teamMissing or invalid business partner, material, plant, purchasing, sales, tax, accounting, or mapping data.
Business exceptionBusiness process ownerWhether the message is valid at all, whether the business should proceed, and whether manual approval or cancellation is required.

Lead answer

Recovery needs two owners, not one vague queue.

Correction owner

The team that can remove the root cause. This may be integration, functional support, master data, security, or the business.

Process owner

The owner who confirms that the expected business document or state exists after recovery. This prevents “green middleware, broken business process”.

Replay owner

Replay must be controlled. The team needs to know whether the message is idempotent, whether duplicates are possible, and whether the target state already exists.

Problem owner

Repeated failures should leave incident mode. A recurring master-data or mapping defect belongs in problem management with a permanent corrective action.

warning

I reject the design when: ownership is based only on the technical component, replay is allowed without duplicate checks, monitoring stops at message status, or nobody reconciles the receiving business object.

Operational rule: classify first, correct second, replay only when safe, then reconcile the business outcome. If the same pattern repeats, convert it into a problem record and remove the cause.
