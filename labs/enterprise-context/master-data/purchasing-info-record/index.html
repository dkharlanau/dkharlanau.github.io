SAP Procurement / Relationship Data

Purchasing Info Record:
how this supplier supplies this material.

A supplier can supply many materials, and one material can have many suppliers. The Purchasing Info Record, or PIR, stores reusable purchasing data for one supplier-material relationship. It is a key source for price, delivery and ordering defaults in purchasing documents.

Start with the mental model arrow_downward
rule

Boundary: a PIR is not the supplier master and not the material master. It describes the supplier × material relationship. It can propose purchasing data, but Source List, Quota Arrangement, outline agreements and document references can still control or influence the actual source and price used in a purchase order.

Mental model

Think supplier × material × purchasing context.

If purchasing behavior is wrong only for one supplier-material pair, the info record is one of the first objects to inspect.

Supplier

Who provides the material?

Material

What is being procured?

Purchasing context

Which purchasing organization and, if used, which plant owns the relationship data?

Info record

What price, delivery time, order unit and supplier-specific purchasing defaults apply?

Info record category

The info type tells SAP which procurement process the record supports.

The supplier-material relationship is not always a standard external purchase. SAP separates the main procurement models by info record category.

Info type Use Lead-level distinction
Standard Normal external procurement from a supplier. The usual supplier-material purchasing relationship for purchase orders.
Subcontracting A supplier produces or processes a material using components provided by your company. The procurement model includes components supplied to the subcontractor and receipt of the finished or processed material.
Consignment Supplier-owned stock is stored at your site and becomes payable when consumed or withdrawn. Stock ownership and settlement logic differ from standard procurement.
Pipeline Continuous supply such as water, gas or another pipeline material. Procurement is consumption-oriented rather than based on normal physical deliveries.

Structure and organizational levels

Know where the field is stored before you change it.

PIR data is split between general supplier-material data and purchasing-organization data. Purchasing data can also be maintained for a plant when a more specific level is required.

Level Typical data Why it matters
Client / general data Supplier-material relationship data, supplier material number, order unit, texts, origin-related data and administration information. This is the reusable base of the relationship and is not limited to one purchasing organization.
Purchasing organization Purchasing controls, planned delivery time, tolerances, conditions, statistics and purchasing texts. The same supplier-material pair can have different purchasing behavior for different purchasing organizations.
Purchasing organization + plant More specific purchasing data and conditions where plant-level maintenance is used. Plant-specific values can model different supply conditions inside the same purchasing organization.
priority_high

Assessment trap: do not answer only “the PIR is supplier + material”. That is the relationship idea, but purchasing data is also scoped by purchasing organization and can be plant-specific.

Maintenance

A PIR can be maintained directly or updated from purchasing activity.

Manual maintenance is useful when the relationship must exist before the first order. Automatic update reduces repeated maintenance when purchasing documents already contain the relevant data.

Manual maintenance

In SAP GUI, ME11 / ME12 / ME13 are the classic create, change and display transactions. In Fiori, the available app depends on the S/4HANA deployment and role design.

Automatic creation or update

Purchasing documents can create or update an info record when the Info Update function is active and the document/process supports it.

Order price history

The PIR can keep purchasing history for the supplier-material relationship. This is useful for comparing previous order prices and dates.

Do not assume full overwrite

Automatic update does not mean that every PIR field is replaced by every purchase order. Always check which data the document and configuration update.

01

Create or change PO

The buyer enters supplier, material and purchasing data.

02

Info Update

The purchasing item is marked for info-record update where applicable.

03

Relationship data

SAP creates or updates relevant PIR data for the supplier-material context.

04

Next document

The updated PIR can propose data in later purchasing documents.

High-value fields

These fields often explain why one supplier-material pair behaves differently.

The exact field set depends on the info type and system design, but these are common diagnostic points.

Field or area Business meaning Typical effect
Order unit / conversion How the supplier expects the material to be ordered. Controls purchasing UoM proposals and conversion to the material base unit.
Supplier material number The material identifier used by the supplier. Supports supplier-facing communication and purchasing documents.
Planned delivery time Expected number of calendar days from ordering to receipt. Supports scheduling and planning expectations for this source.
Minimum / standard quantity and tolerances Supplier-specific quantity rules where maintained. Influences proposals and quantity checks.
Purchasing conditions Gross price, discounts, surcharges, freight and other pricing elements. Can provide a reusable price basis for the purchase order.
Texts Supplier-material-specific purchasing information. Can be copied into purchasing documents according to text determination.

Purchasing conditions

Price is a result of conditions, not one field.

Purchasing uses condition technique to combine price components. The final price depends on the active condition types, their records, calculation rules and the calculation schema.

Price concept Meaning Simple interpretation
Gross price Price before discounts and surcharges. Supplier list or base price.
Net price Gross price after relevant discounts and surcharges. The commercial item price after normal price adjustments.
Effective price Net price adjusted by additional purchasing cost effects such as cash discount, delivery costs, neutral provisions and non-deductible taxes. A broader view of the real procurement cost used by SAP for purchasing analysis.

Condition validity

Time-dependent and time-independent conditions behave differently.

This distinction is important because a master-data condition and a purchase-order condition do not have the same lifecycle.

Time-dependent conditions

They have validity dates and can contain scales, for example a lower price when the ordered quantity reaches a threshold.

Where they are common

Info records, contracts and general purchasing conditions use time-dependent conditions.

Purchase orders

Conditions in a purchase order are time-independent: they belong to that document and do not need a separate validity period.

Quotation / scheduling agreement

Whether time-dependent conditions are available can be controlled by the purchasing document type in Customizing.

functions

Important: time-dependent conditions do not form the same net-price/effective-price subtotals as time-independent document conditions. Treat master-data condition maintenance and document price calculation as related but different layers.

Condition technique

Condition type tells SAP what to calculate; access sequence tells SAP where to look.

Purchasing pricing follows the same general condition-technique idea used elsewhere in SAP, but with purchasing-specific condition types and schemas.

Condition type

Represents one price element such as gross price, discount, surcharge, freight or another cost. It also controls how the value is calculated: amount, percentage or quantity-based.

Condition record

Stores the actual value for a defined key and validity period, for example a supplier-material purchasing price.

Access sequence

Defines the search strategy for a condition type. SAP checks condition tables in sequence and normally moves from a more specific key to a more general one.

Calculation schema

Groups condition types and controls their order, calculation basis, subtotals, manual processing and prerequisites.

Schema control Question to ask
SequenceIn which order are price, discount, freight and other conditions calculated?
Calculation basisIs a percentage calculated from the gross price, a subtotal or another base?
Manual entryMay the buyer enter or change this condition manually?
RequirementUnder which business condition is this condition type active?
Subtotal / statistical treatmentDoes the value contribute to a subtotal or only provide information?
account_tree

Lead mental model: schema → condition type → access sequence → condition table → condition record. If the PO price is wrong, do not change the PIR immediately; first identify which pricing layer produced the condition.

Price determination

SAP searches for a valid price from specific context toward more general context.

The exact result depends on the purchasing document, its references, the active calculation schema, condition records and the organizational level of the available data.

01

Document context

Is the PO created with reference to a contract, quotation, requisition or another document?

02

Valid source data

Are valid purchasing conditions available for this supplier-material relationship?

03

Specific level first

Where the configuration supports it, a more specific organizational key such as plant context is checked before a more general purchasing-organization level.

04

Condition technique

Access sequences and condition records determine which condition values are found.

05

Schema calculation

The calculation schema combines the found conditions into the purchase-order price.

priority_high

Do not memorize one universal “price source hierarchy”. A referenced outline agreement, plant-specific condition, PIR, manual condition and other condition records can interact differently depending on the document and configuration. The reliable method is to inspect the PO condition analysis and trace the condition source.

One material, two suppliers, different relationships.

The PIR does not decide by itself which supplier must be selected. Source List, Quota Arrangement, contracts, availability of valid sources and purchasing decisions can still determine the final source.

Lead diagnosis

When the PO value is wrong, identify the owning layer.

A SAP Lead should trace the value instead of changing the nearest master record.

Symptom Check first Why
Wrong price for one supplier-material pair PIR conditions, validity, plant/purchasing-organization level, PO condition analysis The value may come from a more specific condition record or a document reference.
Wrong supplier is proposed Source List, Quota Arrangement, outline agreements, source determination PIR describes a source; it is not the only source-selection rule.
Wrong delivery lead time PIR planned delivery time and the final PO item value A supplier-specific lead time normally belongs to the relationship, not to generic material data.
Wrong order unit PIR order unit and conversion, then material UoM data The supplier can have a relationship-specific purchasing unit.
Price did not change after master-data update Existing PO conditions and pricing date / redetermination behavior An existing purchasing document keeps its own condition state unless it is explicitly redetermined according to system behavior.
PIR was not created or updated from PO Info Update indicator and purchasing document behavior Automatic update must be active and supported by the process.

Weak answer

“The price comes from the vendor master.”

Lead answer

“I would trace the PO pricing condition to its source. For a supplier-material relationship I would check the PIR, its organizational level and condition validity, but also document references, outline agreements, access sequences and the calculation schema.”

Assessment answer

A compact answer that shows structure and runtime understanding.

record_voice_over

“A Purchasing Info Record stores reusable data for a supplier-material relationship. It has general data at client level and purchasing data at purchasing-organization level, optionally plant-specific. The info type can be standard, subcontracting, consignment or pipeline. The PIR can hold order units, lead time, texts and time-dependent purchasing conditions. In a PO, price determination uses condition technique: condition types are organized in a calculation schema, and access sequences search condition records from specific keys toward more general keys. The PIR can also be created or updated from purchasing documents when Info Update is active.”
