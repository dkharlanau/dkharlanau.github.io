SAP Procurement / Business Partner

Supplier master: identity first, purchasing context second.

In S/4HANA, supplier master data is built on Business Partner. The same business identity can be a customer, a supplier, or both. Procurement behavior then depends on supplier roles and the purchasing and company-code views maintained for that context.

Start with the mental model arrow_downward
rule

Boundary: Do not use “vendor master” as one undifferentiated bucket. General BP data, supplier company-code data, purchasing-organization data, and supplier-material data have different keys and owners.

Mental model

One Business Partner can have several business roles.

The useful diagnostic question is not “Does the supplier exist?” but “Does the supplier exist in the role and organizational scope that this procurement process needs?”

Business Partner

Global identity, name, address, communication, and common relationship context.

Supplier role

Makes the BP usable as a supplier in the relevant S/4HANA business context.

Company code

Adds accounting and payment context.

Purchasing organization

Adds purchasing-specific defaults and partner data.

Key and scope

Know the key before you debug the value.

The same value can be correct in one object or organizational context and wrong in another.

Field or dimensionWhy it mattersTypical process effect
Business Partner / supplier roleDefines the legal/business identity and supplier capability.Allows procurement processes to use the BP as a supplier.
Company codeAccounting-specific supplier data.Connects supplier transactions to AP and payment context.
Purchasing organizationProcurement-specific data and defaults.Controls commercial and purchasing behavior by organization.
Partner functionsOrdering address, goods supplier, invoicing party, and similar roles.Allows different parties to perform different procurement roles.
Commercial defaultsOrder currency, Incoterms, payment-related defaults where maintained.Flows into purchasing documents unless a more specific source supplies another value.

Runtime use

Supplier data enters Procure-to-Pay at several boundaries.

Source determination, purchasing documents, goods receipt, invoice verification, and payment do not all read the same supplier view.

01

Source

Determine whether this supplier is a valid candidate for the material and plant.

02

Purchase

Read purchasing-organization defaults and supplier-material specifics.

03

Receive

Use supplier and delivery context during goods receipt and inbound processing.

04

Invoice

Use supplier accounting and invoice partner context.

05

Pay

Hand off to company-code and payment data in Finance.

Lead diagnosis

Find the owning layer before changing data.

A supplier can be globally valid and still fail in one purchasing organization, company code, source list, or supplier-material relationship.

Start here

Prove BP identity → supplier role → company code → purchasing organization → partner function → supplier-material/source data.

Do not jump here

Do not create duplicate suppliers to solve a missing organizational view. Fix the missing role or scope after confirming governance and identity.
