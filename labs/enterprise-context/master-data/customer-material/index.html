SAP Sales / Master Data / MD.SD.CMIR

Customer Material

A customer-material information record stores the Sales relationship between one customer and one product. It is useful when that customer uses a different product number, description, or customer-specific logistics data. Think of it as the exception layer between the generic customer and generic product masters.

Follow the runtime path arrow_downward

Object profile

01MD.SD.CMIRCatalogue ID
02relationship recordData class
Customer, sales organization, distribution channel and product/material.

Mental model

The object owns the customer–product relationship, not the product itself.

database

Scope: Customer, sales organization, distribution channel and product/material.

Important data

Customer material number, customer description, delivery-related defaults, tolerances, texts and customer-specific logistics values where supported.

Configuration boundary

A customer-material record does not replace the product master. It specializes the customer-product relationship and works together with the generic product and customer records.

Runtime use

One relationship can change the same product for one customer.

During order entry SAP can use the customer-material relationship to translate the customer's product reference and supply supported customer-specific values. This makes the record especially important when the same internal product behaves correctly for most customers but differently for one customer and sales-area context.

01

Match the relationship

Confirm the customer, sales organization, distribution channel, and product used by the order. A record in another sales-area context is not evidence for this document.

02

Read the customer-specific value

Check the customer material number, description, and the logistics or text fields that are actually maintained for this relationship.

03

Compare the fallback

Compare the result with the relevant customer and product defaults. For example, standard delivering-plant proposal can consider a customer-material record before ship-to and product master data.

04

Prove the document result

Check what was stored in the sales order and what later documents consume. Do not change shared product data to repair a customer-specific exception until the source is proven.

Troubleshooting

If only one customer is different, start with the relationship.

troubleshoot

If the issue appears for one customer/product combination, check this record early. For a wrong plant, compare the customer-material value with the ship-to and product defaults. For a wrong customer product number or description, prove the exact relationship key and what the order stored. The useful question is not “is the product master correct?” but “which source won for this customer-product pair?”

Assessment recall

Explain the exception layer in one minute.

record_voice_over

60-second answer: Customer Material is relationship master data between a customer and a product in a Sales context. I use it for customer-specific product numbers, descriptions, and supported logistics defaults. If one customer sees different behavior for the same material, I check this layer before changing the generic product master. I prove the exact customer, sales organization, distribution channel, and product key, compare the relationship value with the fallback customer and product data, and then verify which value the sales order actually stored.
