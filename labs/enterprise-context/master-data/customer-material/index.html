SAP Sales / Master Data / MD.SD.CMIR

Customer Material

A Customer-Material Info Record (CMIR) stores the relationship between one customer and one internal product in a Sales context. It lets SAP understand the customer's product number and can also hold customer-specific logistics values. The product remains one internal SAP product; the relationship adds the customer-specific view.

Follow the runtime path arrow_downward

Object profile

01MD.SD.CMIRCatalogue ID
02relationship master dataData class
Customer + sales organization + distribution channel + internal product/material.

Mental model

One internal product can have a different commercial identity for each customer.

The CMIR does not create a new product. It translates the customer-product relationship into values that Sales can use during order processing.

Internal identity

SAP uses the internal product or material number for stock, ATP, MRP, delivery, goods issue and most internal processing.

Customer identity

The customer can call the same product by a different SKU and description. CMIR keeps that mapping close to the Sales process.

Relationship rules

Supported logistics values such as plant, delivery priority, sales unit, tolerances, partial-delivery settings and texts can specialize this customer-product pair.

hub

Lead rule: use CMIR when the rule belongs to the customer × product relationship. Do not duplicate the product master only because a customer uses another SKU.

Maintenance

Current S/4HANA uses the Manage Customer Materials app; classic GUI transactions still matter in some landscapes.

The exact maintenance UI depends on the S/4HANA deployment and release. Keep the business object separate from the transaction used to maintain it.

Tool Use Boundary
F2499 Manage Customer Materials Create, copy, change, search and delete Customer Material records; manage drafts and important change history. Current S/4HANA Fiori app. It also supports advanced functions such as additional customer materials, classification and address-dependent shipping when the prerequisites exist.
VD51 Create customer-material information. Classic GUI / on-premise-oriented knowledge. Check availability in the target deployment.
VD52 Change customer-material information. Useful in classic and migration validation scenarios where the transaction is available.
VD53 Display customer-material information. Useful for read-only troubleshooting in classic environments.

Do not project classic GUI transactions into SAP S/4HANA Cloud Public Edition without checking the current app and scope. For assessment answers, state the concept first and the maintenance UI second.

Key and fields

The key is narrow; the consequences can reach several Sales decisions.

The standard API entity uses customer, sales organization, distribution channel and material as key fields. The record then carries the customer-specific values for that relationship.

Field or group What it means Why a Lead cares
Customer The customer to which the relationship applies. A correct material mapping for another customer proves nothing for this order.
Sales Organization + Distribution Channel The Sales context in which the record is valid. A frequent cause of “CMIR exists but SAP does not use it” is a different sales-area context.
Internal Material / Product Your company's product identity. This remains the object used by internal logistics.
Customer Material The product number used by the customer. Important for manual order entry, EDI and external order capture.
Customer Material Description A customer-specific product description. Can make customer-facing documents easier to reconcile with the customer's own catalogue.
Plant A customer-product specific delivering plant. It has a higher priority than the ship-to and material-master plant during standard plant proposal.
Delivery Priority A priority value for this customer-product combination. Do not place a customer-specific rule in the general customer master if it applies to one product only.
Minimum Delivery Quantity Minimum quantity to be delivered for this relationship. Can explain why an order behaves differently from the same material for another customer.
Partial Delivery / Maximum Partial Deliveries Controls whether and how the order quantity can be split into partial deliveries. Separate relationship-level agreements from generic customer settings.
Under- / Overdelivery Tolerance Accepted quantity deviation around the order quantity. Important for delivery completion and quantity-control discussions.
Sales Unit and Conversion Customer-specific sales unit and conversion factors where supported. Useful when the customer orders the same product in its own commercial packaging unit.
Item Usage An indicator that can participate in item-category determination. It can change sales-item behavior for one relationship without creating another material.
Rounding Profile Rules used to round order quantities to deliverable units. Check the complete UoM and product setup before using CMIR to compensate for a weak base design.

Runtime path

Translate the customer's SKU first, then prove which customer-specific values reached the order.

Example: the customer sends SKU AX-4711, while SAP internally uses material FG-100045.

01

Resolve the Sales context

SAP knows the relevant customer, sales organization and distribution channel for the document.

02

Receive the customer product reference

The user, EDI message, API or external order channel provides the customer's product number.

03

Resolve the relationship

The customer-material relationship connects AX-4711 with internal product FG-100045 in the valid Sales context.

04

Use the internal product

ATP, inventory, MRP, delivery and accounting-relevant logistics continue with the internal SAP product.

05

Copy supported relationship values

The sales item can receive customer-specific description, plant, sales unit, tolerances, partial-delivery rules, delivery priority, item usage or texts according to the maintained data and scenario.

06

Prove the document result

Check what the sales order actually stored. The master record is a possible source; the document result is the evidence for downstream processing.

fact_check

Diagnostic rule: when the order is wrong, do not stop at “the CMIR is correct”. Prove the exact key, then inspect the values stored in the order item and the later document flow.

Plant determination

CMIR is the most specific standard plant source in the normal Sales sequence.

For standard sales-item plant proposal, SAP documents the priority in this order:

01

Customer Material

Use the plant maintained for this customer-product relationship.

02

Ship-to Party

If CMIR does not provide a plant, use the delivering plant from the ship-to customer master data.

03

Material Master

If neither relationship nor ship-to data provides a plant, use the delivering plant from the material master.

04

Manual or extended logic

If the standard proposal does not satisfy the requirement, evaluate manual entry, address-dependent data, sourcing functionality or a justified extension.

This priority explains a common incident: “the material master plant is correct, but the order gets another plant.” The more specific customer-material relationship can intentionally win.

Customer-specific units

Use CMIR for a customer-specific commercial unit, not for a broken base-UoM design.

A customer may use a different commercial unit name or conversion for the same physical product. S/4HANA Customer Material can hold sales-unit and conversion information where the scenario supports it.

Example

SAP manages the product in PC. One customer orders CASE, where one case contains 20 pieces.

Good design

The base and alternative units remain coherent in the product master. CMIR adds the customer-specific commercial view.

Weak design

Using customer-specific conversion to hide inconsistent product units across Sales, warehouse and purchasing.

Classification

Customer Material can carry default configuration values for configurable products.

SAP supports class type 052 for Customer-Material Info Record classification. This lets a customer-product relationship hold characteristic values that can be passed to sales-document configuration when the prerequisites and Customizing are in place.

01

Use a configurable product

The material must support the relevant configuration process.

02

Use class type 052

Maintain a class and characteristics that are permitted for Customer-Material Info Record classification.

03

Maintain the relationship values

Example: one customer always buys the pump with Voltage = 230V and Housing = Stainless Steel.

04

Adopt values into the sales item

Where configured, the sales document can reuse these characteristic values instead of asking the user to enter the same customer defaults every time.

tune

Boundary: CMIR classification provides customer-specific defaults or values. The configurable product model still owns which characteristics and combinations are valid.

Modern S/4HANA capabilities

Do not repeat old ECC limitations as universal S/4HANA rules.

The F2499 app contains functions that are easy to miss if the design is based only on the classic KNMT mental model.

Additional customer materials

F2499 can assign several customer-material identifiers to one product. This helps with customer renumbering, mergers, legacy SKUs, e-commerce identifiers and parallel external systems.

Address-dependent shipping

If multiple address handling for Business Partners is active, the app can link customer addresses to specific delivering plants for each customer material.

Draft and change support

The app supports draft records and exposes important changes, which improves controlled maintenance compared with treating CMIR as a small hidden table.

warning

Assessment trap: the classic key of table KNMT is useful technical knowledge, but it is not enough to conclude that modern S/4HANA cannot support several customer identifiers or address-dependent behavior.

Boundaries

CMIR, pricing, substitution and sellability solve different problems.

A strong design starts by naming the problem correctly. Similar-looking functions should not be used as substitutes for each other.

Function Question it answers Example
Customer Material What does this customer call our product, and what relationship-specific Sales values apply? Customer SKU AX-4711 → SAP Material FG-100045
Pricing Condition Record What price, discount, surcharge, freight or tax condition applies? Customer + material determines a negotiated price.
Material Determination Should the material entered in the sales document be replaced or substituted by another material? Discontinued material is replaced by its successor.
Listing / Exclusion May this customer order the product at all? A regulated product is excluded for a customer or market.
Product Master What is the product itself? Base UoM, product group, plant-level and Sales-relevant product settings.
Customer / Business Partner What applies to this customer generally? Partner roles, shipping data, payment and Sales-area customer settings.
compare_arrows

Memory model: CMIR translates the customer's language into your product context. Material Determination can change the product. Pricing calculates the commercial amount. Listing and Exclusion control sellability.

EDI and integration

CMIR removes customer-specific product translation from every interface.

In B2B integration, the external order often contains the customer's SKU, not your internal material number. A governed Customer Material relationship lets the Sales process own that translation instead of hard-coding mappings in each interface.

01

External order arrives

Customer, customer SKU, quantity and unit come from EDI, API, portal or another order-capture channel.

02

Resolve the customer product

The relationship converts the external customer identifier into the internal SAP product context.

03

Create the sales item

The internal material drives standard Sales and logistics processing.

04

Return the customer's identity where required

Confirmations, invoices or other outputs can expose the customer reference when the document and form mapping are designed to do so.

integration_instructions

Architecture rule: before building a custom cross-reference table in middleware, check whether Customer Material already owns the business mapping. Keep the mapping close to the business object that consumes it.

API and data access

Use released interfaces before designing new direct-table dependencies.

S/4HANA exposes Customer Material through standard API and CDS surfaces. Direct table access can still be useful for diagnosis in permitted environments, but it should not be the default integration design.

Surface Technical name Use
OData API API_CUSTOMER_MATERIAL_SRV Create, read, update and delete Customer Material data from external applications where the API is available.
API entity A_CustomerMaterial Exposes key and business properties such as customer material, description, plant, item usage, delivery priority, UoM and tolerances.
CDS view I_CustomerMaterial_2 Current Customer Material CDS view for analytical or extension scenarios supported by the target deployment.
Deprecated CDS I_CustomerMaterial Do not start new development on a deprecated view when the successor is available.
Classic table KNMT Important for technical diagnosis and legacy understanding; do not make it the universal integration contract.

Migration

Migration scope depends on the migration approach; validate business behavior, not only record counts.

For direct transfer from ERP to SAP S/4HANA, SAP documents the migration object S4_SD_CUSTOMER_MATERIAL. Its scope includes basic data, long texts and classification data, while address-dependent data is documented as out of scope for that direct-transfer object.

01

Migrate prerequisites first

Customer, product, class and characteristic dependencies must be available before relationship data can be used consistently.

02

Select the migration approach

Direct transfer, staging or another project method can expose different object structures and scope. Do not treat one technical alias as universal across releases.

03

Load the relationship

Move the required basic data, texts and classification according to the selected object and target release.

04

Validate in the target system

Confirm the customer, product and Sales-area keys and inspect the migrated relationship in the available app or transaction.

05

Prove Sales behavior

Create a test sales order using the customer SKU. Check material resolution, plant, UoM, tolerances, texts and configuration values that are in project scope.

rule

Lead acceptance criterion: “10,000 CMIR rows loaded” is not enough. The migrated data is accepted when the business mapping and dependent Sales behavior work in representative orders.

Diagnostics

Start from the symptom and identify the first wrong layer.

CMIR incidents are often solved quickly when the investigation starts with the exact relationship key and the sales-order result instead of changing generic master data.

Symptom First checks Next boundary
Customer SKU does not resolve Customer, sales organization, distribution channel, internal product, customer-material identifier and record validity. Inbound mapping / material determination / custom logic only after the relationship key is proven.
Wrong plant in the sales order CMIR plant first, then ship-to plant, then material-master plant. Check address-dependent shipping if used. Sourcing or extension logic if the standard sequence is not the requirement.
Wrong customer description Customer Material description and what the sales item stored. Output form or interface mapping if the document item is already correct.
Wrong partial-delivery behavior Relationship-level partial-delivery indicator, maximum partial deliveries and document item values. Customer master, document logic or delivery processing only after the item data is proven.
Wrong under- / overdelivery behavior CMIR tolerances and the resulting order/delivery item values. Delivery and completion logic.
Wrong unit conversion Product base/alternative UoM, CMIR sales unit and conversion factors. Interface unit mapping or custom conversion after master data is consistent.
Wrong configurable-product defaults CMIR class type 052 values and adoption settings, then the sales-item configuration instance. Variant Configuration model if the relationship values were transferred correctly.
CMIR exists but order ignores it Exact Sales-area key, product/customer pair, document result and target-release behavior. In classic implementations also verify relevant sales-document settings before assuming an ABAP defect. Enhancement or custom processing only after standard determination is excluded.

Lead decisions

Use the narrowest correct owner for the rule.

CMIR is useful because it limits the blast radius of a customer-specific rule. That benefit disappears when the object becomes a dumping ground for unstable exceptions.

Use CMIR

The value is stable for the same customer-product relationship and has a clear business owner.

Use customer or product master

The value is generic for the customer or product and should apply across many relationships.

Use runtime logic

The value changes by order, date, stock, route, plant load or another runtime condition that master data should not freeze.

Use an extension

The requirement truly depends on a dimension not represented by the standard object and standard S/4HANA capabilities cannot model it safely.

psychology

Lead question: “Should this value still be true for the next order from the same customer for the same product?” If not, master data may be the wrong owner.

Assessment traps

Five shortcuts create weak CMIR answers.

“CMIR is only customer SKU mapping”

The SKU mapping is central, but the object can also carry customer-product specific logistics values, UoM information, classification and modern Fiori extensions.

“The material master plant always wins”

Standard plant proposal gives Customer Material higher priority than ship-to and material-master sources.

“One internal material can have only one customer identifier”

Modern F2499 supports additional customer materials, so do not generalize an old technical limitation into a current product rule.

“CMIR contains the sales price”

Pricing belongs to condition technique. Relationship master data and commercial price are different responsibilities.

“If CMIR is correct, the incident is solved”

The sales order is the runtime evidence. Always check which value reached the item and which later process consumed it.

Assessment recall

Customer language → internal product → relationship rules → document evidence.

record_voice_over

60-second answer: A Customer-Material Info Record is Sales master data for the relationship between a customer and an internal product. I mainly use it when the customer has its own SKU or description, but it can also hold relationship-specific values such as plant, delivery priority, sales unit, partial-delivery settings, tolerances, item usage and texts. In standard plant proposal, CMIR is checked before the ship-to customer and the material master. In modern S/4HANA, F2499 also supports additional customer materials, classification and address-dependent shipping. For integration I prefer the released Customer Material API and current CDS view instead of building new direct-table dependencies. For diagnosis I prove the exact customer and Sales-area key, then check what the sales order actually stored before changing shared master data or code.
