Customer Material

Customer-material information records describe the relationship between one customer and one product. They are useful when the customer uses its own material number, description or logistics expectations. This is more specific than either the generic customer or the generic product master.

Customer, sales organization, distribution channel and product/material.

Mental model

Know what the object owns before tracing a document.

database

Scope: Customer, sales organization, distribution channel and product/material.

Important data

Customer material number, customer description, delivery-related defaults, tolerances, texts and customer-specific logistics values where supported.

Configuration boundary

A customer-material record does not replace the product master. It specializes the customer-product relationship and works together with the generic product and customer records.

Runtime use

How the data enters Order-to-Cash.

During order entry SAP can translate the customer's product reference to the internal product and propose customer-specific data. Because this record is more specific than generic product data, it is often the right place to look when only one customer sees different behavior for the same material.

Troubleshooting

Start with the most specific evidence.

troubleshoot

If the issue appears for one customer/product combination, check the customer-material record early. Compare its values with the generic product and ship-to customer defaults, especially for plant, delivery and text behavior.

Assessment recall

A concise answer that still shows depth.

record_voice_over

Customer Material is a relationship master. I use it when behavior is specific to a customer-product pair, especially customer product numbers, descriptions and logistics defaults.
