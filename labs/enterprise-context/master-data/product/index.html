SAP Sales / Master Data / MD.SD.PRODUCT

Product / Material

The product master is where commercial and logistics behavior meet. A material number alone says very little. Sales reads sales-area data, plant data, units, status and classification-related attributes. These values feed several determinations, so one incorrect field can surface as an order, ATP, shipping, pricing or billing problem.

Object profile

01MD.SD.PRODUCTCatalogue ID
02business masterData class
Product, sales organization, distribution channel, plant and unit-of-measure views.

Mental model

“Material master” is too broad; name the view and the field.

database

Scope: Product, sales organization, distribution channel, plant and unit-of-measure views.

Important data

Item category group, delivering plant, sales unit, product hierarchy, product pricing group, account assignment product group, sales status, loading group, availability-checking group, transportation data, tax classification and alternative units of measure.

Configuration boundary

The product master supplies attributes. Configuration interprets them. For example, item category group is master data, while the item category determination table is configuration.

Lead decision

Do not turn a customer exception into a generic product truth.

Product data has a wide blast radius because several processes can consume the same product and plant views. A local Sales requirement should change the shared product only when it is genuinely true for the wider business.

Shared product behavior

Use the product or plant view when the attribute is intrinsic to how the product should behave for the relevant organizational scope.

Relationship-specific behavior

If one customer needs a different commercial or ordering rule, first check whether customer-product data or another relationship layer owns the exception.

Trade-off

Central defaults are easier to govern, but a broad change can affect pricing, ATP, shipping, planning, or accounting far beyond the original ticket.

Lead check: state the expected blast radius before changing a product field.

Runtime use

One product supplies inputs to several different Sales engines.

Commercial fields can influence item behavior, pricing keys, hierarchy, tax, or account-determination context. Plant and logistics fields can influence availability, loading, shipping, and scheduling. The product master therefore does not “decide” the final result by itself; it supplies inputs that several determination engines interpret.

01

Name the visible symptom

Wrong item category, plant, shipping point, ATP result, price key, tax classification, or account-assignment input are different problems.

02

Find the source field

Identify the exact product field that can feed that mechanism instead of reviewing every material view.

03

Check the owning view

Use the correct sales organization/distribution channel, plant, unit, or other organizational level for the document item.

04

Trace the determination

Follow the configuration that interprets the master-data value. A correct field can still produce an unexpected result if the consuming rule is different.

05

Check the blast radius

Before changing shared product data, identify every process that consumes the field. A plant or logistics change can affect more than the Sales document you started with.

Troubleshooting

Trace one wrong result back to one product field.

troubleshoot

Start from the first wrong derived value in the item, then identify the product field that can feed it. Verify that field in the exact sales-area or plant view used by the order and trace the consuming determination. This is faster and safer than scanning the whole product master or changing a shared field because one document looks wrong.

Assessment recall

Name the view, field, consumer, and blast radius.

record_voice_over

60-second answer: For a product issue I never stop at “material master.” I name the exact field and organizational view, then the determination that consumes it. Sales-area data supports commercial behavior; plant and logistics data support availability and execution; units and classification can affect several processes. I compare the master value with the first wrong document result and check the blast radius before changing shared product data.
