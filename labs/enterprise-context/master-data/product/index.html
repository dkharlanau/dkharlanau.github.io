SAP Sales / Master Data / MD.SD.PRODUCT

Product / Material

The product master is where commercial and logistics behavior meet. A material number alone says very little. Sales reads sales-area data, plant data, units, status and classification-related attributes. These values feed several determinations, so one incorrect field can surface as an order, ATP, shipping, pricing or billing problem.

Object profile

01MD.SD.PRODUCTCatalogue ID
02business masterData class
Product, sales organization, distribution channel, plant and unit-of-measure views.

Mental model

“Material master” is too broad; name the view and the field.

database

Scope: Product, sales organization, distribution channel, plant and unit-of-measure views.

Important data

Item category group, delivering plant, sales unit, product hierarchy, product pricing group, account assignment product group, sales status, loading group, availability-checking group, transportation data, tax classification and alternative units of measure.

Configuration boundary

The product master supplies attributes. Configuration interprets them. For example, item category group is master data, while the item category determination table is configuration.

Lead decision

Do not turn a customer exception into a generic product truth.

Product data has a wide blast radius because several processes can consume the same product and plant views. A local Sales requirement should change the shared product only when it is genuinely true for the wider business.

Shared product behavior

Use the product or plant view when the attribute is intrinsic to how the product should behave for the relevant organizational scope.

Relationship-specific behavior

If one customer needs a different commercial or ordering rule, first check whether customer-product data or another relationship layer owns the exception.

Trade-off

Central defaults are easier to govern, but a broad change can affect pricing, ATP, shipping, planning, or accounting far beyond the original ticket.

Lead check: state the expected blast radius before changing a product field.

GTIN / EAN / UPC

A barcode number identifies a product in a specific unit of measure.

SAP still uses the historical terms EAN and UPC in many fields and configuration activities. In GS1 terminology, the broader term is GTIN. Common formats are GTIN-8, GTIN-12 (UPC), GTIN-13 (EAN) and GTIN-14 for higher packaging levels. The final digit is a check digit. Do not design the solution around the assumption that every code has 13 digits.

qr_code_scanner

Core rule: the GTIN belongs to a product and unit of measure. One piece, one box of 10 pieces and one pallet are different trade items and normally require different GTINs. The UoM conversion therefore matters as much as the barcode itself.

ConceptWhat it meansWhy it matters in SAP
GTINGlobal Trade Item Number. The current umbrella term for globally standardized trade-item identifiers.Used to identify the product and packaging level across sales, procurement, warehouse, retail and interfaces.
EANCommon name for GTIN-13 and a historical SAP term.Still appears in field labels, table fields and Customizing names.
UPCCommon name for GTIN-12, widely used in North America.SAP can validate it through the assigned EAN/UPC category.
EAN categoryDefines rules such as number assignment, length, check-digit logic and allowed format.The category tells SAP how the identifier must be created and validated.
Main EAN / GTINThe primary identifier for one unit of measure.Only one identifier is the main one for that UoM. It is the value normally exposed in the UoM record.
Additional EANs / GTINsAlternative identifiers for the same product and UoM.Useful when the same trade item can be scanned with more than one valid code, for example after packaging or vendor changes.

Maintenance and data model

Maintain the identifier at the same packaging level that it represents.

Base unit

The identifier shown in Basic Data belongs to the base unit of measure. For packaging details and other UoMs, use Additional Data → Units of Measure.

Alternative units

For each alternative UoM, maintain the conversion to the base UoM first, then assign the correct GTIN. Example: 1 CAR = 12 PC. The carton GTIN must describe the carton, not one piece.

Multiple identifiers

If one UoM needs several valid identifiers, use Additional EANs and define which one is the main GTIN for that UoM.

table_view

Technical recall: the main UoM-level EAN/UPC is associated with the material UoM data in MARM. Additional identifiers are held in MEAN. For troubleshooting, always compare the material number, UoM, GTIN and main-GTIN indicator together instead of checking the barcode alone.

Configuration

EAN category controls how SAP accepts or generates the number.

Configuration sits under Material Master settings for International Article Numbers. The exact menu wording differs between S/4HANA editions, but the design questions are stable.

01

Decide the ownership model

For externally governed GS1 numbers, users or interfaces supply the GTIN. For internal company numbering, SAP can generate numbers from an internal range where the category allows it.

02

Define the category

Configure the expected length and format, internal or external assignment, check-digit algorithm and other category rules.

03

Maintain number ranges where needed

Internal assignment requires an appropriate number range. External assignment validates the entered number instead of inventing a new external identity.

04

Maintain product and UoM data

Create the UoM conversion and packaging dimensions, then maintain the main and any additional GTINs.

05

Test the consumers

Test scanning, interfaces, sales, procurement and warehouse processes that read the identifier. Master-data save success does not prove that every downstream system receives the correct GTIN and UoM.

Runtime behavior

The GTIN resolves identity; normal logistics logic continues after that.

A scanner or interface can provide a GTIN instead of an internal material number. The application resolves the product and relevant UoM, then the normal process continues: quantity conversion, item processing, ATP, delivery, warehouse execution, billing or procurement rules are applied according to the document and organizational context.

Sales

GTIN can be used to identify the ordered product or packaging level. It does not replace item category, pricing, ATP or plant determination; it gives those processes the correct product and UoM context.

Warehouse / EWM

RF and barcode-driven steps can use main or additional GTINs to validate the product during goods receipt, picking, packing and physical inventory.

Procurement / Retail

Supplier and retail scenarios may need several product identifiers. Keep the distinction between the generic product GTIN and supplier-specific or customer-specific relationship data.

Integration

Most GTIN incidents are mapping incidents, not barcode incidents.

S/4HANA APIs expose product UoM identifiers together with the alternative UoM, GTIN category and main-GTIN indicator. Material-master IDoc integrations can also transfer UoM-level and additional identifiers. The integration contract must preserve both the identifier and the UoM context.

hub

Interface rule: never map only GTIN → material when packaging level matters. Map GTIN → material + UoM, preserve leading zeroes where the target format requires them, and decide explicitly whether additional GTINs are in scope.

Common problems are easy to miss because the barcode itself can look valid.

Assessment recall

Explain GTIN as identity + packaging + integration.

record_voice_over

60-second answer: In S/4HANA, EAN/UPC is the historical SAP name for a trade-item identifier; today I normally speak about GTIN. The important point is that the identifier belongs to a product and a unit of measure. Each packaging level can have its own GTIN, and one UoM can also have additional GTINs with one main identifier. The EAN category controls validation and number assignment. In an incident I check GTIN, UoM conversion, main versus additional identifier and the interface mapping. I do not treat a customer-specific product code as a generic GTIN, because that would turn a relationship-specific exception into shared product master data.

Lead phrase: GTIN identifies the trade item; it does not replace the SD or logistics determination logic that runs after product identification.

Runtime use

One product supplies inputs to several different Sales engines.

Commercial fields can influence item behavior, pricing keys, hierarchy, tax, or account-determination context. Plant and logistics fields can influence availability, loading, shipping, and scheduling. The product master therefore does not “decide” the final result by itself; it supplies inputs that several determination engines interpret.

01

Name the visible symptom

Wrong item category, plant, shipping point, ATP result, price key, tax classification, or account-assignment input are different problems.

02

Find the source field

Identify the exact product field that can feed that mechanism instead of reviewing every material view.

03

Check the owning view

Use the correct sales organization/distribution channel, plant, unit, or other organizational level for the document item.

04

Trace the determination

Follow the configuration that interprets the master-data value. A correct field can still produce an unexpected result if the consuming rule is different.

05

Check the blast radius

Before changing shared product data, identify every process that consumes the field. A plant or logistics change can affect more than the Sales document you started with.

Troubleshooting

Trace one wrong result back to one product field.

troubleshoot

Start from the first wrong derived value in the item, then identify the product field that can feed it. Verify that field in the exact sales-area or plant view used by the order and trace the consuming determination. This is faster and safer than scanning the whole product master or changing a shared field because one document looks wrong.

Assessment recall

Name the view, field, consumer, and blast radius.

record_voice_over

60-second answer: For a product issue I never stop at “material master.” I name the exact field and organizational view, then the determination that consumes it. Sales-area data supports commercial behavior; plant and logistics data support availability and execution; units and classification can affect several processes. I compare the master value with the first wrong document result and check the blast radius before changing shared product data.
