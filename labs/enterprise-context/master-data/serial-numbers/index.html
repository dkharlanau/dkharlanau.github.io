SAP Logistics / Traceability

Serial numbers: identity below the material level.

Material identifies the product. Batch identifies a lot. Serial number identifies one exact physical unit. This distinction matters when the business needs unit-level traceability, service history, warranty, or controlled goods movements.

Start with the mental model arrow_downward
rule

Boundary: The material master controls serial-number relevance through the serial-number profile. The serial master identifies the unit. A delivery, goods movement, or service document proves where that unit participated in a transaction.

Mental model

Do not confuse product, batch, serial number, and equipment.

These objects answer different questions. A Lead should know which identity owns which part of the lifecycle.

Material / Product

What type of thing is this, and which serial-number procedures apply?

Serial Number

Batch

Which production or procurement lot did it come from, when batch management is also used?

Equipment

Which technical object has maintenance or service lifecycle data, when equipment is created or linked?

Runtime use

Trace the exact unit through the document flow.

The creation and entry point varies by process. Follow the material profile and the actual document where the serial was assigned.

01

Profile

Check the material serial-number profile and the procedure relevant to the process.

02

Document

Identify the goods movement, delivery, receipt, or service step that requires the serial.

03

Assignment

Check which serial number was entered or generated for the material.

04

Status

Verify that the serial status allows the intended movement or follow-on step.

05

Trace

Follow the serial across documents instead of stopping at material or batch level.

Lead diagnosis

Find the owning layer before changing data.

Most serial-number incidents are not solved by changing the serial master first. The defect can be product setup, document assignment, status, stock context, or equipment integration.

Start here

Check material → serial-number profile → process procedure → document assignment → status/location → follow-on document.

Do not jump here

Do not treat a serial number as another batch field. Batch and serial management can coexist, but they provide different levels of identity.

record_voice_over

Assessment answer: “Serial numbers provide individual-unit traceability. The material serial-number profile controls where they are used, while the concrete document records the serial assignment. I separate serial identity from batch identity and equipment lifecycle.”
