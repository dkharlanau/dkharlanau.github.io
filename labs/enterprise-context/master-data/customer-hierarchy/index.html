SAP Sales / Master Data / MD.SD.CUSTHIER

Customer Hierarchy

A customer hierarchy represents commercial relationships above the individual sold-to customer. It can model groups, headquarters and subsidiaries so that pricing or reporting can work with a parent structure without changing the legal customer identity used in the order.

Configuration boundary

The hierarchy is reusable master data. Whether pricing searches it is determined by pricing condition technique and the fields available in the relevant access sequence.

Runtime use

How the data enters Order-to-Cash.

Pricing can use hierarchy nodes as access fields when the hierarchy is active and relevant. The same structure can support reporting and account management, but its commercial effect depends on configuration and validity.

Troubleshooting

Start with the most specific evidence.

troubleshoot

Confirm the customer is assigned to the expected hierarchy node for the document date and sales area. Then check whether the pricing access actually includes the hierarchy field. A correct hierarchy with no relevant access has no pricing effect.

Assessment recall

A concise answer that still shows depth.

record_voice_over

Customer Hierarchy groups customers above the individual sold-to. I check hierarchy type, validity and sales area first, then whether pricing or analytics actually consumes the hierarchy.
