Customer Hierarchy

A sold-to party can be one legal customer and still belong to a much larger commercial structure. Customer hierarchy adds that structure: head office, regional group, subsidiary, store or another parent-child model. Sales can then use the hierarchy for pricing and analytics without replacing the customer identity in the order.

Read KNVH as a relationship arrow_downward

Object profile

01MD.SD.CUSTHIERCatalogue ID
02relationship recordData class
03KNVHClassic technical anchor
Hierarchy type + sales area + validity + child → parent relationship.

Mental model

Think of a customer hierarchy as a time-valid edge, not as a customer attribute.

The useful mental model is child customer → parent customer, valid for one hierarchy type, one sales-area context and one period. The customer master tells us who the customer is. The hierarchy tells us where that customer sits in a commercial tree.

Identity stays stable

The sold-to, ship-to, bill-to and payer remain normal customer roles. A hierarchy node does not replace those document partners.

The relationship is contextual

The same customer can have different hierarchy meaning by hierarchy type, sales area and validity period.

A consumer is required

A correct hierarchy has no automatic price effect. Pricing, analytics or another process must explicitly consume the hierarchy.

account_tree

Memory rule: hierarchy answers “where does this customer belong?”, while partner determination answers “which party performs which role in this document?”. These are related concepts, but they are not the same mechanism.

Classic technical model

KNVH stores customer hierarchy assignments in classic S/4HANA and ERP-oriented models.

For private-cloud and on-premise diagnostics, KNVH is the central technical table to inspect. Do not treat it as a universal integration contract: current SAP S/4HANA Cloud uses a newer hierarchy framework and released applications/interfaces should be preferred there.

database

Read one KNVH row as a relationship: customer KUNNR, in hierarchy type HITYP and sales area VKORG/VTWEG/SPART, is assigned from DATAB to a higher-level customer HKUNNR until DATBI.

Primary key: why these fields matter

Field Meaning Diagnostic question
MANDT Client Are we checking the correct SAP client?
HITYP Customer hierarchy type Are we looking at the hierarchy used by this sales process?
KUNNR Customer / lower-level node Is this the sold-to or hierarchy node we are actually tracing?
VKORG Sales organization Does it match the document sales area?
VTWEG Distribution channel Could a reference/common channel change the effective context?
SPART Division Does the hierarchy assignment exist for the effective division?
DATAB Valid-from date Which version of the relationship was active for the document date?

Why is DATAB part of the key? Because hierarchy membership is time-dependent. The same customer can move to another parent later without deleting the historical relationship. That is why “the hierarchy looks correct today” is not enough when diagnosing an old order.

Fields that explain the relationship

Field What it tells us What to watch
DATBI Valid-to date The row may exist but be outside the sales-document date.
HKUNNR Higher-level customer This is the parent pointer that lets us walk upward through the tree.
HVKORG / HVTWEG / HSPART Higher-level sales area Do not assume the parent relationship is sales-area neutral.
PRFRE Relevant for price determination The relationship can exist while the node is not relevant for pricing.
BOKRE Rebate-relevant indicator in the classic model Useful in classic rebate/settlement-related scenarios; validate the target solution and release.
HZUOR Assignment to hierarchy Do not read it as a live calculated “current depth” without checking the SAP behavior.
GRPNO Copy routine number Technical control detail; usually not the first field to investigate in a pricing incident.

Two easy traps

Do not turn copied indicators into the hierarchy model.

HZUOR is not “the node level”

SAP KBA 2882156 exists specifically because KNVH-HZUOR may not update to show the current hierarchy level after maintenance. Treat the parent links and validity as stronger evidence of the actual tree. Do not assume KNA1-HZUOR and KNVH-HZUOR are two perfectly synchronized depth counters.

Pricing relevance starts outside KNVH

In classic hierarchy processing, pricing relevance in KNVH-PRFRE is connected to the customer sales-area setting KNVV-PRFRE. If the hierarchy exists but pricing relevance is missing, check the customer sales data and hierarchy maintenance before changing the pricing procedure.

warning

Lead habit: first prove the structural relationship, then prove relevance, then prove the consuming rule. A single flag is not enough evidence for the whole chain.

Runtime use

A hierarchy becomes business behavior only when Sales resolves it and a consumer uses it.

Current SAP documentation describes hierarchy determination from the sold-to party, sales area and document date. That is the right diagnostic order: document context first, hierarchy second, pricing or analytics third.

01

Start from the document

Capture sold-to, sales organization, distribution channel, division and the relevant document date. Do not start from a parent node in isolation.

02

Resolve the valid chain

Find the active hierarchy type and follow the child-to-parent assignments that were valid at that time.

03

Check relevance

For pricing, prove that the node is pricing-relevant. For another consumer, identify its own relevance rules instead of assuming pricing logic applies everywhere.

04

Prove the consumer

Inspect the pricing access, analytical hierarchy or downstream logic that actually reads the hierarchy. A valid tree alone changes nothing.

05

Verify the document result

Check the hierarchy data carried into the sales document and the condition result that was actually determined.

Pricing boundary

Classic KNVH knowledge and the newer Cloud hierarchy framework are related, but not identical.

Private cloud / on-premise orientation

KNVH, classic hierarchy maintenance and traditional customer hierarchy partner/pricing concepts remain important diagnostic references. Use SE16H/SE16 for data inspection and SE11 for DDIC structure where those tools are available.

Current Public Cloud orientation

SAP documents Manage Customer Hierarchies and dedicated pricing fields such as CUST_HIER_ROOT_NODE, CUST_HIER_NODE_VALUE and CUST_HIER_NODE_TYPE. Released apps, APIs and CDS views are the safer contract; do not design a cloud integration around direct KNVH access.

payments

Pricing question: which hierarchy field does the active condition access read, in which direction, and how many hierarchy levels may it evaluate? The answer is configuration-dependent.

Beyond pricing

Hierarchy can also affect analytics and billing convergence.

Hierarchy is not only a discount mechanism. SAP also supports analytical roll-ups by hierarchy. In current Sales behavior, hierarchy information can be copied into billing context, and differing hierarchy assignments or versions can become relevant to billing-document split behavior.

Analytics

A report can aggregate sales below a parent node. The reporting question is therefore not “what is the sold-to?” but “which hierarchy and level should own the roll-up?”.

Billing

If otherwise similar sales documents carry different hierarchy assignments or versions, do not investigate billing split only through payer, terms or billing type. Hierarchy context can be part of the split evidence.

Technical map

Know which table answers which question.

Object Question it answers Do not confuse it with
KNA1 Who is the customer at general-data level? The hierarchy relationship itself.
KNVV How does the customer behave in this sales area? The child-to-parent hierarchy edge.
KNVH Which customer/node belongs under which parent, for which hierarchy type, sales area and validity? Normal partner-function assignments.
KNVP Which partner functions are maintained for customer sales data? Hierarchy storage.
VBAK / VBPA What customer and partner context was stored in the actual sales document? Current master data after the document was created.

Troubleshooting

When a hierarchy discount is missing, diagnose the chain in this order.

01

Freeze the document context

Record sold-to, sales area and document date from the failing order.

02

Prove the hierarchy row

Check the correct hierarchy type and a valid KNVH assignment or the released hierarchy app for that deployment.

03

Walk the parent chain

Follow KUNNR → HKUNNR until the expected commercial parent is reached. Check validity at every step.

04

Check pricing relevance

Verify the relevant node and customer sales-area settings. A visible hierarchy node can still be irrelevant for price determination.

05

Trace the pricing access

Prove that the active condition technique actually evaluates a hierarchy key and reaches the expected node/condition record.

06

Prove the result

Compare the hierarchy stored in the document with the pricing condition analysis. Change master data or configuration only after the failing layer is known.

troubleshoot

Fast split: wrong parent or wrong validity = master-data problem. Correct hierarchy but no hierarchy access = pricing design problem. Correct access but no condition record = pricing master-data problem. Correct order but unexpected invoice grouping = inspect hierarchy-related billing split evidence.

Assessment recall

Separate identity, relationship, relevance and consumption.

record_voice_over

60-second answer: Customer Hierarchy models a commercial parent-child structure above the sold-to. In classic S/4HANA or ERP-oriented systems, KNVH is the main technical table: its key contains hierarchy type, customer, sales area and valid-from date, while HKUNNR points to the higher-level customer. The hierarchy is time-dependent, so I diagnose it using the order date, not only today's master data. Then I separate three questions: is the relationship correct, is the node relevant for pricing, and does the active pricing access actually consume the hierarchy? I also keep the deployment boundary clear because current Public Cloud uses the newer hierarchy framework and released hierarchy fields rather than direct table access.
