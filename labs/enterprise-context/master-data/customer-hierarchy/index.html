SAP Sales / Master Data / MD.SD.CUSTHIER

Customer Hierarchy

A customer hierarchy represents commercial relationships above the individual sold-to customer. It can model groups, headquarters and subsidiaries so that pricing or reporting can work with a parent structure without changing the legal customer identity used in the order.

Follow the runtime path arrow_downward

Object profile

01MD.SD.CUSTHIERCatalogue ID
02relationship recordData class
Hierarchy type, sales area, parent/child nodes, validity and pricing relevance.

Mental model

The hierarchy adds commercial context above the sold-to; it does not replace the sold-to.

database

Scope: Hierarchy type, sales area, parent/child nodes, validity and pricing relevance.

Important data

Hierarchy type, higher-level and lower-level customer nodes, validity dates, sales area and pricing relevance.

Configuration boundary

The hierarchy is reusable master data. Whether pricing searches it is determined by pricing condition technique and the fields available in the relevant access sequence.

Runtime use

A parent relationship matters only when a consuming rule uses it.

The order still has its normal customer roles. A valid customer hierarchy can add a higher-level commercial context that pricing or analytics may consume. The hierarchy therefore has two separate questions: is the customer in the expected hierarchy for this date and sales area, and does the downstream rule actually use that hierarchy?

01

Start from the sold-to

Use the customer, sales area, and document date from the actual Sales document. Do not begin from a parent node in isolation.

02

Resolve the valid hierarchy

Check the hierarchy type, parent/child assignment, and validity that apply to this customer at that time.

03

Find the consumer

For pricing, prove that the relevant access sequence contains the hierarchy field and that the runtime node reaches the expected condition key. For analytics, prove which hierarchy level is used for grouping.

04

Keep identities separate

A hierarchy-based discount or report grouping does not change the sold-to, ship-to, bill-to, or payer identity stored in the order.

Troubleshooting

A correct hierarchy can still have zero pricing effect.

troubleshoot

First prove the customer's valid hierarchy assignment for the document date and sales area. Then prove that the pricing access actually uses the hierarchy field and reaches the expected node. If the relationship is correct but the access never reads it, changing hierarchy master data will not repair the price.

Assessment recall

Separate customer identity from hierarchy context.

record_voice_over

60-second answer: Customer Hierarchy models a commercial structure above the individual sold-to, for example a parent group with subsidiaries. It does not replace the customer roles in the order. I first prove the valid hierarchy type, sales area, date, and parent relationship. Then I check the consumer: pricing needs an access that actually uses the hierarchy field, while analytics needs a defined grouping level. A valid hierarchy by itself does not guarantee any pricing effect.
