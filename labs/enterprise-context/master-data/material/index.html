SAP S/4HANA / Cross-Logistics / Material Master

Material

Material master data is one of the main control surfaces in SAP Logistics. The same material can carry commercial, planning, warehouse, accounting and international-trade attributes. A Lead should never answer only “check MM03”. The useful answer names the view or classification object, field, organizational scope, validity and runtime mechanism that consumes it.

info

S/4HANA boundary: current International Trade classification is not just another editable field in a plant view. Product classification is maintained through its own numbering schemes, validity and classification apps. The product master can display the result, but the classification lifecycle has its own owner.

Procurement lens

For Purchasing, the material master is a shared source of defaults and control.

A buyer does not maintain all product data in the purchase order. SAP reads reusable values from the material master and combines them with supplier, purchasing info record, source determination and document data. The material master therefore controls the generic product side of procurement, while supplier-specific conditions belong to supplier-related master data.

PurchasingOrder unit, purchasing group and plant-specific purchasing controlsSupports ordering defaults and buyer responsibility for the material at plant level.
MRPMRP type, lot size, procurement type, planned delivery time and planning parametersControls how requirements are planned and whether external procurement is proposed.

rule

Lead rule: separate generic material data from supplier-specific data. The base unit is shared product data, while valuation is maintained for the valuation area. Different suppliers can still have different prices, planned delivery times, order units or supplier material numbers through purchasing master data such as the Purchasing Info Record.

Organizational levels

The same material can exist globally but still be missing for the plant that wants to buy it.

Material data is split by organizational level. This is why a material number can exist in the client and still fail in a purchasing, MRP, inventory or accounting process for a specific plant.

Client level

Contains data shared across the system, for example the material number, base unit and general description.

Plant level

Contains plant-dependent planning, purchasing and logistics data. MRP and many procurement controls are maintained here.

Storage location

Extends the material to the location where stock can be managed. Not every procurement scenario needs storage-location data at PO creation, but inventory execution may need it later.

Valuation area

Contains accounting and valuation data. Depending on system design, the valuation area is the plant or the company code. This is a fundamental Customizing decision and is difficult to reverse later.

account_tree

Assessment pattern: when a PO or goods movement says that the material is not maintained for an organizational level, do not recreate the material. First check whether the required view and plant, storage location or valuation-area extension exists.

Create versus extend

Creating the material and extending the material are different actions.

In classic SAP GUI, MM01 creates a material and also extends an existing material to new views or organizational levels. MM02 changes data that already exists, and MM03 displays it.

01

Choose material type

The material type controls important behavior such as allowed views, number assignment, field selection and whether quantity/value updates are expected.

02

Choose industry sector

The industry sector also influences screen and field behavior. In the classic material master it is fixed after creation, so choose it correctly at the start.

03

Select views

Select only the business views needed for the process, for example Basic Data, Purchasing, MRP and Accounting.

04

Select organization levels

Enter the plant, storage location, sales area or valuation-area context required by the selected views.

05

Maintain data

SAP opens the selected screens in sequence. Mandatory fields depend on configuration and material type.

06

Extend later

If another plant or department starts using the same material, extend the existing material instead of creating a duplicate material number.

history

Audit point: material changes are written to change documents. For shared master data, check who changed the field, when it changed and which organizational level was affected before correcting production data.

PO defaulting

The purchase order is assembled from several master-data sources.

The material master is important, but it is not the only source. The final PO item is the result of material data, supplier data, purchasing relationships, source documents and purchasing configuration.

01

Material master

Provides generic product data such as description, material group, units and plant-specific controls.

02

Supplier Business Partner

Provides supplier identity, address and payment-related master data relevant to the purchasing relationship.

03

Purchasing Info Record

Can provide supplier-material-specific values such as price, planned delivery time, supplier material number and purchasing conditions.

04

Reference document

A purchase requisition, contract, quotation or previous purchasing document can supply additional values and reduce manual entry.

05

PO and configuration

Document type, purchasing organization, plant and Customizing interpret those defaults and determine the final purchasing behavior.

troubleshoot

Diagnostic shortcut: if the material description or base unit is wrong, start with the material. If only one supplier has the wrong price or supplier-specific value, start with the Purchasing Info Record or contract instead of changing the shared material.

Material valuation

Valuation connects Procurement and Inventory Management to Financial Accounting.

For a valuated stock material, SAP keeps stock quantity and stock value together. A goods movement changes logistics data and can create accounting postings at the same time. The basic relationship is stock value = stock quantity × valuation price, but the valuation price behaves differently depending on price control.

Price controlHow stock is valuedWhat happens to differencesTypical use
S — Standard price Inventory is kept at the fixed standard price from the material master. Differences between procurement price and standard price are posted as price differences instead of continuously changing the inventory price. Common where stable standard costing is required. It is often used for manufactured materials, but it is not limited to them.
V — Moving average price Receipts update stock value and SAP recalculates the average price from total stock value divided by total stock quantity. PO and invoice differences can update stock value and the moving average price when stock coverage allows it; otherwise differences can be posted separately. Common for externally purchased materials whose procurement price changes. It is a design choice, not an automatic rule.

Simple MAP example

Current stock: 10 EA with total value 1,000. New receipt: 10 EA with value 1,400. New stock is 20 EA with value 2,400, so the moving average price becomes 120 per EA.

Why stock coverage matters

If an invoice arrives after part of the stock has already been consumed, SAP cannot always put the full invoice difference back into the remaining stock without creating an unrealistic valuation. Part of the difference can therefore go to a price-difference account.

Valuation class

The valuation class groups materials for automatic account determination.

The valuation class is maintained in the Accounting view. It allows many materials with the same accounting meaning to post to the same type of inventory account. The allowed valuation classes depend on configuration for the material type.

What it does

It tells automatic account determination which accounting group the material belongs to, for example raw materials, trading goods or finished goods.

What it does not do

The valuation class does not directly contain a G/L account number. It is one input into the account-determination logic.

Inventory posting

For stock postings, transaction key BSX is used in standard automatic account determination together with valuation-related settings.

GR/IR clearing

For a standard PO goods receipt, the offset normally goes through the GR/IR clearing logic, commonly represented by transaction key WRX.

account_balance

Lead answer: “valuation class determines the stock account” is useful but incomplete. The final G/L account comes from automatic account determination using several inputs, including the transaction/event key, valuation grouping and valuation class. In classic configuration, the main account-determination transaction is OBYC.

Procurement assessment recall

Material Master: the answer in 60 seconds.

record_voice_over

60-second answer: The material master is a central logistics object used by Purchasing, Inventory Management, MRP and Finance. Its data is split into views and organizational levels. Basic data is shared, while Purchasing and MRP are mainly plant-dependent, and Accounting data is maintained for the valuation area. When I create a material, material type and industry sector control the available structure; later I extend the same material to additional views or plants instead of creating a duplicate. In procurement, the material provides generic defaults, while supplier-specific data normally comes from the supplier master, Purchasing Info Record, contracts or quotations. For stock valuation I check price control S or V and the valuation class. The valuation class is an input to automatic account determination, not a G/L account by itself.

QuestionStrong answer
Material exists, but PO says it is not maintained for the plant. What do you do?Check whether the material is extended to the required plant and whether the required Purchasing/MRP/Accounting views exist. Do not create a second material.
Can MM02 add a missing plant view?No. Use the create/extend function, classically MM01, to add missing views or organizational levels. MM02 changes data that already exists.
What is the difference between S and V?S keeps inventory at a fixed standard price and posts variances separately. V recalculates the moving average from current stock value and quantity.
Does valuation class equal the G/L account?No. It groups materials for account determination. The G/L account is derived by automatic account determination using valuation and transaction inputs.
Where should a supplier-specific material price be maintained?Normally in purchasing relationship data such as the Purchasing Info Record, contract or condition records, not in the generic material master.

Connected master data

Material is the hub; other master objects refine identity and behavior.

Classification

Classes and characteristics describe materials and other objects with reusable attributes used for search, batch selection, configuration and integration.

Batch and Serial

Batch identifies a lot; serial number identifies one individual unit. A material can use either or both depending on traceability requirements.

Customer / Supplier relationship

Customer Material and Purchasing Info Record add relationship-specific data without changing the generic material truth.

International Trade

Trade classification links the product to legal code systems. It is country/numbering-scheme and validity aware, so it should not be reduced to one permanent “statistical number”.

Foreign trade classification

The old question “Where is STAWN?” is too small for S/4HANA.

In ECC, consultants often learned one material field as the statistical or commodity-code field. In current S/4HANA International Trade, the stronger mental model is a classification lifecycle: maintain a legal code system, make its content valid, assign the numbering scheme to the relevant country or region, classify the product for a validity period, and let the downstream trade process consume that classification.

01

Numbering scheme

Defines the legal or statistical code system, for example an EU commodity-code scheme.

02

Numbering-scheme content

Contains the actual codes, descriptions, validity and, where relevant, customs units.

03

Country / region assignment

Connects the numbering scheme to the countries or regions for which it is relevant.

04

Product classification

Assigns the code to the product time-dependently. Classification can be maintained in mass.

05

Runtime consumption

Cross-border logistics, customs, compliance and statistical reporting read the classification needed for their legal context.

06

Audit and reclassification

When the tariff changes, a new valid assignment can replace the old one without losing classification history.

Terminology that matters

Commodity code, customs tariff number, ECCN and statistical value are different objects.

This distinction is a good Lead-level assessment check. If these terms are mixed together, design and troubleshooting quickly move to the wrong owner.

ConceptWhat it representsDo not confuse it with
Commodity code A foreign-trade classification number inside a commodity-code numbering scheme. It is commonly used for statistical and customs-related classification. In the EU, the Combined Nomenclature is a typical example. One universal product code valid for every country and every year.
Customs tariff number A separate International Trade nomenclature. S/4HANA supports its own numbering schemes and can also model tariff numbers that depend on product end use. The commodity-code object. They are related, but SAP models them separately.
Control class A legal-control classification such as an Export Control Classification Number (ECCN) where the legal regulation requires it. A customs/statistical tariff code.
Country / region of origin An origin attribute used by trade and customs processes. The tariff or commodity classification. Origin answers “where”; classification answers “what under this nomenclature”.
Statistical value A monetary value required for statistical reporting such as Intrastat. In standard S/4HANA pricing, condition type GRWR can calculate it. The commodity code. One classifies the product; the other values the movement for statistics.

ECC to S/4HANA

Do not migrate the old field mindset as the new architecture.

Legacy fields can still matter for migration, compatibility or localization, but they should not be treated as the design authority for the current S/4HANA International Trade classification model.

Classic mental modelCurrent S/4HANA International Trade modelLead implication
MARC-STAWN / plant-level statistical-number thinking Product classification is represented by dedicated International Trade data. SAP exposes product commodity codes through CDS I_ProdCommodityCodeDEX, with /SAPSLL/MARITC as the main persistence table in the documented extraction mapping. Do not build a new interface or diagnostic only around the legacy field.
Maintain the value directly in a material screen Dedicated Fiori apps classify and reclassify products time-dependently. Product master functions can display current International Trade classification. Separate product maintenance from trade-classification governance.
One number belongs to the material Classification depends on numbering scheme, country/region and validity date. Always ask “which scheme, country and key date?” before saying the code is wrong.
Foreign Trade means one import/export tab International Trade separates commodity codes, customs tariff numbers, control classes, compliance and Intrastat functions. Find the legal object and the process owner before changing data.
warning

Localization caveat: some country-specific solutions can still expose local foreign-trade fields in product/material maintenance. Do not generalize a localization field into the generic S/4HANA International Trade architecture.

Configuration and master-data setup

Configure the code system first; classify products second.

A product cannot be classified correctly if the numbering scheme or its content is missing, inactive, mapped to the wrong country, or valid for the wrong period.

01

Define numbering scheme

Define the classification framework for commodity codes. Customs tariff numbers have their own corresponding setup.

02

Define scheme content

Choose how the codes are supplied: maintained locally, loaded from a data provider, or transferred from SAP GTS.

03

Define format and UoM system

Maintain number length and the Unit of Measure System when supplementary/customs units are part of the content.

04

Activate content

Only valid and activated content should be used for product classification.

05

Assign scheme to country/region

This connects the legal code system to the classification countries that use it.

06

Classify and reclassify

Assign codes to products with validity dates. Plan annual tariff changes before the new legal period starts.

public

EU example: SAP Best Practices uses numbering-scheme content EU01 as an example for the Combined Nomenclature of the European Union. Treat delivered content IDs as implementation content, not as a reason to hard-code one scheme into integrations.

Authorizations: in current S/4HANA Cloud Public Edition content, the main role templates are SAP_BR_TRD_CLS_SPECIALIST and SAP_BR_ADMINISTRATOR_SLL. Private Cloud/on-premise catalogs and role IDs differ, so verify the target release instead of copying Cloud roles into another deployment.

Time dependency

Validity is part of the classification key.

Tariff content changes over time. A product that was correctly classified last year can require a different code next year. S/4HANA therefore supports time-dependent assignment and reclassification instead of forcing the new code to overwrite historical meaning.

Future-dated changes

Prepare the next classification before the legal effective date and make the new assignment valid from that date.

History

Use display/history functions to explain which code was valid on the date of the business transaction.

Mass reclassification

Use mass functions when an annual tariff update changes many products. Do not treat hundreds of manual MM02 changes as a control process.

Audit evidence

Keep classification remarks, change history and ownership visible because tariff classification is a legal master-data decision.

Intrastat and statistical value

Classification and value meet in reporting, but they come from different controls.

Intrastat needs the correct product classification and the correct statistical value. When the report is wrong, first identify which of those two inputs is wrong.

01

Product classification

The valid commodity code comes from International Trade classification for the relevant numbering scheme, country and date.

02

Movement and document context

Quantity, partner, countries, dates and other logistics data come from the relevant procurement, delivery and billing flow.

03

Statistical value

Standard condition type GRWR can calculate the statistical value in pricing and place it in KOMP-GKWRT.

04

Document persistence

SAP documents that the statistical value is stored in the respective billing or purchasing documents.

05

Intrastat declaration

The declaration combines the required transaction data for the reporting period.

fact_check

Assessment correction: do not say that all International Trade data is maintained or stored only at delivery level. Product classification is master data, while statistical value is a pricing-derived document value and SAP explicitly supports it in both Sales billing and Purchasing contexts.

AI-assisted classification

Use proposals to reduce search work, not to remove legal ownership.

Starting with S/4HANA 2023 FPS03, SAP supports trade-classification proposals for commodity codes and customs tariff numbers using SAP HANA fuzzy search over similarly described products. Current Cloud content also exposes classification decisions created with agent assistance.

What the system can propose

The system compares an unclassified product with previously classified products and can calculate proposal scores from similar descriptions.

What still needs governance

A similarity score is not legal proof that a tariff classification is correct. The trade specialist still needs controlled source data, review and an auditable decision.

Where AI helps

Use proposals for candidate ranking, exception queues and faster review of large product sets. Measure acceptance quality and rework, not only how many suggestions were generated.

Where AI should stop

Do not let an opaque model update legal classifications directly without role control, effective dates, traceability and a defined correction process.

Integration boundary

Choose one owner for tariff content and one controlled path into S/4HANA.

The numbering-scheme content can be local, supplied by a data provider or transferred from SAP GTS. That is an architectural choice. Avoid maintaining the same legal code list independently in S/4HANA, middleware, GTS and a customs broker without clear ownership.

External data provider

Use governed content integration when tariff updates are supplied externally. Validate versions, activation and application logs before classifying products.

SAP GTS

When GTS owns trade content or compliance, make the distribution direction explicit and avoid parallel manual maintenance.

APIs and extraction

For integrations, prefer released APIs/CDS where available. SAP documents commodity-code extraction with I_ProdCommodityCodeDEX instead of requiring consumers to read legacy material fields.

Transportation Management

TM can have its own commodity-code source and apps for transportation. Confirm whether TM consumes product classification or a TM-specific code before diagnosing “the commodity code”.

Lead diagnosis

Trace the first wrong derived value.

01

Name the symptom

Wrong item category, ATP result, plant, shipping point, source, valuation, commodity code or warehouse behavior are different paths.

02

Name the view or scheme

Sales, plant, MRP, accounting, warehouse and International Trade data have different scopes. For trade classification add numbering scheme, country and key date.

03

Name the field or assignment

Find the exact master-data input that can influence the failed result.

04

Trace the mechanism

Configuration and runtime logic interpret the field; the material master does not make every decision by itself.

05

Check blast radius

A shared material or classification change can affect Sales, Procurement, MRP, Inventory, EWM, Production, Finance, customs and reporting.

Foreign trade diagnostics

Start with scheme, country and date before changing the product.

No commodity code available

Check whether the numbering-scheme content exists, is activated and is valid for the required period before investigating the product assignment.

Product is unclassified

Check the classification country, numbering scheme and key date. A code can exist globally while no valid product assignment exists.

Old code still appears

Check validity periods and reclassification history. Do not overwrite history simply to make today’s screen look correct.

Intrastat value is wrong

Separate classification from valuation. If the code is correct, inspect GRWR, pricing, Incoterms and the statistical-value document flow.

Code differs by country

That can be correct. Product classification is legal-context dependent; compare numbering schemes rather than expecting one global number.

TM shows another code

Confirm the TM commodity-code source. A correct International Trade classification does not prove that TM is configured to consume the same source.

Lead design decisions

Classification is a governed decision, not a field-filling task.

Ownership

Define who owns the legal code content, who classifies products, who approves exceptions and who resolves blocked trade documents.

Content source

Choose local maintenance, a data provider or SAP GTS deliberately. The choice affects update speed, licensing, monitoring and recovery.

Annual change process

Plan future-dated tariff updates, mass reclassification, testing and cutover before the new tariff period becomes legally effective.

Migration

Map legacy fields to the S/4 numbering-scheme model. Validate country and validity semantics instead of copying old field values blindly.

Integration contract

Expose stable business keys and validity. Do not make external systems depend on internal tables when a released interface exists.

Control evidence

Keep change history and reviewer evidence because tariff classification can affect duties, declarations and legal controls.

Assessment recall

Do not say “material master”; say which view, classification and why.

record_voice_over

60-second answer: Material master data is cross-functional. Sales-area views provide commercial inputs, plant and MRP views control planning and availability, logistics fields influence shipping and warehouse execution, and accounting views control valuation. For International Trade I do not treat the old statistical-number field as the design model. I identify the numbering scheme, classification country, validity date and product assignment, then separate commodity code, customs tariff number, legal-control class and statistical value. If the result is wrong, I trace the first wrong source and the process that consumed it before changing shared product data.

Likely follow-up

Why is S/4HANA better for tariff changes? Product assignments are time-dependent and can be reclassified in mass, so a new legal period does not require destroying the previous classification history.

Likely follow-up

Is GRWR the commodity code? No. GRWR is a pricing condition used to determine statistical value. The commodity code is product classification master data.
