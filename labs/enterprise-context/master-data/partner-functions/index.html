Customer Sales Partner Functions

Sales documents rarely contain only one customer role. Sold-to, ship-to, bill-to and payer can be different Business Partners. Customer sales partner functions store the maintained relationships that allow SAP to propose those roles consistently for a sales area.

Follow the runtime path arrow_downward

Object profile

01MD.SD.PARTNERRELCatalogue ID
02relationship recordData class
Customer, sales area, partner function and partner counter.

Mental model

Configuration asks for a role; master data supplies the partner.

database

Scope: Customer, sales area, partner function and partner counter.

Important data

Partner function, assigned partner, sales area, partner counter for multiple relationships and relevant validity or relationship data.

Configuration boundary

The partner procedure is configuration; the partner assignment is master data. A required role can therefore fail either because configuration expects it or because no suitable master relationship exists.

Runtime use

The document resolves roles first, then downstream processes consume those roles.

At document creation the partner-determination procedure defines which partner functions are relevant and how they are resolved. Customer or Business Partner master data supplies maintained relationships. The document stores the resulting sold-to, ship-to, bill-to, payer, and any other relevant partners, and later processes consume the role that matters to them.

01

Name the wrong role

Do not say “the customer is wrong.” State whether the wrong value is sold-to, ship-to, bill-to, payer, or another partner function.

02

Read the procedure

Check whether that role is required, optional, repeatable, or otherwise controlled by the active partner-determination design.

03

Read the relationship

Verify the maintained partner assignment for the relevant customer and sales area, including multiple relationships where they are allowed.

04

Prove the document partner

Compare the master relationship with the partner actually stored in the sales document and check whether a user, reference document, or interface changed it.

05

Follow the consequence

Trace only the downstream behavior owned by that role: destination, invoice recipient, payer context, output recipient, or another role-specific result.

Troubleshooting

“Wrong customer” is too vague; name the partner function.

troubleshoot

Start with the actual partner set in the document and identify the wrong role. Then compare the active partner procedure with the sales-area relationship that should supply that role. If master data is correct but the document differs, inspect reference-document transfer, explicit interface input, or a permitted manual change before editing the shared relationship.

Assessment recall

Separate role control from partner master data.

record_voice_over

60-second answer: I separate the rule from the relationship. Partner determination defines which roles the Sales document needs and how they are resolved; customer or Business Partner master data provides the maintained partners for those roles. When a partner is wrong, I name the function first, compare the procedure and master relationship, then prove what the document actually stored and whether reference data, an interface, or a manual change replaced it. That is safer than changing the sold-to record because a ship-to or payer is wrong.
