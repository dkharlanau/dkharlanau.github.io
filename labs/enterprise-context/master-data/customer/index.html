SAP S/4HANA / Sales / Customer Master Data

Customer

In SAP S/4HANA, the customer is managed through the Business Partner framework. The Business Partner gives us one identity. Customer roles add the application-specific data needed by Sales and Finance. The important Lead question is therefore not only “which customer field is wrong?”, but “which role, organizational scope, relationship, configuration rule, or runtime document owns this value?”

Mental model

One identity, several application views.

A customer record is easier to understand when we separate identity from process-specific extensions. General Business Partner data is shared. Customer roles expose Sales and Accounting views. CVI keeps the Business Partner and customer application objects synchronized.

Business Partner

Shared identity such as name, addresses, communication data, tax identifiers, bank details, industries and relationships. The BP category describes whether the partner is a person, organization or group.

Sales customer

Role FLCU01. Sales-area data is maintained for Sales Organization + Distribution Channel + Division and controls order, shipping and billing defaults.

FI customer

Role FLCU00. Company-code data controls Accounts Receivable behavior such as reconciliation account, payment processing and dunning.

account_tree

Lead rule: do not treat BP and customer as two independent masters. The BP is the central identity; customer views are application extensions synchronized through Customer/Vendor Integration (CVI).

Creation flow

Create the identity first, then extend the scopes the process needs.

The exact screen differs by deployment and app, but the logical sequence is stable.

01

Choose BP category and grouping

Category defines the identity type. Grouping controls the BP number range and is part of the numbering design.

02

Create the Business Partner

Maintain the shared identity: name, address, communication, tax and other general data.

03

Add customer roles

Add FLCU01 for Sales data, FLCU00 for FI-AR data, or both when the customer participates in the complete Order-to-Cash process.

04

Maintain organizational views

Create the required sales areas and company-code segments. A customer can exist globally but still be unusable in one sales area or company code.

05

Maintain partner relationships

Confirm sold-to, ship-to, bill-to and payer relationships, plus any additional partner functions required by the active procedure.

06

Save and prove synchronization

Check that CVI created or updated the customer-side records and that the customer works in the intended process, not only that the BP screen saved.

info

S/4HANA boundary: transaction BP and supported Fiori apps are the main maintenance entry points. Classic transactions such as XD01, VD01 and FD01 belong to older ERP or compatible private/on-premise operating models and should not be presented as the universal S/4HANA method.

Control model

BP grouping, customer account group and CVI solve different problems.

They are often mixed together in interview answers. Keep the three responsibilities separate.

BP grouping

Controls Business Partner numbering and the assigned BP number range. It is chosen when the BP is created and is not a field we should expect to change casually later.

Customer account group

Controls customer-side field status, customer number range and important SD behavior such as the partner-determination procedure assigned to the customer type.

CVI mapping

Connects the BP model with the customer application model. It maps BP groupings and customer account groups and synchronizes the linked objects.

link

Numbering decision: the BP number and customer number can be designed to match or to use different ranges. The important point is that CVI mapping is explicit and integrations do not assume equality when the landscape does not guarantee it.

Partner relationships

“Customer” is not one runtime role.

Sales documents resolve partner functions. The same legal customer can order, receive, be invoiced and pay through different partner records.

RoleBusiness questionTypical downstream effect
Sold-to partyWho places the order?Primary sales relationship, sales-area context and many commercial defaults.
Ship-to partyWho receives the goods?Delivery address, receiving context, route and logistics-relevant data.
Bill-to partyWho receives the invoice?Billing address and invoice communication context.
PayerWho settles the receivable?Payment-oriented billing and Accounts Receivable context.

The standard four roles are common, but mandatory status and additional functions are controlled by partner determination. See the dedicated Partner Functions page for the full control chain and internal function codes.

Runtime

Master data proposes behavior; the document becomes runtime truth.

A sales order does not simply “read the customer.” It resolves partners, reads scope-specific master data, combines it with configuration and may copy values into the document. After that, reference documents, interfaces or permitted manual changes can produce a result different from the current master.

01

Resolve the sales area

The sold-to party must be extended to the required Sales Organization, Distribution Channel and Division.

02

Resolve partners

Partner determination supplies ship-to, bill-to, payer and any additional roles required by the procedure.

03

Read commercial defaults

Pricing, tax, shipping and billing fields are read from the relevant master-data scope and combined with other determination inputs.

04

Run cross-process checks

Availability, credit, delivery blocks, material settings and other controls can stop the process even when the customer master itself is valid.

05

Store document values

The document now carries partners and copied values. Troubleshooting must compare the document result with the master and the rule that produced it.

Cross-process boundary

Credit Management is related to the customer, but it is not the same master-data layer.

credit_score

In SAP Credit Management, credit-relevant master data belongs to Business Partner role UKM000. The credit profile and credit-segment data hold fields such as risk class, check rule and credit limit. If an order is blocked by credit, checking only KNVV or the customer sales-area screen is not enough. Follow the credit-management object and the credit segment.

Specificity

The generic customer record is not always the most specific source.

When a document value looks wrong, first check whether another master-data relationship intentionally overrides or specializes the generic customer default.

Multiple addresses

Modern S/4HANA scenarios can use several BP addresses and, where activated, address-dependent customer Sales data. Do not create a new customer only because one customer has several receiving locations without first checking the address and partner model.

Customer hierarchy

A hierarchy is a separate relationship structure used for scenarios such as hierarchy pricing, reporting or commercial control. It does not replace the sales-area customer record.

Customer-material record

Customer-specific product numbers, descriptions and selected sales defaults can be more specific than generic customer or material data.

Lifecycle and control

Blocking is not one global switch.

Different blocks protect different parts of the process. The right scope matters.

Sales-area blocks

Order, delivery, billing and sales-support blocks can stop selected Sales activities for one sales area or more broadly, depending on maintenance and configuration.

Company-code block

Posting blocks control FI activity. Do not confuse an FI posting block with a Sales order or delivery block.

End of purpose

Data lifecycle, blocking and deletion must also respect retention, open business and data-protection rules. Modern S/4HANA provides BP/customer blocking processes and apps for this lifecycle.

block

Support habit: when a process is blocked, identify the exact block type and scope before removing it. A block can be intentional control, not bad master data.

Diagnostics

Start from the symptom and prove the failing layer.

Customer-master incidents are often solved faster when we separate BP identity, customer scope, partner relationship, CVI synchronization and the downstream process.

SymptomLikely boundaryFirst evidence to check
Customer exists but order cannot use the sales area Sales extension Does role FLCU01 contain the exact Sales Organization + Distribution Channel + Division? Check the corresponding sales-area customer data.
FI posting cannot use the customer in a company code FI customer extension Does role FLCU00 contain the company-code segment and required accounting fields such as the reconciliation account?
BP saves but customer synchronization fails CVI Check BP grouping ↔ customer account-group mapping, number ranges, mandatory fields and the CVI postprocessing log. In supported private/on-premise scenarios, MDS_PPO2 is a key postprocessing transaction.
Wrong ship-to, bill-to or payer Partner relationship / partner determination Compare the partner stored in the document with the customer sales partner assignment and the active partner procedure.
Wrong price or tax Determination chain Identify which customer field is only an input and then trace the pricing or tax determination that consumes it.
Order is credit blocked SAP Credit Management Check BP role UKM000, credit profile, credit segment, exposure and the active credit check instead of treating it as an ordinary sales-area master-data issue.
Interface has BP number but expects customer number Identity mapping / CVI Check the BP-to-customer link and whether the numbering design guarantees equal numbers. Do not infer equality from one environment.

Lead decisions

Good customer design reduces duplicate identities and hidden exceptions.

There is no single correct pattern for every landscape. These are the decisions worth making explicitly.

One customer or several ship-to parties?

Use separate partner records when business ownership, logistics, tax, reporting or integration requires a distinct partner identity. Use address capabilities when the difference is only location and the deployment supports the required address-dependent behavior.

Same BP and customer number?

Matching numbers simplify search and integration. Different numbers can preserve legacy numbering or governance. Either design works only when CVI mapping and interfaces handle it deliberately.

Customer and supplier on one BP?

If the same legal partner plays both roles, one BP can carry customer and supplier roles. Do not merge different legal or operational parties only to reduce master-data count.

Assessment recall

Answer with layers, ownership and proof.

record_voice_over

60-second answer: In S/4HANA I treat the customer as a Business Partner with customer roles, not as a separate identity. General BP data is shared. FLCU01 carries Sales customer data by sales area, and FLCU00 carries FI customer data by company code. CVI maps the BP model to the customer application model. In a Sales issue I first identify the exact partner function and sales area, then check the customer field that should supply the value, the configuration that interprets it, and finally the value stored in the document. Credit is a separate BP role, UKM000, so I do not mix ordinary customer master data with credit master data.

Likely follow-up: BP grouping vs account group?

Grouping belongs to Business Partner numbering. The customer account group controls customer-side number range, field status and related customer behavior. CVI maps the two models.

Likely follow-up: why can a valid BP fail in Sales?

The BP can exist without the required FLCU01 sales-area extension, partner assignments, or mandatory Sales data for the exact organizational scope.

Likely follow-up: where do you troubleshoot?

Start with the failing document and scope, then verify BP/customer data, partner relationships, CVI status, determination logic and downstream process controls.
