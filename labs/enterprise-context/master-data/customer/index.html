SAP S/4HANA / Sales / Customer Master Data

Customer

In S/4HANA, Customer is not an isolated master object. It is a Business Partner extended with customer roles and organizational views. For Sales, the key layer is the customer role with sales-area data. For Finance, company-code data matters. The same business partner can therefore support several business processes without duplicating the identity.

Data model

Separate the customer identity from the organizational views.

Business Partner

Shared identity, name, addresses, communication data and relationships. This is the cross-application identity anchor.

Sales Area

Sales Organization + Distribution Channel + Division. This is where Sales-specific customer behavior is maintained.

Company Code

Accounting-specific customer data used by FI-AR, such as reconciliation and payment-related settings.

Lead diagnosis

Start from the wrong document value, then prove the source.

01

Identify the symptom

Wrong payer, shipping condition, payment term, pricing key, tax classification, or block are different problems.

02

Check the exact role and scope

Confirm BP, customer role, sales area or company code used by the process.

03

Check more specific layers

Partner relationships, customer-material data, hierarchy or document overrides can be more specific than a generic customer default.

04

Trace the consumer

Separate the maintained value from the configuration that interprets it.

Assessment recall

Customer is a BP capability, not a second identity.

record_voice_over

60-second answer: In S/4HANA I start with the Business Partner identity and then check the customer roles and organizational views. FLCU01 carries Sales customer data by sales area; FLCU00 carries customer accounting data by company code. For a Sales issue I name the exact sales-area field, verify whether a more specific relationship record overrides the generic value, and then trace the determination that consumes it.
