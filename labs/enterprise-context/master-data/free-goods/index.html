SAP Sales / Master Data / MD.SD.FREEGOODS

Free Goods Condition Record

Free goods define quantity-based commercial incentives such as 'buy ten, receive one free'. The record describes when the incentive applies and how the free quantity is calculated.

Follow the runtime path arrow_downward

Object profile

01MD.SD.FREEGOODSCatalogue ID
02condition recordData class
Customer/product key, validity, minimum quantity and calculation rule.

Mental model

The record defines the entitlement; configuration defines the free-item behavior.

database

Scope: Customer/product key, validity, minimum quantity and calculation rule.

Important data

Condition key, validity period, minimum quantity, free-goods quantity or ratio, unit and inclusive/exclusive calculation mode.

Configuration boundary

The condition record stores the commercial entitlement. Procedure assignment and item behavior are configuration.

Runtime use

A quantity threshold becomes an entitlement, then a Sales item structure.

Free-goods determination searches the relevant customer/product context and validates date, quantity, unit, and the maintained calculation rule. When the order qualifies, the record supplies the entitlement. Configuration then decides how that entitlement appears in the Sales document and how the resulting free quantity behaves.

01

Follow the determination key

Use the actual customer, product, date, and other condition fields to prove which free-goods record can be reached.

02

Check qualification

Validate the ordered quantity, unit, minimum threshold, validity period, and the record used for the entitlement.

03

Calculate the benefit

Read the maintained free quantity or ratio and distinguish inclusive from exclusive logic before comparing document quantities.

04

Read the resulting item structure

If entitlement is correct but the free item behaves incorrectly, move from master data to item-category and process configuration.

05

Keep recommendations separate

Cross-selling can suggest another product; Free Goods creates a commercial quantity entitlement. The two mechanisms answer different business questions.

Troubleshooting

Separate “not entitled” from “entitled but wrong item behavior”.

troubleshoot

If no free quantity appears, prove the condition key, validity, ordered quantity, unit, and threshold first. If the record qualifies and the calculated entitlement is correct but the document structure or pricing is wrong, move to free-goods and item-category configuration. Do not create another condition record to repair an item-behavior problem.

Assessment recall

Entitlement first; item behavior second.

record_voice_over

60-second answer: Free Goods is quantity-based commercial entitlement stored in condition master data. I first prove the runtime key, validity, ordered unit and threshold, then calculate the expected free quantity and distinguish inclusive from exclusive logic. The record tells me what the customer is entitled to; configuration decides how the free quantity appears and behaves in the Sales document. That is also different from Cross-Selling, which only recommends an additional product.
