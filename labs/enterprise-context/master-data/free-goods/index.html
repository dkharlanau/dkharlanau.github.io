Free Goods Condition Record

Free Goods is the SD mechanism for quantity-based commercial incentives such as “buy ten, get one free”. The condition record defines the entitlement. Pricing and item configuration decide how the free quantity is represented and valued.

Start with worked examples arrow_downward

Object profile

01MD.SD.FREEGOODSCatalogue ID
02condition recordData class
Customer/product key, validity, minimum quantity, calculation rule and free quantity.

Mental model

Free Goods determines quantity first. Pricing determines value second.

A pricing condition answers “what amount should I charge?”. A Free Goods record answers “does this order qualify for a free quantity, and how much?”. Keep these two questions separate when you design or troubleshoot the process.

database

Ownership boundary: the condition record stores the commercial entitlement. Procedure assignment, item category, pricing relevance, ATP and delivery behavior belong to configuration and downstream processing.

Important record data

Condition key, validity period, minimum quantity, reference quantity, free quantity, unit and calculation rule.

Lead question

Do not ask only “which transaction maintains Free Goods?”. Ask where the promotion is owned, what quantity qualifies, how partial confirmation should behave, and how Finance wants the benefit reported.

Worked examples

The easiest way to understand Free Goods is to follow the quantities.

The scenarios below are illustrative examples. They show the reasoning pattern; they are not evidence from a specific client system.

Example 1 — inclusive

The commercial offer is “10 pieces for the price of 9”.

Customer orders: 10 EA
Delivered:       10 EA
Chargeable:       9 EA
Free:             1 EA

The physical quantity stays 10. SAP separates the free part from the chargeable part. This model fits a promotion where the customer receives the requested quantity but pays for less.

Example 2 — exclusive

The commercial offer is “buy 10, get 1 extra”.

Customer orders: 10 EA
Chargeable:      10 EA
Free:             1 EA
Delivered:       11 EA

The free quantity is additional. This model is useful for bonus units, launch samples or an extra promotional material.

rule

Memory rule: inclusive means part of the requested quantity is free; exclusive means free quantity is added on top of the requested quantity.

Calculation rule

“10 + 1” is still incomplete until we define how 25 pieces should behave.

Assume the agreement is one free unit for every ten ordered units. The business result depends on the calculation rule.

Ordered quantity Proportional idea Whole-unit idea Question for the business
9 EA Below the qualifying base 0 free Does a minimum threshold block the promotion?
10 EA 1 free 1 free This is the simple qualifying case.
19 EA Could scale proportionally 1 free Do incomplete groups count?
20 EA 2 free 2 free Both models align on a full multiple.
25 EA Could produce a proportional result before rounding 2 free What are the UoM and rounding rules?

The technical configuration only makes sense after the commercial rule is clear. “Buy 10, get 1” sounds precise, but it is not precise enough for 19, 25 or 29 units.

Runtime use

A quantity threshold becomes an entitlement, then a Sales item structure.

Free-goods determination searches the relevant customer/product context and validates date, quantity, unit and the maintained calculation rule. When the order qualifies, the record supplies the entitlement. Configuration then decides how that entitlement appears in the Sales document and how the resulting free quantity behaves.

01

Follow the determination key

Use the actual customer, product, date and other condition fields to prove which free-goods record can be reached.

02

Check qualification

Validate the ordered quantity, unit, minimum threshold, validity period and the record used for the entitlement.

03

Calculate the benefit

Read the maintained free quantity or ratio and distinguish inclusive from exclusive logic before comparing document quantities.

04

Read the resulting item structure

If entitlement is correct but the free item behaves incorrectly, move from master data to item-category and process configuration.

05

Trace downstream execution

A free item can still consume stock, require confirmation, move through delivery and create cost. “Free” is a commercial statement, not a logistics shortcut.

ATP and delivery example

A promotion can be correct commercially and still create a bad logistics result.

Consider a simple exclusive promotion.

Ordered main product:     100 EA
Free Goods entitlement:    10 EA
Confirmed main quantity:   40 EA

Possible business rule A

Keep all 10 free units because the customer qualified based on the original order quantity.

Possible business rule B

Reduce the free quantity to 4 because only 40% of the main quantity is currently confirmed.

Possible business rule C

Do not deliver the free item until the main item reaches the required delivery quantity.

local_shipping

Lead decision: define the partial-delivery rule with Sales and Logistics before go-live. Otherwise ATP may expose a requirement that was never specified in the promotion design.

Pricing and Finance example

Zero customer price does not mean zero business cost.

A free product normally still leaves inventory and therefore still has cost. Finance may want only the cost of the free item, or explicit visibility of the promotional discount.

Model 1 — no price on the free item

Free item price: 0
Revenue:         0
Cost:            visible downstream

This keeps the Sales document simple. The commercial effect is visible mainly through the free item and its cost.

Model 2 — price, then discount to zero

Gross value:        100 EUR
Promotion discount: -100 EUR
Net value:             0 EUR

This can give Finance and profitability reporting a clearer view of the commercial value given away.

The configuration question is therefore not only “should the free item be priced?”. The stronger question is how should the promotion be represented in margin and profitability reporting?

Choose the right mechanism

Do not use Free Goods for every zero-price scenario.

Business question Better starting mechanism Illustrative case
Should an existing item become free? Pricing / 100% discount MAT-X is already ordered; only its commercial value changes.
Should quantity be created or split because of a promotion? Free Goods For every 10 MAT-A, grant 1 free unit.
Which material should replace the one entered? Material Determination Customer enters old MAT-A; SAP substitutes MAT-B.
May this customer order the material at all? Listing / Exclusion MAT-B is blocked for customer C1000.

Troubleshooting

Separate “not entitled” from “entitled but wrong item behavior”.

01

Document context

Check the real sales area, customer, material, date, quantity and sales unit. Do not debug from a screenshot of the condition record alone.

02

Qualification

Verify validity, minimum quantity and the calculation rule. A record can exist and still not qualify.

03

Determination path

Prove which key and access should find the record. If a required runtime field is empty or different, the record is unreachable.

04

Expected entitlement

Calculate the expected free quantity manually. This separates condition-master problems from later item-behavior problems.

05

Downstream behavior

If the quantity is correct but pricing, ATP, delivery or billing is wrong, move to the owning configuration layer instead of creating another condition record.

troubleshoot

Diagnostic rule: first prove entitlement. Then prove item behavior. This prevents master-data fixes from being used to hide configuration problems.

Design questions

Before configuration, make the promotion precise.

Commercial rule

Inclusive or exclusive? Same product or another product? Minimum quantity? Whole units or proportional result? Which UoM controls the threshold?

Execution rule

What happens with partial ATP confirmation, split deliveries, backorders and unavailable promotional stock?

Financial rule

Should the benefit appear only as a zero-value free item, or should Finance see the gross value and promotional deduction separately?

Assessment recall

Entitlement first; item behavior second.

record_voice_over

60-second answer: Free Goods is a quantity-based commercial entitlement in SD condition master data. I first prove the runtime key, validity, ordered unit and threshold, then calculate the expected free quantity and distinguish inclusive from exclusive logic. The record tells me what the customer is entitled to; configuration decides how the free quantity appears and behaves in the Sales document. In a real design I also clarify ATP and partial deliveries, pricing and profitability reporting, and the boundary to normal discounts or material substitution.
