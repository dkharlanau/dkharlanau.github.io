SAP S/4HANA / EAM / Cross-Logistics Master Data

Equipment

Equipment represents an individual technical object that needs its own lifecycle. It becomes relevant when logistics traceability is not enough and the unit also needs installation, maintenance, warranty, service or technical-history data.

Boundary

01SerialLogistics identity
02EquipmentTechnical lifecycle
Do not create Equipment for every serialized unit by default.

When it matters

Use Equipment when the individual unit becomes an asset to manage.

Serial only

Enough for receive, move, ship, return and prove logistics history.

Serial + Equipment

Needed when maintenance orders, technical location, installation history, warranty or service lifecycle must follow the unit.

Lead trade-off

Equipment gives stronger lifecycle control but adds governance, status management and maintenance responsibility.
