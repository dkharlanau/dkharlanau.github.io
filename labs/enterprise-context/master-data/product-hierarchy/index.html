Product Hierarchy and Sales Groupings

Product hierarchy gives Sales a commercial classification that is broader than a single material. It is useful when pricing, reporting or segmentation should operate on groups of products rather than maintaining rules for every material number.

Follow the runtime path arrow_downward

Object profile

01MD.SD.PRODHIERCatalogue ID
02relationship recordData class
Product, sales area and hierarchy/grouping value.

Mental model

A hierarchy classifies products; it has no business effect until a rule consumes it.

database

Scope: Product, sales area and hierarchy/grouping value.

Important data

Product hierarchy, product group-related sales fields, sales organization/distribution channel scope and version or validity where the implementation uses dedicated hierarchy structures.

Configuration boundary

A product hierarchy value is master data. The decision to include it in pricing access sequences, analytics models or selection logic belongs to configuration/design.

Runtime use

The hierarchy lets one rule operate above individual material level.

A product carries a hierarchy or grouping value in its Sales context. Pricing can use that value as part of a condition key, while analytics can use it to aggregate products into commercial families. The hierarchy becomes operational only when a consuming rule, report, or selection model is designed to read it.

01

Read the product context

Confirm the product and Sales view used by the document, then identify the maintained hierarchy or grouping value.

02

Name the business use

Is the hierarchy meant to drive pricing, reporting, segmentation, selection, or another rule? A populated field alone explains nothing.

03

Trace the consumer

For pricing, follow the condition table and access that uses the hierarchy. For analytics, confirm the level and aggregation logic used by the report.

04

Compare at the right level

When several materials share a hierarchy node, test whether the result is consistently group-based before changing one product record.

Troubleshooting

Do not blame the hierarchy until you prove the consumer.

troubleshoot

Check the hierarchy value on the exact product Sales view used by the order. Then inspect the pricing access, report, or selection logic that is supposed to consume it. If the field is correct but the consumer never reads it, changing the product hierarchy cannot repair the result.

Assessment recall

Classification first; business effect second.

record_voice_over

60-second answer: Product Hierarchy is a commercial classification above individual material level. The product master supplies the hierarchy value, but that value has no effect by itself. I first verify the correct Sales view and hierarchy node, then the consumer: for pricing, the condition key and access; for analytics, the grouping level and report logic. This separates wrong classification from a rule that simply does not use the hierarchy.
