Batch Master and Classification

Batch master data identifies a specific lot of a product and can carry characteristics such as production date, shelf life, origin or quality attributes. Sales and delivery use those values when the product is batch-managed and selection matters.

Product, plant, batch, status and classification characteristics.

Mental model

Know what the object owns before tracing a document.

database

Scope: Product, plant, batch, status and classification characteristics.

Important data

Batch number, material/product, plant or batch level depending on design, status, shelf-life dates and classification characteristics.

Configuration boundary

Batch characteristics and status are master data. The batch-determination strategy, search procedure and selection class are configuration.

Runtime use

How the data enters Order-to-Cash.

Batch determination during delivery or earlier processes can search eligible batches according to strategy and classification. Warehouse execution and traceability then continue with the selected batch.

Troubleshooting

Start with the most specific evidence.

troubleshoot

Confirm that the product is batch-managed and identify the plant/storage context. Check batch status and classification values, then trace the determination strategy if an expected batch is not selected.

Assessment recall

A concise answer that still shows depth.

record_voice_over

Batch Master describes individual lots. I separate batch attributes from the strategy that selects a batch, and I verify plant, status, characteristics and availability together.
