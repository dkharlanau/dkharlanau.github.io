Material Determination Record

Material determination lets Sales translate a material entered by a user or interface into another product according to maintained rules. The simple case is a direct replacement. The more interesting case is product selection: SAP can evaluate several alternatives, use availability, and build the Sales item structure from the result.

The record stores the mapping. Configuration decides how SAP finds it and what happens after it is found.

Mental model

Think in three layers: search, record, behavior.

Most material-determination incidents become easier once these layers are separated. A valid condition record cannot help if SAP never reaches its key. A correct access can still return the wrong business result if the substitution reason or product-selection settings do not match the requirement.

1. Determination engine

The procedure, determination type, access sequence and condition tables decide which keys SAP will search and in which order.

2. Condition record

The record contains the entered material, substitute material or alternatives, validity, substitution reason and the business dimensions defined by the condition table.

3. Runtime behavior

The substitution reason and product-selection design decide whether SAP replaces directly, asks the user, checks alternatives with ATP, creates subitems, or can redetermine the product later.

rule

Lead rule: do not start by creating another VB11 record. First prove that the document uses the expected procedure and that the access sequence reaches the key you intend to maintain.

Where it fits

The same mechanism can solve very different business problems.

Identifier translation

A customer, scanner or upstream process enters an external identifier and Sales resolves it to the internal material used for order processing.

Product transition

An old or discontinued product points to its successor. This is useful during controlled phase-out, but the business still needs rules for pricing, communication and open orders.

Seasonal or promotional replacement

A normal product can be replaced for a defined period by promotional or seasonal packaging. Validity dates become part of the commercial rule.

Availability-driven selection

Several alternatives can be maintained in priority order and evaluated with availability. This is not just a master-data lookup; ATP and item structure become part of the result.

swap_horiz

Do not confuse mechanisms: Customer-Material Info maps customer-specific product identity, Item Proposal helps users enter a basket, Listing/Exclusion controls eligibility, Cross-Selling suggests related products, and Material Determination changes the requested product according to a substitution rule.

Record anatomy

A good record is more than “old material → new material.”

VB11 stores the business rule that the determination engine will consume. The exact key depends on the condition table, so the same entered material can have a different substitute for another customer, Sales organization, channel or other configured context.

Determination type

Selects the material-determination logic whose access sequence will search for the record. Delivered identifiers depend on deployment and release, so do not treat one key such as A001 or Y001 as universal.

Validity

Valid-from and valid-to dates control when the mapping can be used. For classic product selection, the requested delivery date is an important runtime date for the validity check.

Material entered

The product or identifier entered in the Sales document. This is the source side of the substitution rule.

Substitute material

The product SAP should use or propose. A record can contain several alternatives, and their maintained sequence is meaningful.

Unit of measure

Some substitution scenarios need a target unit of measure as part of the mapping. Treat UoM conversion as business behavior, not a display detail.

Substitution reason

The reason is a control object. It can influence messages, user selection, printing of the entered product, item structure and later redetermination behavior depending on the scenario.

format_list_numbered

Useful design trick: in availability-driven product selection, the original material can be maintained as the first alternative. SAP can try to confirm the requested product first and only move to substitutes when it cannot cover the demand.

Configuration chain

The condition record works only inside the condition-technique chain.

The classic/private/on-premise configuration follows the same pattern as other SD determination techniques. The important assessment point is the ownership of each object, not memorizing transaction codes without the dependency between them.

01

OV26 — Field catalog

Defines which fields are available for material-determination keys. Classic extensibility can add customer fields when the standard catalog is not enough.

02

OV16 — Condition tables

Build the key combination, for example entered material only or a more specific customer/Sales-context/material key.

03

OV11 — Access sequences

Define which condition tables are searched and in which order. Put specific business rules before deliberate fallback rules.

04

OV12 — Determination types

Assign the access sequence to the material-determination type. This object connects the business determination to the search path.

05

OV13 — Procedures

Group one or more determination types into the procedure that a Sales document can use.

06

OV14 — Assign procedure

Assign the material-determination procedure to the relevant Sales document type. Without the assignment, the expected determination is not triggered for that document type.

07

OVRQ — Substitution reasons

Configure how the found alternative should behave. This is where a simple mapping becomes a user message, a manual choice, automatic product selection or later redetermination behavior.

08

VB11 — Maintain records

Create the actual requested-to-substitute mapping, validity and alternatives. VB12 changes it and VB13 displays it.

account_tree

Key-design trade-off: a customer-specific key gives precise control but multiplies master-data volume. A broad entered-material key is easier to maintain but can substitute the product in business contexts where the replacement is not allowed. Use the smallest key that still protects the real business rule.

Runtime

The same record framework can lead to three different user experiences.

When a product is entered, SAP first has to reach a valid material-determination record. What happens next depends on the substitution reason and the configured product-selection behavior.

01

Capture the entered product and context

Start from what the user or interface actually entered, the Sales document type, Sales area, customer context and requested delivery date.

02

Determine the procedure and type

Read the procedure assigned to the Sales document type, then the determination type and its access sequence.

03

Build the runtime key

For each access, SAP fills the condition-table fields from the document. If a required field is empty or different, an apparently correct VB11 record will never match.

04

Find a valid record

Check key fields, determination type, validity and the alternatives stored in the record. Do not use today’s date as the only validity test in product-selection scenarios.

05

Apply substitution behavior

SAP can replace directly, present alternatives for selection, or evaluate alternatives through product-selection logic and availability.

06

Continue with the resulting Sales item

Pricing, ATP, item category, plant, shipping, batch and later delivery behavior must be explained from the material and item structure that actually result from substitution.

Three runtime patterns

“Material substitution” does not always mean the same document structure.

Direct replacement

The entered material resolves to another product and order processing continues with the substitute. This is the cleanest model for identifier translation or a controlled successor product where user choice is not required.

Manual selection

SAP can display available alternatives and let the user choose. This fits cases where substitution needs commercial judgment rather than a silent automatic decision.

Automatic product selection

SAP can evaluate alternatives in sequence and use availability to cover the requested quantity. In classic product selection, the result can be represented with a main item and subitems for confirmed alternatives.

inventory_2

Example: an order requests 350 pieces. Alternative A confirms 100, B confirms 200 and C confirms 50. Product selection can split the confirmed requirement across those alternatives. This is why the troubleshooting question is not only “which substitute was found?” but also “how was the requested quantity covered?”

Substitution reason

Do not reduce OVRQ to a four-field cheat sheet.

The familiar Entry, Warning, Strategy and Outcome controls are useful, but their exact meaning depends on the substitution scenario. A strong answer explains the business effect instead of assuming that the same value means the same item structure everywhere.

Entry

Controls whether the material originally entered remains visible or printable in relevant outputs. This matters when the customer must see what was requested even though another material is delivered.

Warning / Strategy

These settings influence user interaction and manual versus automatic behavior. For classic automatic ATP-based product selection, SAP documents additional restrictions on these fields, so do not copy a manual-selection setup into automatic product selection without checking the scenario.

Outcome

Outcome is especially easy to misremember. In classic automatic product selection, outcome variants can control whether a new product selection is allowed again during delivery processing; they are not simply a generic “replace item versus create subitem” switch.

warning

Assessment trap: if someone asks what Outcome A or B means, first anchor the answer to the process variant and release. The safe Lead answer is to explain the effect you need — user choice, ATP selection, item structure, printing, or delivery redetermination — and then map that effect to the configured reason in the target system.

Validity and time

The condition record can be valid today and still be invalid for the order.

In classic product selection, SAP compares the substitution rule with the requested delivery date. Forward scheduling can also move the relevant date. This matters for product launches, phase-outs and promotions where several mappings exist across adjacent validity periods.

Support mistake

“The VB11 record is valid today, so SAP should use it.” That is not enough evidence. Check the order’s requested delivery date and the actual dates used by the product-selection process.

Design consequence

A time-based replacement is part of order-promising behavior. If the requested date changes, the substitute can change even though the user entered the same source material.

Later redetermination

The substitute selected in the order is not always the final product decision.

Classic product selection can be re-evaluated later. Rescheduling can revisit Sales orders that contain product selection, and some substitution-reason outcomes allow a new product selection in the delivery. This is powerful for service level, but dangerous if the business believes the first substitute is already a customer commitment.

Rescheduling

Transaction V_V2 can re-evaluate requirements. Changed availability or changed substitution settings can therefore produce a different result from the one seen when the order was created.

Delivery redetermination

Depending on the substitution reason and outcome, product selection can be allowed again in delivery processing. Diagnose this before calling an order-to-delivery material change a copy-control defect.

Business promise

If the customer must receive the exact alternative confirmed at order time, define how that promise is protected. Flexibility and commitment stability are different business goals.

Interactions

Substitution changes the context for later determinations.

Material determination is early in the Sales item flow. Once the resulting product or subitem structure is known, the downstream rules must be evaluated for that result rather than for the identifier originally typed by the user.

Pricing

Decide whether the commercial price should follow the entered product, the substitute, a contract reference, or another pricing key. Do not assume this is solved by material determination itself.

ATP / aATP

Classic automatic product selection can use availability to evaluate alternatives. Advanced ATP also has its own product-substitution capabilities, which must be designed separately.

Plant and shipping

A substitute can have different plant determination, loading group, transportation group, shipping point or route consequences. The replacement can therefore change the logistical promise.

Batch determination

When both functions are relevant, check their processing sequence. SAP documents product substitution before automatic batch determination for the selected quantity in the classic scenario.

Texts and outputs

Decide whether the customer should see the entered material, the substitute, or both. The substitution reason can participate in this behavior, but output design must match the commercial policy.

Billing and document flow

Confirm which item becomes delivery- and billing-relevant and how the original request remains traceable. Product-selection subitems make this more important than in a simple direct replacement.

Troubleshooting

Use the first failed decision, not the final symptom.

Nothing happens after material entry

Start with Sales document type and the OV14 procedure assignment. Then prove the active determination type. Do not inspect alternatives before proving that determination actually runs.

Expected access is not executed

Inspect the access sequence and condition-table fields. Compare the real runtime key with the key combination used in VB11.

Access runs but finds no record

Check determination type, all key values, requested delivery date, validity, source material and any customer or Sales-context dimensions.

Wrong alternative wins

Check the sequence of alternatives, the original-product entry if used, availability and the substitution reason. Priority is data, not an informal expectation.

Order and delivery use different products

Check the substitution reason outcome and whether new product selection is permitted in delivery. Then check whether availability changed after order creation.

Product changes after rescheduling

Check V_V2 behavior, current substitution settings and current availability. The new result may be expected redetermination rather than data corruption.

troubleshoot

Best support tool: switch on the Material Determination Analysis before entering the item. The analysis shows accesses and substitution decisions for the current transaction. It is temporary analysis data, so it is not a retrospective log you can open later for an already saved order.

Material Determination vs aATP

Do not merge classic SD substitution with aATP Product Substitution.

Both can answer “what can we sell instead?”, but they belong to different decision models. This distinction is increasingly important in S/4HANA designs.

Classic SD Material Determination

Condition-technique driven. Strong fit for commercial replacement rules, identifier translation, time-based substitutions and classic automatic product selection. The Sales document procedure and substitution reason are central controls.

aATP Product Substitution

Part of advanced order promising. It can model substitution chains and advanced alternatives in the confirmation process, and can work together with location alternatives depending on the aATP design.

fork_right

Lead decision: if the rule is “customer enters old SKU, always sell successor SKU,” start from Material Determination. If the rule is “during advanced confirmation, search product and location alternatives according to supply policy,” evaluate aATP substitution. Do not implement the same business decision twice in both layers.

link

About substitution chains: do not memorize “A → B → C is not supported” as a universal S/4HANA rule. Chain behavior is feature-specific. Current aATP documentation explicitly supports product-substitution chains, while other Sales scenarios can have different restrictions.

Deployment boundary

The concept is stable; delivered identifiers and configuration access are not.

Material determination exists across current S/4HANA deployment models, but the way you configure and maintain it is not identical. Keep the conceptual chain stable and verify the concrete app, delivered keys and extensibility model in the target edition.

Private Edition / on-premise

Classic GUI transactions such as OV16, OV11, OV12, OV13, OV14, OVRQ and VB11/VB12/VB13 remain useful technical anchors. Current S/4HANA on-premise documentation still describes this condition-technique model.

Public Cloud

Use the released apps and configuration activities for the cloud scope. Current Public Cloud documentation uses delivered identifiers such as Y001 for a standard material-determination type in its example scope, so do not assume a classic A001 key is universal.

cloud

Assessment answer: explain the business and determination model first. Mention transaction codes only after you state the deployment boundary. That shows you understand S/4HANA rather than memorizing an ECC customizing path.

Lead design checks

Before configuration, answer the operating questions.

01

What business decision is being automated?

Identifier translation, successor product, promotion, shortage protection and supply substitution have different ownership and downstream risks.

02

Who is allowed to decide the substitute?

The system, the order-entry user, Sales operations or supply policy? This determines whether silent, manual or availability-driven behavior is appropriate.

03

What date controls the rule?

Define whether the replacement follows requested delivery date, a launch date, phase-out date or another business milestone. Then test boundary dates explicitly.

04

Can the substitute change later?

Decide whether rescheduling or delivery may redetermine the product. If not, protect the commitment intentionally instead of assuming SAP will keep the first result.

05

Which downstream rules follow the substitute?

Check pricing, plant, ATP, shipping, batch, outputs, delivery and billing. A technically correct substitution can still be a bad end-to-end design.

06

Is classic SD or aATP the real owner?

Put the decision in one layer. Duplicate substitution logic creates conflicting answers and difficult production diagnostics.

Assessment recall

Explain the engine, the record and the downstream effect.

record_voice_over

90-second answer: Material Determination in SAP Sales is a condition-technique mechanism that converts an entered material into another material or a set of alternatives. I separate it into three layers. First, configuration: condition table, access sequence, determination type and procedure decide how SAP searches, and the procedure must be assigned to the Sales document type. Second, the VB11 condition record contains the entered material, substitute or alternative materials, validity and substitution reason. Third, runtime behavior: the substitution reason can drive direct replacement, manual selection or automatic product selection with ATP and subitems. In support I first prove the active procedure and access, then the runtime key and validity, then the substitution reason and alternative sequence. I also check the requested delivery date, because validity is not simply “is the record valid today?”. Finally, I separate classic SD Material Determination from aATP Product Substitution, because aATP owns a different advanced-confirmation decision model.

Strong follow-up

If the record exists but nothing happens, I do not create another record. I prove OV14, the determination type, the access sequence and the runtime key first.

Strong support point

I activate Material Determination Analysis before item entry because the analysis is transaction-local and cannot simply be opened later as a historical log.

Strong architecture point

If the requirement is driven by advanced supply confirmation and substitution chains, I evaluate aATP rather than forcing all substitution logic into classic SD condition records.
