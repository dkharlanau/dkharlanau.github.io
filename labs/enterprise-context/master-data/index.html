SAP Sales / Logistics / Master Data

Master data is upstream behavior,
not a list of records.

A strong SAP Lead reads master data as reusable business decisions. We ask what truth is being maintained, at which key and organizational level, who owns it, how the runtime document consumes it, and what can break when that truth changes.

High-leverage fields

Many important logistics controls are fields inside a master object, not separate master-data objects.

For assessment questions, name the object, the organizational scope, and the field. Saying only “customer master”, “material master”, or “vendor master” is too broad.

Keep the layers separate

These things matter to Sales, but they are not another Sales master-data object.

This boundary prevents a common assessment mistake: mixing reusable data, enterprise structure, configuration, and runtime documents into one list.

Enterprise structure

  • Company Code
  • Sales Organization
  • Distribution Channel
  • Division and Sales Area
  • Plant and Shipping Point

These objects define organizational and execution context. They scope master data and process responsibility.

Open SAP Organizational Structure for the full Sales assignment map, Plant and Shipping Point links, intercompany boundary, pricing connection, and diagnostic sequence.

Configuration / mechanisms

  • Pricing procedure, condition type, access sequence
  • Partner determination procedure
  • Item category and schedule-line determination
  • Copy control and output rules
  • ATP, shipping-point, route, and account-determination rules

Configuration decides how SAP reads and combines master data. The rule is not the same thing as the maintained record.

Runtime documents

  • Inquiry and quotation
  • Sales Order
  • Outbound Delivery
  • Billing Document
  • Manual document overrides

Master data proposes or determines values. After a value is copied into a document, the document has its own lifecycle.

Order-to-Cash use

Map the catalogue to the process.

Do not memorize isolated definitions. Remember which objects become important at each process step, then follow the ownership boundary when the flow moves from Sales into fulfillment or Procurement.

01

Order entry

Business Partner, partner functions, addresses, Product, Customer Material, Item Proposal, Listing / Exclusion, Material Determination, Cross-Selling, Sales BOM, and Variant Configuration.

02

Pricing and commercial terms

Customer sales data, Customer Hierarchy, Product hierarchy/grouping, Pricing Condition Records, Free Goods, customer/product attributes, and configuration values where pricing-relevant.

03

ATP and shipping

Product sales/plant data, Customer Material, ship-to/address data, units of measure, availability and loading fields, plus Classification, Batch and Serial Number data where relevant.

04

Credit

Payer/credit-account context plus Credit Master Data: credit segment, limit, risk class, check rule, and validity.

05

Delivery

Ship-to and address, Product/UoM data, loading and logistics fields, Classification, Batch Master, Serial Numbers, International Trade classification for cross-border flows, and document partners carried into execution.

06

Billing and output

Bill-to and payer, company-code customer context, payment/tax/account attributes, pricing conditions, reusable texts, output recipient context, plus Intrastat inputs such as valid product classification and statistical value where relevant.

swap_horiz

Procurement handoff: when demand requires external supply, trace Product → Supplier → Purchasing Info Record → Source List / Quota Arrangement. Do not treat this as one fixed sequence: source determination decides which records are relevant for the concrete requirement, date, plant, and purchasing context.

Precedence to remember

Priority is specific to a determination.

There is no universal rule that “customer data overrides product data”. Learn the priority for the field you are tracing.

01

Delivering plant

Standard proposal path: Customer Material → ship-to customer → Product/Material. A document value or specialized scenario can change the final result.

02

Customer-specific item data

Where Customer Material maintains the relevant value, it can specialize the generic customer or product default.

03

Pricing

Pricing procedure and access sequence are configuration. They search condition tables and find a valid Pricing Condition Record. The record is data; the search logic is configuration.

04

Partners and addresses

Partner determination defines the roles. Customer/BP relationships supply partner candidates. Address determination then selects the relevant physical address for the partner and context.

Assessment recall

A 60-second answer should start with the object map.

Use architecture language only after you show that you know the actual master-data landscape.

record_voice_over

60-second answer: “I treat Sales master data as reusable upstream decisions, not as a list of tables. First I classify the behavior: shared business truth, relationship-specific data, condition-style policy, or adjacent logistics and sourcing data. Then I prove the exact key and organizational scope, the precedence or search logic that consumes it, and the document value that resulted. If something is wrong, I compare expected and actual provenance before changing shared data. The design goal is the smallest correct owner with clear governance and no hidden side effects.”

When a value is wrong

Identify the object and exact key first. Then check the relevant field and any more specific relationship or condition record.

Only then diagnose deeper

Trace precedence, the document value, replication state, and ownership. These are diagnostic layers, not the definition of Sales master data itself.

Go deeper only when needed

Use the catalogue as the map; use the deep dives for mechanism detail.

extension

Keep the boundary clean: purchase contracts and scheduling agreements are long-term procurement documents, not another customer/material-style master object. Production versions, routings, inspection plans, equipment structures, service masters, Retail assortments, compliance data, promotions, and settlement objects belong to their owning module or specialized layer and should be linked rather than flattened into one universal list. International Trade classification is maintained through the product classification framework; see the Material page for commodity-code, customs-tariff and Intrastat boundaries.
