Use one boundary: master data is reusable maintained data that survives beyond one sales document. Business Partner and Product are business masters. Customer-material and partner assignments are relationship data. Pricing, material determination, listing/exclusion, and free-goods records are validity-based condition data. Sales Organization, pricing procedure, and a Sales Order belong to different layers.

What this makes visible: Listing / Exclusion, Material Determination, Free Goods, Cross-Selling, Sales Item Proposals, hierarchies, and reusable texts are part of the Sales data landscape. They should not disappear behind the broad labels “customer master” and “material master”.

High-leverage fields

Many important Sales controls are fields inside a master object, not separate master-data objects.

For assessment questions, name both the object and the field. Saying only “customer master” or “material master” is too broad.

Keep the layers separate

These things matter to Sales, but they are not another Sales master-data object.

This boundary prevents a common assessment mistake: mixing reusable data, enterprise structure, configuration, and runtime documents into one list.

Enterprise structure

  • Company Code
  • Sales Organization
  • Distribution Channel
  • Division and Sales Area
  • Plant and Shipping Point

These objects define organizational and execution context. They scope master data and process responsibility.

Configuration / mechanisms

  • Pricing procedure, condition type, access sequence
  • Partner determination procedure
  • Item category and schedule-line determination
  • Copy control and output rules
  • ATP, shipping-point, route, and account-determination rules

Configuration decides how SAP reads and combines master data. The rule is not the same thing as the maintained record.

Runtime documents

  • Inquiry and quotation
  • Sales Order
  • Outbound Delivery
  • Billing Document
  • Manual document overrides

Master data proposes or determines values. After a value is copied into a document, the document has its own lifecycle.

Order-to-Cash use

Map the catalogue to the process.

You do not need to memorize nineteen isolated definitions. Remember which objects become important at each O2C step.

01

Order entry

Business Partner, partner functions, addresses, Product, Customer Material, Item Proposal, Listing / Exclusion, Material Determination, Cross-Selling, Sales BOM, and Variant Configuration.

02

Pricing and commercial terms

Customer sales data, Customer Hierarchy, Product hierarchy/grouping, Pricing Condition Records, Free Goods, customer/product attributes, and configuration values where pricing-relevant.

03

ATP and shipping

Product sales/plant data, Customer Material, ship-to/address data, units of measure, availability and loading fields, plus Batch data where relevant.

04

Credit

Payer/credit-account context plus Credit Master Data: credit segment, limit, risk class, check rule, and validity.

05

Delivery

Ship-to and address, Product/UoM data, loading and logistics fields, Batch master/classification, and document partners carried into execution.

06

Billing and output

Bill-to and payer, company-code customer context, payment/tax/account attributes, pricing conditions, reusable texts, and output recipient context.

Precedence to remember

Priority is specific to a determination.

There is no universal rule that “customer data overrides product data”. Learn the priority for the field you are tracing.

01

Delivering plant

Standard proposal path: Customer Material → ship-to customer → Product/Material. A document value or specialized scenario can change the final result.

02

Customer-specific item data

Where Customer Material maintains the relevant value, it can specialize the generic customer or product default.

03

Pricing

Pricing procedure and access sequence are configuration. They search condition tables and find a valid Pricing Condition Record. The record is data; the search logic is configuration.

04

Partners and addresses

Partner determination defines the roles. Customer/BP relationships supply partner candidates. Address determination then selects the relevant physical address for the partner and context.

Assessment recall

A 60-second answer should start with the object map.

Use architecture language only after you show that you know the actual master-data landscape.

record_voice_over

Answer shape: “In Sales I separate business masters such as Business Partner and Product; relationship and content data such as Customer Material, partner functions, hierarchies, texts, and item proposals; condition-style data such as pricing, material determination, listing/exclusion, and free goods; and adjacent data such as credit, units, batches, BOMs, and variant configuration. Then I state the exact organizational/key level, the fields that drive the process, and the runtime consumer.”

When a value is wrong

Identify the object and exact key first. Then check the relevant field and any more specific relationship or condition record.

Only then diagnose deeper

Trace precedence, the document value, replication state, and ownership. These are diagnostic layers, not the definition of Sales master data itself.

Go deeper only when needed

Use the catalogue as the map; use the deep dives for mechanism detail.
