SAP S/4HANA / EWM / Warehouse Product

Warehouse Product

The generic material/product describes what the product is. EWM adds warehouse-specific product data that controls how the product should behave inside a warehouse. This is the layer to inspect when Sales and Inventory data look correct but putaway, picking, replenishment or warehouse-task behavior is still wrong.

Diagnostic split

01ProductEnterprise default
02WarehouseEWM behavior
Same product, different warehouse execution context.

Runtime use

Warehouse-specific data becomes important only after the process reaches EWM.

Putaway

Warehouse product settings can participate in storage-type and putaway behavior together with EWM configuration.

Stock removal

Picking and removal behavior can depend on warehouse-specific product attributes and process settings.

Replenishment and handling

Warehouse-level parameters can support replenishment, packaging, handling and execution rules.

troubleshoot

Lead diagnostic: if the same product behaves differently by warehouse, compare warehouse-specific product data before changing the global material master.
