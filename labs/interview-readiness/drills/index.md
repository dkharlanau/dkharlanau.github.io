  <section class="research-canvas__boundary" data-reveal>
    <span class="material-symbols-outlined" aria-hidden="true">rule</span>
    <p><strong>Use one rule:</strong> answer before opening the guidance. A drill is useful when it exposes a missing question, weak boundary, unsupported claim, or operational gap.</p>
    <p><strong>Lead standard:</strong> include business impact, ownership, process, data, integration, controls, failure handling, delivery constraints, and the reason for the decision.</p>
  </section>

  <section class="research-canvas__inventory" id="drills" data-reveal>
    <header><p class="research-canvas__eyebrow">Four drill types</p><h2>Train the failure modes of senior interviews.</h2><p>Use the drill that attacks the weakest part of your answer. More reading is not automatically the answer. Humans have tried that approach for centuries.</p></header>
    <div class="ir-grid">
      <article class="ir-card"><p class="ir-kicker">Architecture decisions</p><h3>Decision Cards</h3><p>Compare IDoc vs API vs event, synchronous vs asynchronous, embedded vs decentralised EWM, clean-core extensions, and other common boundaries.</p><p><a class="ir-link" href="/labs/interview-readiness/decision-cards/">Open Decision Cards <span class="material-symbols-outlined" aria-hidden="true">arrow_forward</span></a></p></article>
      <article class="ir-card"><p class="ir-kicker">Production reasoning</p><h3>Diagnostic Lab</h3><p>Work from symptoms to evidence through duplicate orders, IDoc failures, blocked billing, stock mismatches, API timeouts, and queue backlogs.</p><p><a class="ir-link" href="/labs/interview-readiness/diagnostic-lab/">Open Diagnostic Lab <span class="material-symbols-outlined" aria-hidden="true">arrow_forward</span></a></p></article>
      <article class="ir-card"><p class="ir-kicker">Senior pressure</p><h3>Boss Battles</h3><p>Handle a long case where cost, time, architecture, ownership, and evidence change while you are speaking.</p><p><a class="ir-link" href="/labs/interview-readiness/boss-battles/">Start Boss Battle <span class="material-symbols-outlined" aria-hidden="true">arrow_forward</span></a></p></article>
      <article class="ir-card"><p class="ir-kicker">Project proof</p><h3>Evidence Bank</h3><p>Check whether saved project stories contain a decision, trade-off, result, lesson, and enough range to support Lead-level claims.</p><p><a class="ir-link" href="/labs/interview-readiness/evidence-bank/">Review Evidence Bank <span class="material-symbols-outlined" aria-hidden="true">arrow_forward</span></a></p></article>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="research-canvas__inventory" data-reveal>
    <header><p class="research-canvas__eyebrow">Suggested sequence</p><h2>Learn → Connect → Practice → Defend → Prove.</h2><p>The drills sit between knowledge review and the scored Assessment Lab. They are deliberately uncomfortable but not scored as formal assessment attempts.</p></header>
    <div class="ir-grid">
      <article class="ir-card"><h3>1. Decision Card</h3><p>Pick one architecture choice and give a two-minute recommendation.</p></article>
      <article class="ir-card"><h3>2. Diagnostic Case</h3><p>Trace evidence without jumping to your favourite technical cause.</p></article>
      <article class="ir-card"><h3>3. Boss Battle</h3><p>Defend the recommendation when constraints change.</p></article>
      <article class="ir-card"><h3>4. Evidence Bank</h3><p>Attach a real project story to the capability you claim.</p></article>
    </div>
