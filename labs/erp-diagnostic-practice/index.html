Quantity & units: Ten ordered. One hundred delivered?

Integration recovery: One dispatch, two inventory postings

Migration reconciliation: The totals match. The migration does not.

A fictional migration report says source and target totals are both 1,500.00 EUR. The reviewer has approved the batch using only that total.

Your task: Compare records by their stable keys. Decide whether the matching grand total is sufficient to accept the migration.

Before you use the result

Does this run SAP or reproduce a real incident?

No. The records are original synthetic examples, and each exercise states its own rules. You practise reasoning about ERP data without connecting to SAP. Real systems may use different processes and controls.

What does a passing result mean?

Your calculation and selected conclusions match the fixed rules of that case. Written reasoning is not graded. The result is an educational check, not a credential, hiring assessment, or promise of project readiness.

What happens to my answers?

The lab holds them in the open page's memory. Switching cases preserves your draft during that visit; restarting clears that case; reloading clears all cases. The lab does not send your notes for scoring. Enter only the supplied fictional data.

These exercises model data relationships, replay behaviour, and reconciliation rules. They do not reproduce SAP screens, transactions, configuration, or release-specific behaviour.
