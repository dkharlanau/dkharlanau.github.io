    <p><strong>Boundary:</strong> Business AI models enterprise use, decisions, technologies, outcomes, controls, and evidence. AI Ready keeps deeper engineering patterns. Enterprise Context keeps detailed SAP and business-system models.</p>

  <section class="research-canvas__inventory" id="case-kinds" data-reveal>
    <header>
      <p class="research-canvas__eyebrow">Controlled case kinds</p>
      <h2>Not every useful case is the same type of evidence.</h2>
      <p>Case kind is a controlled property. It prevents a customer implementation, a foundation capability, and a vendor solution example from looking equally proven.</p>
    </header>
    <div class="research-route-list">
      <a href="/labs/business-ai/cases/"><span>AI</span><strong>ai_implementation</strong><small>A company uses AI, ML, optimization, document intelligence, robotics, or agentic logic inside a real business process.</small><i class="material-symbols-outlined" aria-hidden="true">psychology</i></a>
      <a href="/labs/business-ai/cases/#adani-master-data-foundation"><span>DATA</span><strong>ai_foundation</strong><small>A capability such as trusted master data is not AI by itself but is required for reliable AI and automation.</small><i class="material-symbols-outlined" aria-hidden="true">database</i></a>
      <a href="/labs/business-ai/cases/#dataxstream-quote-order-ai"><span>SOL</span><strong>solution_evidence</strong><small>A product or partner example shows a useful architecture shape but weaker end-customer evidence.</small><i class="material-symbols-outlined" aria-hidden="true">extension</i></a>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="research-canvas__inventory" id="node-types" data-reveal>
    <header>
      <p class="research-canvas__eyebrow">Node types</p>
      <h2>What exists in the model.</h2>
      <p>Stable IDs matter more than display labels. A platform can be renamed without changing the business relationships around it.</p>
    </header>
    <div class="research-route-list">
      <a href="/labs/business-ai/domains/"><span>DOM</span><strong>BusinessDomain</strong><small>An ownership area such as Sales, Finance, HR, IT, Manufacturing, Legal, or Master Data.</small><i class="material-symbols-outlined" aria-hidden="true">domain</i></a>
      <a href="/labs/business-ai/processes/"><span>E2E</span><strong>EndToEndProcess</strong><small>A cross-role chain such as Lead-to-Cash, Source-to-Pay, Record-to-Report, or Incident-to-Resolution.</small><i class="material-symbols-outlined" aria-hidden="true">route</i></a>
      <a href="/labs/business-ai/processes/"><span>STEP</span><strong>ProcessStage</strong><small>A stage, decision, exception, handoff, or task where AI can change work.</small><i class="material-symbols-outlined" aria-hidden="true">conversion_path</i></a>
      <a href="/labs/business-ai/patterns/"><span>PAT</span><strong>Pattern</strong><small>A reusable business and architecture shape that can survive a vendor change.</small><i class="material-symbols-outlined" aria-hidden="true">schema</i></a>
      <a href="/labs/business-ai/technologies/"><span>FAM</span><strong>TechnologyFamily</strong><small>A stable capability class such as RAG, prediction, optimization, workflow, agents, document intelligence, or governance.</small><i class="material-symbols-outlined" aria-hidden="true">category</i></a>
      <a href="/labs/business-ai/technologies/#platforms"><span>PLT</span><strong>EnterprisePlatform</strong><small>A concrete platform or product family that implements one or more technology capabilities.</small><i class="material-symbols-outlined" aria-hidden="true">memory</i></a>
      <a href="/labs/business-ai/cases/"><span>CASE</span><strong>BusinessAICase</strong><small>A public implementation, foundation, or solution-evidence case in a defined company and process context.</small><i class="material-symbols-outlined" aria-hidden="true">case_study</i></a>
      <a href="/labs/business-ai/scenarios/"><span>OUT</span><strong>ScenarioOutcome</strong><small>An observed strong pattern, mixed result, or failure with a concrete operating lesson.</small><i class="material-symbols-outlined" aria-hidden="true">compare_arrows</i></a>
      <a href="/labs/business-ai/practices/#failure-patterns"><span>FAIL</span><strong>FailurePattern</strong><small>A recurring shape such as authoritative hallucination, biased history, metric tunnel vision, or prediction tied directly to capital.</small><i class="material-symbols-outlined" aria-hidden="true">warning</i></a>
      <a href="/labs/business-ai/practices/"><span>BP</span><strong>BestPractice</strong><small>A vendor-neutral operating rule that prevents or limits recurring failure patterns.</small><i class="material-symbols-outlined" aria-hidden="true">rule</i></a>
      <a href="/labs/business-ai/matrix/"><span>DEC</span><strong>DecisionProfile</strong><small>A Lead-level decision shape connecting process, AI job, autonomy, risk, KPI, authority, controls, failure patterns, and evidence.</small><i class="material-symbols-outlined" aria-hidden="true">grid_view</i></a>
      <a href="#node-types"><span>ORG</span><strong>Company</strong><small>The organization operating or reporting the implementation.</small><i class="material-symbols-outlined" aria-hidden="true">business</i></a>
      <a href="#node-types"><span>IND</span><strong>Industry</strong><small>Industry context used to test transferability.</small><i class="material-symbols-outlined" aria-hidden="true">factory</i></a>
      <a href="#node-types"><span>KPI</span><strong>Metric</strong><small>A measured or reported business, process, or technical result.</small><i class="material-symbols-outlined" aria-hidden="true">monitoring</i></a>
      <a href="#node-types"><span>SRC</span><strong>EvidenceSource</strong><small>The source supporting a case, platform statement, outcome, technology statement, or metric.</small><i class="material-symbols-outlined" aria-hidden="true">source</i></a>
      <a href="#node-types"><span>CTRL</span><strong>ControlPoint</strong><small>A rule, approval, identity, hard constraint, safety boundary, or posting control that remains explicit.</small><i class="material-symbols-outlined" aria-hidden="true">verified_user</i></a>
      <a href="#node-types"><span>!</span><strong>Limitation</strong><small>A missing measurement, evidence weakness, undisclosed detail, transferability risk, or known boundary.</small><i class="material-symbols-outlined" aria-hidden="true">warning</i></a>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="research-canvas__inventory" id="edge-types" data-reveal>
    <header>
      <p class="research-canvas__eyebrow">Relationship types</p>
      <h2>How the knowledge connects.</h2>
      <p>The useful graph path should answer: where in the business, what pattern, which technology, which platform, how much authority, what happened, what evidence, and which control.</p>
    </header>
    <div class="research-route-list">
      <a href="#edge-types"><span>→</span><strong>EndToEndProcess CROSSES BusinessDomain</strong><small>A process may cross several ownership areas.</small><i class="material-symbols-outlined" aria-hidden="true">arrow_forward</i></a>
      <a href="#edge-types"><span>→</span><strong>EndToEndProcess HAS_STAGE ProcessStage</strong><small>Locates AI at a concrete place in the process.</small><i class="material-symbols-outlined" aria-hidden="true">arrow_forward</i></a>
      <a href="#edge-types"><span>→</span><strong>ProcessStage USES_PATTERN Pattern</strong><small>Connects a business problem to a reusable AI or automation shape.</small><i class="material-symbols-outlined" aria-hidden="true">arrow_forward</i></a>
      <a href="#edge-types"><span>→</span><strong>Pattern NEEDS TechnologyFamily</strong><small>Shows capability requirements without choosing a vendor yet.</small><i class="material-symbols-outlined" aria-hidden="true">arrow_forward</i></a>
      <a href="#edge-types"><span>→</span><strong>EnterprisePlatform PROVIDES TechnologyFamily</strong><small>Maps platform capabilities to the stable technology layer.</small><i class="material-symbols-outlined" aria-hidden="true">arrow_forward</i></a>
      <a href="#edge-types"><span>→</span><strong>ProcessStage HAS_CONTROL ControlPoint</strong><small>Keeps exact rules, approvals, identity, safety, and side effects visible.</small><i class="material-symbols-outlined" aria-hidden="true">arrow_forward</i></a>
      <a href="#edge-types"><span>→</span><strong>Company IMPLEMENTS BusinessAICase</strong><small>Who operates or publishes the implementation context.</small><i class="material-symbols-outlined" aria-hidden="true">arrow_forward</i></a>
      <a href="#edge-types"><span>→</span><strong>BusinessAICase IMPLEMENTS_PATTERN Pattern</strong><small>One primary pattern and optional secondary patterns classify the case.</small><i class="material-symbols-outlined" aria-hidden="true">arrow_forward</i></a>
      <a href="#edge-types"><span>→</span><strong>BusinessAICase APPLIES_TO EndToEndProcess or ProcessStage</strong><small>Links public evidence to the process map.</small><i class="material-symbols-outlined" aria-hidden="true">arrow_forward</i></a>
      <a href="#edge-types"><span>→</span><strong>BusinessAICase USES EnterprisePlatform</strong><small>Only platforms disclosed by a public source are attached.</small><i class="material-symbols-outlined" aria-hidden="true">arrow_forward</i></a>
      <a href="#edge-types"><span>→</span><strong>BusinessAICase OCCURS_IN Industry</strong><small>Keeps industry context explicit.</small><i class="material-symbols-outlined" aria-hidden="true">arrow_forward</i></a>
      <a href="#edge-types"><span>→</span><strong>BusinessAICase REPORTS Metric</strong><small>A business or operational result reported for the implementation.</small><i class="material-symbols-outlined" aria-hidden="true">arrow_forward</i></a>
      <a href="#edge-types"><span>→</span><strong>ScenarioOutcome ABOUT EndToEndProcess or ProcessStage</strong><small>Places a success, mixed result, or failure inside the business process.</small><i class="material-symbols-outlined" aria-hidden="true">arrow_forward</i></a>
      <a href="#edge-types"><span>→</span><strong>ScenarioOutcome USES_PATTERN Pattern</strong><small>Shows which reusable design shape was involved in the observed outcome.</small><i class="material-symbols-outlined" aria-hidden="true">arrow_forward</i></a>
      <a href="#edge-types"><span>→</span><strong>ScenarioOutcome EXHIBITS FailurePattern</strong><small>Turns one company incident into a reusable architecture warning.</small><i class="material-symbols-outlined" aria-hidden="true">arrow_forward</i></a>
      <a href="#edge-types"><span>→</span><strong>BestPractice MITIGATES FailurePattern</strong><small>Connects a failure lesson to a concrete design or operating control.</small><i class="material-symbols-outlined" aria-hidden="true">arrow_forward</i></a>
      <a href="#edge-types"><span>→</span><strong>BestPractice APPLIES_TO ProcessStage</strong><small>Lets a design review ask which controls belong at each step.</small><i class="material-symbols-outlined" aria-hidden="true">arrow_forward</i></a>
      <a href="#edge-types"><span>→</span><strong>DecisionProfile APPLIES_TO EndToEndProcess or ProcessStage</strong><small>Places the assessment decision inside a real business flow.</small><i class="material-symbols-outlined" aria-hidden="true">arrow_forward</i></a>
      <a href="#edge-types"><span>→</span><strong>DecisionProfile USES_PATTERN Pattern</strong><small>Connects the process decision to reusable AI behavior rather than a vendor name.</small><i class="material-symbols-outlined" aria-hidden="true">arrow_forward</i></a>
      <a href="#edge-types"><span>→</span><strong>DecisionProfile REQUIRES ControlPoint</strong><small>States the minimum control boundary for the recommended autonomy.</small><i class="material-symbols-outlined" aria-hidden="true">arrow_forward</i></a>
      <a href="#edge-types"><span>→</span><strong>DecisionProfile MEASURES Metric</strong><small>Separates model metrics from the business scorecard.</small><i class="material-symbols-outlined" aria-hidden="true">arrow_forward</i></a>
      <a href="#edge-types"><span>→</span><strong>DecisionProfile TESTS FailurePattern</strong><small>Names the recurring failure shape that should be challenged before scale.</small><i class="material-symbols-outlined" aria-hidden="true">arrow_forward</i></a>
      <a href="#edge-types"><span>→</span><strong>DecisionProfile SUPPORTED_BY ScenarioOutcome</strong><small>Joins an assessment answer to strong, mixed, or failed public evidence.</small><i class="material-symbols-outlined" aria-hidden="true">arrow_forward</i></a>
      <a href="#edge-types"><span>→</span><strong>Metric SUPPORTED_BY EvidenceSource</strong><small>Lets a reader trace a number to its publisher.</small><i class="material-symbols-outlined" aria-hidden="true">arrow_forward</i></a>
      <a href="#edge-types"><span>→</span><strong>ScenarioOutcome SUPPORTED_BY EvidenceSource</strong><small>Keeps failures and mixed results source-tracked instead of turning them into anecdotes.</small><i class="material-symbols-outlined" aria-hidden="true">arrow_forward</i></a>
      <a href="#edge-types"><span>→</span><strong>BusinessAICase HAS_LIMITATION Limitation</strong><small>Keeps uncertainty in the graph instead of hiding it in prose.</small><i class="material-symbols-outlined" aria-hidden="true">arrow_forward</i></a>
      <a href="#edge-types"><span>→</span><strong>EnterprisePlatform SUPPORTED_BY EvidenceSource</strong><small>Keeps fast-moving platform statements source-tracked and dated.</small><i class="material-symbols-outlined" aria-hidden="true">arrow_forward</i></a>
    </div>
  </section>
