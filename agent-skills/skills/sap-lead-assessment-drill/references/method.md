# Method

Score reasoning, not product-name matching. The candidate should show that they can frame the business outcome, expose the trade-off, separate system-shape options, allocate authority, ask for evidence, and communicate a defendable recommendation.

Use the six rubric dimensions from the canonical Lead rubric. Each score needs short evidence from the answer. Check hard-fail patterns separately: vendor-first thinking, one-option thinking, hidden authority, AI treated as authorization, missing counter-metric, and invented system facts.

The improved answer should be short enough to speak in about 60–90 seconds, use English B2, and preserve uncertainty where evidence is missing.
