# Method

Classify each signal first. A broken ID, invalid edge direction, or required missing target is structural. Missing evidence, weak case coverage, stale review, or absent counter-evidence is a research gap. Do not mix the two categories because they need different actions.

Rank gaps by decision usefulness, strategic process relevance, evidence weakness, and whether the missing relationship affects authority, controls, metrics, or transferability. Merge duplicate symptoms into one root item.

For any proposed relationship, name the evidence that would justify it. If the evidence does not exist, route the item to research instead of adding the edge. A healthy area should be allowed to stay healthy without artificial maintenance work.
