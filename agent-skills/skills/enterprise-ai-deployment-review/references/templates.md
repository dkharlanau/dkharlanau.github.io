## Economics

Baseline volume, human time, labor-rate assumption, model/tool/storage costs, fixed costs, oversight, rework and accepted-output count. Compare equivalent quality. Report cost per accepted outcome and distinguish released capacity from cash savings.

## Launch recommendation

State go, conditional go or hold, the exact scope, blockers, conditions, accountable approver and stop mechanism. An agent recommends; an authorized human decides.
