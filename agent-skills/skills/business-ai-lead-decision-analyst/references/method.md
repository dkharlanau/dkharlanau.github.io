# Method

Start with the business decision and process boundary. Build the graph path before choosing technology. Separate exact business rules from probabilistic tasks, then define system-of-record, integration, and authority boundaries.

Compare materially different options, not only vendor variants. For each option, show read, propose, validate, approve, and execute authority. Attach controls to side effects and explain recovery or rollback for risky actions.

Evidence strength controls recommendation strength. A first-party case may support feasibility without proving transferability. Negative evidence and failure patterns should challenge the preferred option. A proof gap lowers confidence and can become an experiment condition instead of a hidden assumption.
