# Examples

## Sales

Question: should AI change a promised delivery date? Compare explain-only, proposal, and bounded execution options. Keep ATP and allocation rules deterministic, identify customer-commitment authority, use SAP capability links, and require evidence before any autonomy increase.

## Procurement and logistics

Question: should document AI post supplier invoices automatically? Separate extraction from tolerance, duplicate, approval, and posting rules. Compare assistive validation with bounded auto-posting. Use exception and negative evidence to define stop conditions.

## Integration and AI

Question: should an agent retry failed interfaces? Separate diagnosis from replay authority. Require idempotency, duplicate prevention, production scope, rollback, and evidence of the actual failure class. If side effects are unclear, recommend proposal-only handling.
