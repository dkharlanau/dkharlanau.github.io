# Examples

## Sales and integration

A customer order arrives from an external channel with a requested date that conflicts with current availability. Ask the candidate to decide where AI may assist, which ERP rules remain authoritative, how the integration should fail safely, and who may change the customer commitment.

## Procurement and document AI

An invoice extraction service has high field accuracy but invoice exceptions still create posting risk. Ask for the boundary between extraction, deterministic tolerance checks, human approval, and any bounded automation. Score control design separately from product recall.

## Master data and AI

A team wants AI to classify and enrich new material records. Ask for duplicate handling, source-of-truth ownership, approval, distribution, reconciliation, and evidence needed before autonomy expands. A strong answer may choose a conservative architecture and still score highly.
